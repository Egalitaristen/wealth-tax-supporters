# Suriname (data/sr)

- **Geography:** 10 districten as regions (`G0002`–`G0011`, P-codes `SR01`–`SR10`) and 62 ressorten as the local tier (`G0012`–`G0073`, P-codes `SR0101`–`SR1007`) from the OCHA / HDX COD-AB Suriname shapefiles and gazetteer (valid 2019-10-24, CC BY-IGO). Converted using `topo_to_boundaries.py` on `sur_admbnda_adm2_2017.shp` (`--name-prop ADM2_NL --code-prop ADM2_PCODE --project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2`) and on `sur_admbnda_adm1_2017.shp` (`ADM1_NL`, `ADM1_PCODE`), then combined with `merge_boundaries.py`.
- **Seats:** 51 seats in the unicameral De Nationale Assemblée (2020–2025 term: VHP 20, NDP 16, ABOP 8, NPS 3, PL 2, BEP 2). Official list at `https://dna.sr/het-politiek-college/leden/`.
- **Sources that work:** `dbsuriname.com` (Dagblad Suriname, excellent WP API), `waterkant.net` (detailed political and economic reporting), `sun.sr` (Suriname U-News), `belastingdienst.sr` (official tax administration), `dna.sr` (national assembly legislation archive).
- **Blocked sources:** `keynews.sr` answers 403 to plain HTTP clients.
- **Context:** Suriname has an existing recurring net wealth tax: the **Wet Vermogensbelasting 1944** (G.B. 1944 no. 185, 0.8% annual net wealth tax). The primary political debate focuses on severe under-collection (barely 5M–6M SRD collected annually), lack of enforcement against large private bank assets and gold mining, loophole exploitation via non-taxable private foundation structures (*stichtingen voor particulier beheer*), and the inflation-induced erosion of the statutory threshold (SRD 100,000). Prominent support comes from trade union confederation Ravaksur (proposing threshold updates to SRD 1.6M and expanding the tax to legal entities to yield 500M SRD), opposition politicians (BEP leader Ronny Asabina, former NDP MP Amzaad Abdoel), reform party PRO, tax consultants (Roy Shyamnarain), and legal scholars (Carlo Jadnanansing). Finance Minister Stanley Raghoebarsing opposed wealth taxation in 2024.
