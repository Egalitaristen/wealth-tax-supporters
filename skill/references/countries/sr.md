- **Context:** Suriname has an existing recurring net wealth tax: the **Wet Vermogensbelasting 1944** (G.B. 1944 no. 185, 3% in Article 14 of the tax administration’s 2024-hosted consolidation (subsequent amendments not checked)). The primary political debate focuses on severe under-collection (barely 5M–6M SRD collected annually), lack of enforcement against large private bank assets and gold mining, loophole exploitation via non-taxable private foundation structures (*stichtingen voor particulier beheer*), and the inflation-induced erosion of the statutory threshold (SRD 100,000). Prominent support comes from trade union confederation Ravaksur (proposing threshold updates to SRD 1.6M and expanding the tax to legal entities to yield 500M SRD), opposition politicians (BEP leader Ronny Asabina, former NDP MP Amzaad Abdoel), reform party PRO, tax consultants (Roy Shyamnarain), and legal scholars (Carlo Jadnanansing). Finance Minister Stanley Raghoebarsing opposed wealth taxation in 2024.
