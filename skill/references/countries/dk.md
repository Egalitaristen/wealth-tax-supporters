# Denmark research guide

## Geography

Denmark proper has five regions and 98 municipalities (kommuner). Greenland and the Faroe Islands are self-governing parts of the Kingdom and are not included in this Denmark-proper geography. The official DAGI register is maintained by Klimadatastyrelsen: https://www.eng.klimadatastyrelsen.dk/data/the-danish-administrative-geographical-division-dagi. The municipality code list and region assignments were obtained from https://grundfast.dk/kommunekoder.json (which attributes the list to DAGI), and codes checked against https://admin.danmarksadresser.dk/adressedata/kodelister/kommunekodeliste. Christiansø (0411) is a special area rather than a municipality and is excluded. Region and municipality codes use the four-digit DAGI coding system. These rows do not imply local records were searched.

## Map

The map uses DAGI municipal and regional polygons from Vejdirektoratet’s public ArcGIS service, retrieved 2026-10-29: https://kort.vd.dk/server/rest/services/Grunddata/Danmarks_Administrative_Geografiske_Inddelinger/MapServer/7 (municipalities) and https://kort.vd.dk/server/rest/services/Grunddata/Danmarks_Administrative_Geografiske_Inddelinger/MapServer/40 (regions). The service identifies Klimadatastyrelsen as its source. DAGI is CC BY 4.0: https://datafordeler.dk/vejledning/brugervilkaar/kds-geografiske-data/. Attribution: Klimadatastyrelsen, DAGI via Vejdirektoratet; simplified. The query used output coordinates EPSG:4326, geometryPrecision=4 and maxAllowableOffset=0.002. One combined GeoJSON was constructed from the two layers, filtering special area Christiansø 0411, padding municipality codes to four digits and joining official names from geo_units.csv. Use topo_to_boundaries.py with --project lonlat --scale 10000 --simplify 1, --name-prop name --code-prop code, then split codes 1081–1085 into regions. Combined projection ensures shared coordinates. All 98 municipality and five region codes matched. The official DAGI downloads require an API key; the public road authority service provided the same underlying data without one.
