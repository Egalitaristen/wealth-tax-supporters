# Chile (data/cl)

- **Seats:** `python skill/scripts/seats.py data/cl --fix-status`. The Cámara (155) is read from the Chamber's own web service, `opendata.camara.cl/camaradiputados/WServices/WSDiputado.asmx/retornarDiputadosPeriodoActual` (name, party memberships, no district), with the three earlier periods (`retornarDiputadosXPeriodo`, ids 8 to 10) for former members. **Check the feed before trusting a run:** on 15 November 2026 it listed 155 deputies whose `FechaInicio` is 2030-03-10 (the end of the period), and its Ids and members did not match the Ids the register recorded on 30 October (id 1074 was Marisela Santibáñez then and Daniel Farcas now; Luis Cuello, Irací Hassler and 13 other supporters recorded as sitting were absent, while Marcelo Schilling, Cosme Mellado and others were listed as sitting). The Chamber's register rows were therefore left as of 30 October and not ended from that feed; run the adapter again once the feed is consistent and read every line. The Senado (50) is read from the Spanish Wikipedia list of current senators (`Anexo:Senadores actuales de la República de Chile`, via the MediaWiki API; a secondary source): `senado.cl` sits behind a Cloudflare check that is not bypassed, and the Senate's web service `tramitacion.senado.cl/wspublico/senadores_vigentes.php` still lists only the 2022 cohort (31 senators). Replace the Wikipedia reading with the Senate's own list if it becomes readable. Parties are spelled one way across the two sources by `_cl_party`.
