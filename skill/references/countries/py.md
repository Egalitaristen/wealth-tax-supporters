# Paraguay (data/py)

- **Geography:** 18 regions (17 departamentos plus Distrito Capital Asunción, codes `PY00` to `PY17`) and 263 distritos / municipios (codes `PY0000` to `PY1704`) from the OCHA / HDX COD-AB Paraguay dataset (DGEEC / INE, CC BY-IGO). Run `topo_to_boundaries.py` on `pry_admin2.geojson` (`--project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2 --name-prop adm2_name --code-prop adm2_pcode`) for units, and on `pry_admin1.geojson` for regions, then merge with `merge_boundaries.py`.
- **Seats:** `python skill/scripts/seats.py data/py --fix-status` reads the Senate's list of senators by party and movement (`senado.gov.py/index.php/menu-senadores/sub-menu-nomina-por-partido-movimiento.html`: party headers, each senator linked to the Congress's SILpy record) and the Chamber's alphabetical list (`diputados.gov.py/diputados/nomina-alfabetica`: a table of 80 rows, name in capitals surname first, party or alliance code). The Senate has 45 senators in one nationwide constituency (`national_seat_roles` is `^Senador` in `meta.json`); the 80 deputies are elected by department, but the Chamber's list does not give the department, so no place is stored. Alliance codes of the departmental alliances (CN2023, APUNP, APUNG, ATJPÑ, AC, APAD) are grouped as Concertación. The pages carry the members' phone numbers and emails: the adapter reads neither.
- **Sources that work:** `ultimahora.com`, `abc.com.py`, `lanacion.com.py`, `decidamos.org.py`, `latfem.org`, `revistaplus.com.py`, and `ods.congreso.gov.py`.
- **Wikidata:** `match_wikidata.py` has an entry for `PY` (`Q733`, languages `es`, `gn`, `en`).
