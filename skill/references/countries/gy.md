# Guyana (data/gy)

- **Geography:** 10 administrative regions (`GY01` to `GY10`) and 27 sub-districts (`GY0101` to `GY1002`) from the OCHA / HDX COD-AB Guyana shapefile (`guy_admbnd_2021_shp.zip`, valid 2021-12-16, CC BY-IGO). Generated with `topo_to_boundaries.py` on `adm2` and `adm1` (`--project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2`) and merged with `merge_boundaries.py`.
- **Seats:** The unicameral National Assembly has 65 voting seats (33 PPP/C, 31 APNU+AFC, 1 Joinder List). Official list at `parliament.gov.gy/about-parliament/parliamentary-parties/`. No positions recorded yet as no MP supporter has been identified.
- **Context:** Guyana historically operated an annual net wealth tax codified under the Property Tax Act (Cap. 81:21), introduced in 1962 on the recommendation of Cambridge economist Nicholas Kaldor. "Net property" encompasses worldwide immovable and movable property minus liabilities. The tax was amended in 2013 and 2018. Through the Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2024 (Act No. 2 of 2024), the government inserted Section 6(y) exempting individuals from property tax effective from the year of income starting 1 January 2025. Corporate net property tax remains in force.
