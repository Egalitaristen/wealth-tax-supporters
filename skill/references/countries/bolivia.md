# Bolivia (data/bolivia)

- **Geography:** 9 departamentos, 112 provincias and 339 municipios from the OCHA / HDX COD-AB Bolivia GeoJSON (GeoBolivia, valid 2024-09-16, CC BY-IGO); codes are the OCHA P-codes (`BO` plus the INE codes). Run `topo_to_boundaries.py` three times (`bol_admin3.geojson` with `adm3_name` and `adm3_pcode`, then `bol_admin2.geojson` and `bol_admin1.geojson`; `--project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2`) and merge with `merge_boundaries.py units.json --regions prov.json --upper dep.json`.
- **Seats:** `python skill/scripts/seats.py data/bolivia --fix-status`. The Cámara de Senadores (36) is read from the Senate's own API, `apisi.senado.gob.bo/page/senadores/pleno` (72 entries: 36 titulares and 36 suplentes, a suplente carrying `titular_id`; party in `bancada.sigla`, department in `brigada_catalogo`). The Cámara de Diputados (130) is read from `diputados.gob.bo`'s WordPress API (`/wp-json/wp/v2/diputados`, 255 entries, 3 pages of 100): the site does not say who is titular or suplente, but each photo file (`/wp-json/wp/v2/media?include=<acf.foto>`) is named with a department and `TI` or `SU` (some `TITU`, `SUPLE`, or nothing): whoever is not marked suplente is a titular, which gives exactly 130 (124 marked and 6 unmarked); the party (*Bancada*) and department (*Brigada*) are read from each titular's page. A suplente who has taken a titular's seat is not detected: check a supporter who is a suplente by hand.
- **Sources that work:** eju.tv, correodelsur.com, lanacion.com.py, the Oxfam en Bolivia site and impuestos.gob.bo. Lexivox carries Ley 1357; the Gaceta Oficial was not read.
- **Wikidata:** `match_wikidata.py` has an entry for `BO`.
