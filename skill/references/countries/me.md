# Montenegro: research guide

Search terms: porez na bogatstvo, oporezivanje bogatstva, fond bogatstva, porez na imovinu bogatih. Progressive income taxation (2012 debate, 2022 "Europe Now" reform) and the extra-profit levy are excluded.

## Geography and map
25 municipalities with ISO 3166-2:ME codes (ME-01..ME-25). Map: ELWar 2020 municipality shapefiles (Glaurdić et al., doi 10.7802/2358, CC BY 4.0; open copy at orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/58375), 24 shapes matched to the ISO codes by name. Zeta (ME-25, created 2022) has no shape and is drawn inside Podgorica. Eurostat GISCO LAU, HDX COD-AB and geoBoundaries (OSM, ODbL) were checked and not usable. A newer official boundary (Monstat/Uprava za nekretnine) would replace this.

## Sources that work
