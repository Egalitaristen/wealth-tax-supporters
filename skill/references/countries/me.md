# Montenegro: research guide

Search terms: porez na bogatstvo, oporezivanje bogatstva, fond bogatstva, porez na imovinu bogatih. Progressive income taxation (2012 debate, 2022 "Europe Now" reform) and the extra-profit levy are excluded.

## Geography and map
25 municipalities with ISO 3166-2:ME codes (ME-01..ME-25). No open map with official codes: HDX has no COD-AB for Montenegro; geoBoundaries has 23 OSM-derived units (ODbL, outdated). Follow up with Monstat/Uprava za nekretnine.

## Sources that work
RSE, CdM, Investitor.me, paragraf.me. zakoni.skupstina.me holds bills and minutes (search it directly next run).
