# Namibia (data/na)

Seed pass R001 (2026-11-13). Check that URLs still resolve before relying on them.

- **Geography:** 14 regions (`NA01`-`NA14`) and 107 constituencies (`NA0101`...) from the OCHA COD-AB Namibia layers (valid 2020-01-09, CC BY-IGO 3.0), via the HDX API (`package_show?id=cod-ab-nam`); the geojson zip downloads with a plain GET. Namibia has had 121 constituencies since the 2023 delimitation; the OCHA layer still has 107, so the register does too. Local authorities (57) are not loaded. Recipe: `topo_to_boundaries.py nam_admin2.geojson --name-prop adm2_name --code-prop adm2_pcode --project lonlat --scale 120 --simplify 0.35` plus the admin1 file as regions, then `merge_boundaries.py`.
- **Seats:** `bodies.csv` B0001 is the National Assembly's 96 elected seats from the Electoral Commission's declaration of 3 December 2024 (`https://d4f7y6nbupj5z.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/20241203-Speech_Results-Declaration-speech-Namibia-3Dec2024ETN3.pdf`, table of seats per party; the gazette of 3 January 2025 is at `ecn.na/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Results-publication-2024PNA.pdf`). Up to 8 members are appointed by the President and are not shown. `parliament.na` has no member list that was read, and Namibia is not in `seats.py`.
- **Wealth tax status:** none in force; no estate duty or capital gains tax. The 2015-2016 "Solidarity Wealth Tax" was an income-bracket surcharge (excluded). The 10% dividend tax and the NamRA high-net-worth unit are income-tax measures (excluded).
- **What exists:** two grade C rows (a March 2026 opinion piece). The BIG Coalition's 2024 submission, which reportedly names "a relatively small wealth tax", is the lead to chase.
- **Sources that work:** neweralive.na, namibian.com.na (opinion pages), thebrief.com.na, civic264.org.na, parliament.na PDFs. **Blocked or paywalled:** namibiansun.com and republikein.com.na (paywall after the lede, not bypassed), allafrica.com (403), web.archive.org (not fetchable here).
- **Watch out:** "solidarity (wealth) tax", "high-net-worth" and "tax the rich" mean income taxes; BIG financing proposals mostly mean income-tax claw-back and inheritance or capital gains taxes.
