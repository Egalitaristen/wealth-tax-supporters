# Latvia: no net-wealth tax, thin register

A first-pass guide (seed, 2026-11-04). Latvia has no net-wealth tax; the 'solidarity tax' is an income levy. The register holds one 2013 petition group and one MEP vote.

## Geography

5 NUTS 3 regions (2024 version) over 43 pašvaldības (36 novadi and 7 valstspilsētas), from Eurostat GISCO LAU 2024 and NUTS 2024 (CC BY 4.0), built as for Lithuania.

## Sources that worked and blocked

- europarl.europa.eu roll-call PDFs refuse scripts; the Wayback copy (`web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/<url>`) works.
- ERR (err.ee, news.err.ee) pages read with plain curl; the date is in `article:published_time`.
- Search engines return mostly property-tax and income-tax material; check every hit against the definition.

## Exclusions to remember

See `data/lv/exclusions.csv` and `data/lv/leads.csv`: progressive income tax, inheritance tax, property tax and (Estonia) company asset tax are out of definition.
