# Philippines research and geography guide

PSGC source: [PSA classification portal](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/), 3Q 2026. The public 3Q workbook could not be downloaded due to TLS errors; the register was built from the [July 2026 PSGC mirror](https://github.com/bendlikeabamboo/barangay/blob/main/barangay/data/barangay_flat.json) and cross-checked against PSA's 3Q counts and change notices. It holds 18 regions, 82 provinces, 149 cities and 1,493 municipalities. Omit the mirror's synthetic Isabela City parent and attach the official city to Region IX; attach the eight special geographic area municipalities directly to BARMM. Use 10-digit PSGC codes. Barangays are excluded from the local-government tier in this register.

Map: [geoph](https://github.com/jronnybravo/geoph), CC BY 4.0; credit PSA, NAMRIA, OCHA/HDX and geoph. Unofficial synthesized 2024 configuration, generalized and not authoritative. Convert its `cities_municipalities.shp` and `regions.shp` using `skill/scripts/topo_to_boundaries.py` with `--project lonlat --scale 5000 --simplify 1`, then merge layers; remove the `PH` prefix from local P-codes and right-pad to the 10-digit PSGC codes. Confirm matching against every local register row and check vintage changes before refreshing.

Search in English and Filipino. “National wealth tax” can refer to local shares of natural-resource revenue rather than private net wealth; verify the tax base before inclusion. The official House bill archive at `docs.congress.hrep.online/legisdocs/` provides PDFs and named authors; House member pages sometimes block automated access. Party-list representatives serve nationwide constituencies, so country-root placement is appropriate where no smaller constituency applies.

Record access: filed bill PDFs download with plain `curl`; the House bill search and tracking pages and the Senate site need a real browser (see `skill/references/records/ph.md`).

## Seats

Adapter `philippines` (15 November 2026) for the House of Representatives (318 seats; 317 shown as sitting, the page hides 7 under 'Inactive' and the adapter leaves them out). `congress.gov.ph` sits behind a Cloudflare check that scripts do not pass (403), so the list is read once in a browser: open `https://www.congress.gov.ph/house-members` (the check clears after about eight seconds), and read each card (`h6.hm-fullname`, with the member id in the link `/house-members/view/ID`, the type and the place or party-list name) into rows of `[id, name, type, place]`, skipping the hidden cards. The site names no party for a district member and the party-list group for a party-list member, so the chart is grouped as district and party-list and each supporter keeps the party already in positions.csv (`keep_party`). Recipe: open the page in Claude in Chrome, run a short script that reads the table into JSON, and send it to the localhost receiver (`wts-review/recv.py`, port 8765) as a form post, because Chrome blocks a `fetch` to localhost until its local-network prompt is answered while a form post is not (`f=document.createElement('form'); f.method='POST'; f.enctype='text/plain'; f.action='http://127.0.0.1:8765/NAME.json'; textarea name `x` holding the JSON; f.submit()`). The receiver saves `x=[...]`, which the adapter reads as is. Then run `python skill/scripts/seats.py data/ph --fix-status --local https://www.congress.gov.ph/house-members=FILE`.

The Senate (24 seats; `senate.gov.ph/senators/20-congress-senators` lists 24 senators and no party) has no adapter: `bodies.csv` keeps the existing Senate row with the IPU party split, which the official list cannot give. The 24 names were read on 15 November 2026 and match the 24 seats. `web.senate.gov.ph` returned HTTP 523; use `senate.gov.ph`.
