# Philippines research and geography guide

PSGC source: [PSA classification portal](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/), 3Q 2026. The public 3Q workbook could not be downloaded due to TLS errors; the register was built from the [July 2026 PSGC mirror](https://github.com/bendlikeabamboo/barangay/blob/main/barangay/data/barangay_flat.json) and cross-checked against PSA's 3Q counts and change notices. It holds 18 regions, 82 provinces, 149 cities and 1,493 municipalities. Omit the mirror's synthetic Isabela City parent and attach the official city to Region IX; attach the eight special geographic area municipalities directly to BARMM. Use 10-digit PSGC codes. Barangays are excluded from the local-government tier in this register.

Map: [geoph](https://github.com/jronnybravo/geoph), CC BY 4.0; credit PSA, NAMRIA, OCHA/HDX and geoph. Unofficial synthesized 2024 configuration, generalized and not authoritative. Convert its `cities_municipalities.shp` and `regions.shp` using `skill/scripts/topo_to_boundaries.py` with `--project lonlat --scale 5000 --simplify 1`, then merge layers; remove the `PH` prefix from local P-codes and right-pad to the 10-digit PSGC codes. Confirm matching against every local register row and check vintage changes before refreshing.

Search in English and Filipino. “National wealth tax” can refer to local shares of natural-resource revenue rather than private net wealth; verify the tax base before inclusion. The official House bill archive at `docs.congress.hrep.online/legisdocs/` provides PDFs and named authors; House member pages sometimes block automated access. Party-list representatives serve nationwide constituencies, so country-root placement is appropriate where no smaller constituency applies.
