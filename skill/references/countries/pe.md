# Peru: geography and seats

## Map

Source: [OCHA / HDX Peru COD-AB](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-per), Instituto Geográfico Nacional; dataset valid 2020, reviewed 2025; CC BY 3.0 IGO (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/). District P-codes use PE plus official UBIGEO. District register is a 2026 INEI-based compilation, not yet checked against a current official list. Of its 1,893 districts, 1,872 have matched shapes. The 21 unmatched codes are 030612, 050413, 050512, 050513, 050514, 050515, 080915, 080916, 080917, 080918, 090724, 090725, 120307, 120604, 120606, 130112, 160405, 180107, 221006, 250306 and 250307. Historical boundaries can cover areas that have since changed. The composite polygon PE120699 (Mazamari–Pangoa) has no matching official district in the register and is omitted, leaving a gap there.

Download the dataset's GeoJSON ZIP from HDX and extract per_admin1.geojson, per_admin2.geojson and per_admin3.geojson. Run topo_to_boundaries.py three times with `--project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2 --strip-prefix PE`, using `adm3_name` and `adm3_pcode` for districts, `adm2_name` and `adm2_pcode` for provinces, and `adm1_name` and `adm1_pcode` for departments. For districts add `--where adm3_pcode!=PE120699`, `--code-scheme "INEI UBIGEO (OCHA P-codes without PE prefix)"` and the licence and attribution above. Merge with `merge_boundaries.py districts.json --regions provinces.json --upper departments.json --out data/pe/boundaries.json`. Recheck P-code matches and geographic currency before replacing these files.

## Parliamentary seats

The [Senate](https://senado.congreso.gob.pe/senador/) and [Chamber of Deputies](https://diputados.congreso.gob.pe/diputado/) pages embed a JSON array in a script element with the `data-congresista-dataset` attribute. Count `group` for each 2026–2031 record and check the result against the 60 and 130 seats, respectively; do not add private contact data to the register. On 28 October 2026, five Senate entries had no condition, while two deputies were marked elected and one had no condition. Counts use all roster entries and are explicitly flagged as such in the chamber notes. Check current status against an individual's official profile before lighting a supporter seat. Mirtha Vásquez's current [Senate profile](https://senado.congreso.gob.pe/senador/vasquez-chuquilin-mirtha-esther/) confirms she sits for the national constituency. Retain the former unicameral Congress and its historical positions separately.
