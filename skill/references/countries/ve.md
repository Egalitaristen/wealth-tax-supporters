# Venezuela (data/ve)

- **Geography:** 25 first-level areas (23 estados incl. La Guaira, Distrito Capital, Dependencias Federales) and 336 municipios from the OCHA / HDX COD-AB Venezuela GeoJSON (INE, valid 2021-02-23, CC BY-IGO); codes are the OCHA P-codes (`VE` plus the INE codes). Run `topo_to_boundaries.py ven_admin2.geojson --name-prop adm2_name --code-prop adm2_pcode` and the same for `ven_admin1.geojson` (`adm1_name`, `adm1_pcode`), both with `--project lonlat --scale 100 --simplify 0.2`, then `merge_boundaries.py units.json --regions regions.json`. `geo_units.csv` is built from the same two files (`estado` and `municipio` rows, parents by `adm1_pcode`).
- **Seats:** no adapter. The Asamblea Nacional and the 2017 Constituent Assembly (ANC) member lists were not read, so there is no seat chart. Legislators sit at the country level until their constituency is known.
- **The law:** the Impuesto a los Grandes Patrimonios is a "Ley Constitucional" of the ANC (Gaceta 41.667, 3 July 2019; reprint 41.696). The Travieso Evans copy of 41.667 and the Venamcham copy of the reprint are readable with `pypdf` (the signature images of the law are not text); `pdftotext` mangles the accents there.
- **Sources that work:** El Español (EFE), Diario Las Américas, NTN24, law-firm bulletins. La Patilla and Aporrea answer a bot check and were not read around; the TSJ historic Gaceta server did not answer and the ANC site was not tried; mppef.gob.ve (Seniat press) works.
- **Wikidata:** `match_wikidata.py` has no entry for `VE` (not added, to leave that shared file unchanged); fill QIDs by hand.
