# North Macedonia research guide

Geography: OCHA/HDX COD-AB North Macedonia 2025 (https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-mkd, CC BY-IGO): 8 planning regions, 80 opštini. Four Skopje municipalities share ADM2 P-code MK00809, so the register uses the dataset's PCODEX (MK0080901 ...) as unit code. Map: `--name-prop ADM2_EN --code-prop PCODEX --scale 140 --simplify 0.4 --project lonlat`.

Terms: данок на богатство (wealth), данок на луксуз (богатство) (SDSM/Levica wording; ambiguous with luxury-goods tax), данок на имот (property: excluded), солидарен данок (windfall: excluded). Sources: northmacedonia.rferl.org (works), okno.mk, civilmedia.mk, lokalno.mk; levica.mk answers 403 to scripts (not bypassed). First pass: Levica (A, 2024 programme as reported by RFE/RL), Apasiev (C, 2016), SDSM (C).
