## Geography and map
556 općine and gradovi (LAU codes, e.g. `02658`) under 20 counties and Grad Zagreb (NUTS 3 `HR021`…). Built from Eurostat GISCO LAU and NUTS 2024 like Slovenia (see `si.md`); Vela Luka falls in a gap of the generalised county shape and was assigned to HR037 (Dubrovačko-neretvanska) by hand. ## Seats

The Hrvatski sabor (151 seats) is charted by `seats.py` (`hr`) from the Sabor's own list of representatives, https://www.sabor.hr/hr/zastupnici (150 with mandate status "Aktivan" on 15 November 2026, one seat Vacant; 41 "U mirovanju" keep their seat while a substitute serves and are not former, 18 "Završen"). The list gives party and district, not clubs, and no start date. No politician in the Croatian register sits today (Katarina Peović's mandate ended in 2024).
