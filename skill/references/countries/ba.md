# Bosnia and Herzegovina: research guide

Search terms: porez na bogatstvo, oporezivanje bogatstva, progresivni porez na najbogatije. Exclude the Republika Srpska law on origin of assets (special 75% tax), property, luxury and income taxes.

## Geography and map
Register: 2 entities, Brčko District, 10 FBiH cantons (ISO 3166-2:BA codes) and municipalities listed in Wikipedia's "Municipalities in Bosnia and Herzegovina" with cantonal membership checked by hand; RS shows all 64 local units (the 64th, the city of Istočno Sarajevo, was added; it overlaps six municipalities that stay as rows). Municipalities have no code. Map: ELWar 2020 municipality shapefiles (Glaurdić et al., doi 10.7802/2358, CC BY 4.0, made from the official gazettes; open copy at orbilu.uni.lu/handle/10993/58375). Municipality codes in the register are ELWar IDs (BIH_nnn), not official; replace them if the Agency for Statistics (bhas.gov.ba) publishes an open coded list. Istočno Sarajevo (city) and Stanari have no separate shape. Eurostat GISCO LAU, HDX COD-AB and geoBoundaries (ODbL) were checked and not usable. Cantons and entities are not drawn as shapes.

## Sources that work
Index.hr, Klix, N1, RSE, paragraf.ba, FES library (library.fes.de redirects to collections.fes.de). The parliaments' registries were not searched.
