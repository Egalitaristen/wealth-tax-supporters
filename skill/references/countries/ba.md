# Bosnia and Herzegovina: research guide

Search terms: porez na bogatstvo, oporezivanje bogatstva, progresivni porez na najbogatije. Exclude the Republika Srpska law on origin of assets (special 75% tax), property, luxury and income taxes.

## Geography and map
Register: 2 entities, Brčko District, 10 FBiH cantons (ISO 3166-2:BA codes) and municipalities listed in Wikipedia's "Municipalities in Bosnia and Herzegovina" with cantonal membership checked by hand; RS shows 63 of 64, one is missing. Municipalities have no code. No open map: HDX has no COD-AB for BiH; geoBoundaries ADM2 has only 12 units. Follow up with the Agency for Statistics (bhas.gov.ba) codes.

## Sources that work
Index.hr, Klix, N1, RSE, paragraf.ba, FES library (library.fes.de redirects to collections.fes.de). The parliaments' registries were not searched.
