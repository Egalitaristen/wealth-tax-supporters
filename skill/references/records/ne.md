# Niger: where the official record is and how to read it

This file covers official legislative and governmental records on wealth taxation and fiscal policy in Niger.

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **Register of bills and tax code:** The Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) maintains tax legislation at `https://dgi.gouv.ne`.
- **Search terms:** `impôt sur la fortune`, `ISF`, `taxe sur la fortune`, `impôt sur le patrimoine`, `justice fiscale`, `harajin dukiya`.
- **Statutory base:** The General Tax Code (*Code Général des Impôts du Niger*). It establishes taxes on business profits (ISB), individual salaries (IUTS), VAT, registration duties on inherited estate shares, and real property taxes (Taxe Foncière Unique - TFU). It contains no annual net wealth tax.
- **Transitional executive ordonnances:** Since the July 2023 coup d'état, legislative enactments are issued as ordonnances by the Conseil National pour la Sauvegarde de la Patrie (CNSP), including Ordonnance n° 2023-13 creating the *Fonds de Solidarité pour la Sauvegarde de la Patrie* (FSSP).

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Plenary and committee records:** The Assemblée Nationale du Niger (`https://www.assemblee.ne`) was dissolved following the 26 July 2023 coup d'état. Historical plenary debates were published via the Journal Officiel de la République du Niger (JORN) and reported daily by the *Agence Nigérienne de Presse* (ANP, `https://www.anp.ne`) and *Le Sahel* (`https://lesahel.org`).
- **Roll calls:** Under the 2010 Constitution, votes in the Assemblée Nationale were generally recorded by show of hands or secret ballot; nominal roll-call records (*scrutins publics*) were reserved for votes of confidence or constitutional amendments. No wealth-tax bill or amendment was tabled between 2010 and 2023.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **Dissolved National Assembly:** 171 seats elected in 2020–2021: PNDS-Tarayya (79), MODEN/FA Lumana Africa (19), MNSD-Nassara (13), CPR-Inganci (8), MPN Kiishin Kassa (6), ANDP-Zaman Lahiya (3), RPP-Farilla (2), ARD-Adaltchi Mutuntchi (2), AMEN-AMIN (2), others and diaspora seats.
- **Current status:** Political party activities were suspended post-coup; executive and legislative powers reside in the CNSP and the transitional cabinet led by Prime Minister Ali Lamine Zeine.

## 4. Other official records

- **Court and administrative audits:** The Cour des Comptes du Niger (`https://courdescomptes.ne`) publishes annual audit reports on state accounts and public enterprises.
- **International tax agreements:** The bilateral double taxation convention between France and Niger (originally 1 June 1965, amended 1972) was officially denounced on 5 December 2023.

## 5. Access notes

- `assemblee.ne` has intermittent uptime following institutional suspension.
- `anp.ne`, `lesahel.org`, and `lesechosduniger.com` are freely accessible.
- UN Digital Library (`digitallibrary.un.org`) provides open voting records for UN General Assembly resolutions.
