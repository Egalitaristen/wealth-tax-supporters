# Suriname: where the official record is and how to read it

This file covers official legislative and governmental records on wealth taxation in Suriname.

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **Register of bills:** De Nationale Assemblée (DNA) maintains a legislation repository at `https://dna.sr/wetgeving/` and `https://dna.sr/wetgeving/surinaamse-wetten/geldende-teksten-t-m-2005/`.
- **Search terms:** `vermogensbelasting`, `belasting op vermogen`, `Wet Vermogensbelasting 1944`, `belastingvrije som`, `stichting`.
- **Statutory base:** The core statute is the **Wet Vermogensbelasting 1944** (Gouvernementsblad 1944 no. 185; promulgated G.B. 1960 no. 85, amended by S.B. 1980 no. 104/116, S.B. 1984 no. 106, S.B. 2002 no. 107, and S.B. 2004 no. 152).
- **Executive and reform packages:** The Ministry of Finance and Planning (`https://gov.sr/ministeries/ministerie-van-financien-en-planning/`) and Belastingdienst Suriname (`https://www.belastingdienst.sr`) prepare tax packages (such as the *Algemene Wet Belastingen* and *Invorderingswet* packages).

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Plenary and committee records:** DNA plenary sessions are publicly announced at `https://dna.sr/het-politiek-college/vergaderingen/`. DNA streams plenary debates live and maintains video archives on its official YouTube channel (`DNA Suriname`). Full text verbatim Hansard transcripts are not published as open searchable text on the DNA portal; plenary debates are covered with direct verbatim reporting by domestic news platforms (*Dagblad Suriname*, *Waterkant*, *SUN*).
- **Roll calls:** DNA generally votes by acclamation or party faction voting, with individual roll calls held on constitutional or contentious issues. Individual speeches during budget and tax debates provide the primary record of member positions.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **Official member list:** Current sitting members for the 2020–2025 term are listed at `https://dna.sr/het-politiek-college/leden/`.
- **Historic members:** Former members are indexed at `https://dna.sr/het-politiek-college/gewezen-leden/`.
- **Chamber composition:** 51 seats: VHP 20, NDP 16, ABOP 8, NPS 3, PL 2, BEP 2.
- **Wikidata coverage:** High coverage for DNA members (Amzad Abdoel `Q17269702`, Ronny Asabina `Q17269685`, etc.).

## 4. Other official records

- **Court and legal records:** Surinaams Juristenblad (SJB) and legal treatises on Surinamese tax law cover tax litigation and jurisprudence under the Wet Vermogensbelasting 1944.
- **Trade union claims:** Ravaksur (Raad van Vakcentrales in Suriname) publishes formal tripartite negotiation packages and demands directly covered in the press.

## 5. Access notes

- `dna.sr` and `belastingdienst.sr` are freely accessible to plain HTTP clients without bot checks.
- News portals `dbsuriname.com` and `waterkant.net` support standard REST/WP API endpoints.
- `keynews.sr` returns 403 to plain HTTP clients.
