# Serbia: where the official record is

Notes from R001 (2026-11-13). Nothing here is a source for a claim in the register.

- **Bills and amendments:** `https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/archive/files/lat/pdf/predlozi_zakona/<convocation>/...` (one PDF per bill). The site search is poor; use web search with *predlog zakona* and the title. No wealth tax bill found. The asset-origin bill is `.../2019/3199-19 - Lat..pdf` (not read).
- **Stenograms, votes, members:** per-convocation pages on `parlament.gov.rs`; the pages still showed the 2022 convocation in November 2026. The 15th convocation has not confirmed its mandates yet.
- **Election results:** RIK, `https://www.rik.parlament.gov.rs/542645/rezultati/`, is script-driven and returns no data to plain clients; minutes are at `/zapisnici/779676`. Use a press report of the final results or a browser.
- **Coverage log:** no bill, motion, amendment or speech on a wealth tax was found or searched in the stenograms (R001).
