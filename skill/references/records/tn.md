# Tunisia: where the official record is and how to read it

Seed pass record notes (R001, 2026-11-11). Nothing here is a source for a claim in the register.

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **Finance bill (PLF) 2026**, article 50: *impôt sur la fortune*. Enacted as article 88 of the 2026 finance law (renumbered). The wording was taken from the press ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/10/16/impot-sur-la-fortune-qui-sera-reellement-concerne-par-le-plf-2026/)); the Official Gazette (JORT, `jort.gov.tn`) text was not read.
- **Explanatory note** of the tax administration (DGELF), *Note générale n° 13 de 2026*, June 2026: `https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/مذكرة-عامة-عدد-13-لسنة-2026.pdf` (not read).
- **Predecessor:** decree-law 2022-79 (finance law 2023) article 23, the real estate wealth tax.
- **Search terms:** Arabic *الضريبة على الثروة*, *الفصل 50*, *مشروع قانون المالية لسنة 2026*; French *impôt sur la fortune*, *article 50*, *PLF 2026*.

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Committee minutes** (محضر جلسة, finance and budget committee) are PDFs at `https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/<id>`: 115857 (11 November 2025, hearing of the Finance Minister), 115861 (14 November, professional bodies), 115867 (18 November, article by article, no wealth tax passage in the pages read). The minutes summarise what each speaker said without naming deputies.
- **Plenary:** sittings of 1, 2 and 4 December 2025. The press gives the counts (72 for, 14 against, 16 abstentions on article 50; 89-23-12 on the law). No per-member list and no plenary minutes were found on arp.tn; `arp.tn` has a "أرشيف المداولات" (debate archive) not searched in R001.
- **Standing order:** article 112 lets the initiator re-table an article, which is how the minister brought article 50 back.
- **Joint sittings:** the finance committees of the ARP and of the CNRD examine the bill together before the plenary.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **ARP:** `https://arp.tn/deputies` (names, Arabic only; paginated by a script) and `https://arp.tn/blocs` (404). Blocs, not parties, structure the chamber.
- **CNRD:** `https://www.cnrd.tn` (not read).
- Wikidata coverage of 2023 members was not checked.

## 4. Other official records

- Ministry of Finance (`finances.gov.tn`) and DGELF notes; the Court of Audit and the Constitutional Court were not searched.

## 5. Access notes

- Press pages open to a plain client with a browser User-Agent; allAfrica gives 403.
- arp.tn documents download with a browser User-Agent. Read PDFs with `pypdf` when `pdftotext` drops Arabic glyphs.
- The `arp.tn/ar_SY/...` 404 page lists the real menu paths (`/deputies`, `/blocs`); old paths of the previous site no longer work.
