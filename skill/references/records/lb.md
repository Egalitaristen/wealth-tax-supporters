# Lebanon: where the official record is

Notes from R001 (2026-11-13). Nothing here is a source for a claim in the register.

- **Bill proposals, members, blocs:** `lp.gov.lb` (Parliament). Not read in R001; the Joumblatt bill of 14 April 2021 is known from the Legal Agenda Parliamentary Observatory's analysis, which also lists its brackets.
- **Parliamentary monitoring:** the Legal Agenda Parliamentary Observatory (`legal-agenda.com`, "المرصد البرلماني") assessed the 2021 proposals that never reached the plenary agenda.
- **Election results:** Ministry of Interior and Municipalities; the register used the Wikipedia table for the 15 May 2022 election. The 2026 election was postponed to 2028 by a vote of 9 March 2026.
- **Coverage log:** one bill found through a secondary report; the parliament's bill register and committee records were not read.
