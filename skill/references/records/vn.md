# Vietnam: where the official record is and how to read it

Records guide for the National Assembly of Vietnam (*Quốc hội Việt Nam*) and Ministry of Finance (*Bộ Tài chính*). Seed pass (R001, November 2026).

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **National Assembly Portal:** [quochoi.vn](https://quochoi.vn). The official legislative portal publishes bills (*dự án luật*), resolutions (*nghị quyết*), session agendas, and deputy speeches.
- **Digital Library of the National Assembly:** [thuvienso.quochoi.vn](https://thuvienso.quochoi.vn). Hosts parliamentary research papers, comparative tax law studies, and legislative summaries.
- **Ministry of Finance Portal:** [mof.gov.vn](https://mof.gov.vn) and [vibonline.com.vn](https://vibonline.com.vn) (VCCI regulatory consultation portal). Publishes draft laws during public consultation periods.
- **Search terms:**
  - `thuế tài sản ròng` (net wealth tax)
  - `thuế người giàu` / `thuế người siêu giàu` (tax on the rich / super-rich)
  - `thuế tỷ phú` (billionaire tax)
  - `thuế tài sản` (property/wealth tax)
- **Bills on record:** No net wealth tax bill has been submitted to the National Assembly. In April 2018, the Ministry of Finance published the *Dự án Luật Thuế tài sản* (Draft Law on Property Tax) for public consultation, proposing annual taxes on housing above 700M/1B VND, non-agricultural land, and luxury vehicles (cars >1.5B VND, aircraft, yachts). It was shelved following public consultation and never placed on the formal legislative agenda (*chương trình xây dựng luật, pháp lệnh*).

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Plenary and committee records:** National Assembly plenary debates are broadcast on television (VTV1) and covered by the parliamentary daily *Đại biểu Nhân dân* ([daibieunhandan.vn](https://daibieunhandan.vn)). Written verbatim transcripts (*biên bản kỳ họp*) are prepared internally by the Office of the National Assembly (*Văn phòng Quốc hội*) and summarized in session press releases.
- **Roll calls:** Plenary votes are conducted electronically. In standard legislative practice, the National Assembly votes by pressing electronic buttons at seats, and the presiding officer announces the aggregate totals (number in favour, against, abstaining, and percentage). Individual named roll-call vote records (*biên bản biểu quyết ghi danh*) are not published for general legislative votes.
- **Article votes:** When controversial articles are considered, the Standing Committee (*Ủy ban Thường vụ Quốc hội*) may submit separate articles for preliminary vote before the full bill is adopted. No wealth tax article has ever reached a vote.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **Official member list:** [quochoi.vn/hoatdongcuaquochoi/cackydai/Pages/danhsachdaibieu.aspx](https://quochoi.vn/hoatdongcuaquochoi/cackydai/Pages/danhsachdaibieu.aspx) lists all 499 deputies of the 15th National Assembly (2021–2026) with their provincial delegation (*đoàn đại biểu Quốc hội*), full biography, committee assignment, and party status.
- **Parties:** 485 members belong to the Communist Party of Vietnam (*Đảng Cộng sản Việt Nam*); 14 members are independents / non-party deputies (*ngoài Đảng*).

## 4. Other official records

- **Central Committee of the CPV:** Resolutions of the Central Committee set binding legislative directions. Resolution 18-NQ/TW (5th Plenum, 13th Central Committee, June 2022) directed the enactment of progressive tax rates on individuals holding multiple real estate properties or speculative land, initiating the current draft Real Estate Tax law (*Luật Thuế bất động sản*).
- **Audit and Statistics:** State Audit of Vietnam (*Kiểm toán Nhà nước*, [sav.gov.vn](https://sav.gov.vn)) and General Statistics Office (*Tổng cục Thống kê*, [gso.gov.vn](https://gso.gov.vn)) publish public finance data and administrative classifications.

## 5. Access notes

- `quochoi.vn` and `mof.gov.vn` respond cleanly over HTTPS without geoblocking or aggressive bot challenges.
- Vietnamese text uses Unicode UTF-8 (precomposed). Official documents are predominantly in Vietnamese; summaries and press releases from the Government Portal ([baochinhphu.vn](https://baochinhphu.vn)) are occasionally available in English.
