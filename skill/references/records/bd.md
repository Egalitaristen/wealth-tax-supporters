# Bangladesh: where the official record is and how to read it

Seed pass record notes (R001, 11 November 2026). Nothing here is a source for a claim in the register; the register's own sources are. Parliament and ministry sites were not reachable in R001, so this guide is mostly a list of what to try.

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **Budget speeches and Finance Acts.** The wealth surcharge is set in the annual Finance Act. The speech text is on the Ministry of Finance site (`mof.gov.bd`) and the NBR site (`nbr.gov.bd`); neither was opened in R001. The 2019 speech is quoted in [bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/budget2019-20/finance-minister-proposes-0.1-surcharge-for-wealth-over-tk-500m).
- **Wealth Tax Act, 1963**: repealed in 1999 (per the CPD/TBS article in the evidence). The repeal act and its debate were not found.
- **Wealth Tax Act, 2026 (draft)**: reported by the Observer ([observerbd.com/news/579042](https://observerbd.com/news/579042), lead D0006); not read. The government dropped the plan before the 11 June 2026 budget.
- **Search terms.** English: wealth tax, wealth surcharge, net wealth. Bengali: সম্পদ কর, নিট সম্পদ কর, সম্পদ সারচার্জ, সম্পদ কর আইন. Not wealth taxes: inheritance tax (উত্তরাধিকার কর), property tax, holding tax, land development tax.
- **Government proposals.** NBR "Medium and Long-term Revenue Strategy FY2025-26 to FY2034-35" (June 2025; per the Financial Express); not read.

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Jatiya Sangsad.** Debate records are not published online in a searchable form that was reachable; the parliament site is `parliament.gov.bd`. Budget debate reports appear in the Daily Star, Financial Express, UNB and Dhaka Tribune; read those and quote the member's own words only from a page that prints them.
- **Roll calls.** Not found.
- **Committees.** Standing Committee on the Ministry of Finance: not checked.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **Member list.** No machine-readable list reached; no Seats tab. The parliament's own member pages are the place to look; Wikipedia and Wikidata list constituencies (named `Comilla-10` style).
- Kamal's constituency, Comilla-10, is placed in the Cumilla district unit (G0021); constituencies do not match districts one to one.

## 4. Other official records

- **Task Force on Economic Strategy Redesign** (interim government, 2024 to 2025): report known only from Prothom Alo; not found online.
- **NBR data** on wealth-surcharge payers (about 11,000 taxpayers in 2026 per TBS).
- **CPD** pre-budget and post-budget reactions are on `cpd.org.bd` and are republished with a date; CPD pages often reprint a press article, so cite the date of the original article.

## 5. Access notes

- Plain scripts read The Daily Star, TBS, Prothom Alo English, CPD, bdnews24, Financial Express and Asia News Network. Dhaka Tribune and Bangla Tribune answer 403. The Wire needs a browser (article body only in JSON-LD).
- A `User-Agent` of a normal browser is enough for the readable sites. Dates are in `article:published_time` or `datePublished` meta tags; the Daily Star prints "Published :" in the text.
- Bengali outlets were not read; open them in a browser.
