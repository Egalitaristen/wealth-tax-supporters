# United Kingdom: official legislative records

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- [UK Parliament bills and publications](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/3041/publications) lists successive bill texts, amendments, selection and proceedings papers, and Hansard by stage. Search “wealth tax”, “net wealth tax”, “wealth levy”, “tax on wealth”, “billionaire tax”, and review Finance Bill amendment papers separately: bill titles do not identify individual new clauses.
- [Early day motions](https://edm.parliament.uk/early-day-motion/62680/2-wealth-tax) print the text, sponsors and individual dated signatures. They are non-binding motions and signing is not a division vote. [EDM 1725](https://edm.parliament.uk/early-day-motion/64124/proposal-for-a-wealth-tax) calls for a 2% annual tax above £10m.
- The 14 September 2021 [committee amendment paper](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/58-02/0160/amend/HSocLevy_day_cwh_0914.pdf) shows NC9, a review of a tax on assets over £5m; NC8 concerns income from wealth. NC9 is an assessment, not a tax-creating clause: do not infer support from its names alone. Its selection and outcome remain to be checked against the official papers.

## 2. Debates and votes

- [Hansard](https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/2021-09-14/debates/379AC113-D248-4B5D-B50B-C6839E4257AE/HealthAndSocialCareLevyBill) provides speaker-labelled verbatim Commons proceedings in HTML, with clauses and divisions in context. Search the named new clause and all stages; confirm question put and each member's own entry in the official list. Discard votes on a whole Finance Bill or an unrelated review.
- UK bills use clauses and numbered new clauses, rather than gazette article numbering. Compare the amendment paper, grouping and selection, proceedings and reprinted versions; do not assume an NC was selected or enacted from its appearance on the amendment paper. The 2021 Lords bill inspected is HL Bill 52; mapping NC9 requires the Commons selection and proceedings papers.
- The 2022 repeal debate includes Burgon's own one-off wealth-tax statement, but he explicitly says his new clause 2 addresses income from wealth; do not treat a vote on that clause as a wealth-tax vote.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- [Official Commons member record](https://members.parliament.uk/member/4493/career) identifies member, party and constituency across terms. Check living status separately and match historic MPs carefully.

## 4. Other official records

- The bill publications page links Lords debates and Commons committee papers. Devolved parliaments, Treasury papers and questions remain to be systematically searched.
