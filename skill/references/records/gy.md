# Guyana: where the official record is and how to read it

This file covers official legislative and governmental records on wealth taxation in Guyana.

## 1. Bills, motions and proposals

- **Register of bills:** The Parliament of the Co-operative Republic of Guyana maintains an official repository of bills and statutory enactments at:
  - `https://parliament.gov.gy/chamber-business/bills-status`
  - `https://parliament.gov.gy/chamber-business/acts-of-parliament`
- **Search terms:** `"property tax"`, `"Property Tax Act"`, `"net property"`, `"wealth tax"`, `"Cap. 81:21"`, `"Section 6(y)"`, `"Kaldor Budget"`.
- **Statutory base:**
  - Principal statute: **Property Tax Act (Chapter 81:21)**, originally enacted as the *Property Tax and Gift Tax Act 1962* (Act No. 19 of 1962), promulgated under the Cheddi Jagan PPP administration on the advice of Nicholas Kaldor.
  - *Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2013* (Act No. 11 of 2013; Bill No. 11/2013): introduced by Minister Dr. Ashni Singh; updated valuation date to 2011, raised the individual exemption threshold from GYD $7.5M to GYD $40M, and set the corporate threshold to GYD $10M.
  - *Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2018* (Act No. 19 of 2018; Bill No. 17/2018): introduced by Minister Winston Jordan; raised filing thresholds to GYD $40M for both individuals and companies, and reduced the rate on the first GYD $20M above threshold from 0.75% to 0.5%.
  - *Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2024* (Act No. 2 of 2024; Bill No. 2/2024): introduced by Minister Dr. Ashni Singh; inserted Section 6(y) into the Property Tax Act, exempting all individuals from property tax and return filing obligations from income year 2025 onwards (Year of Assessment 2026 onwards). Companies remain subject to the tax.
- **Executive and budget speeches:** Ministry of Finance (`https://finance.gov.gy`) publishes annual national budget speeches, estimates of revenue and expenditure, and tax circulars.

## 2. Debates and votes

- **Plenary records (Hansard):** Verbatim sitting transcripts (Hansards) of the National Assembly are published at `https://parliament.gov.gy/publications/hansard`. Modern sittings (2010–present) are published as text-searchable PDFs grouped by Parliament and Sitting Number.
- **Audio and visual archives:** Sittings are live-streamed and archived on the official YouTube channel (`Parliament of Guyana`).
- **Roll calls and voting procedures:** Voting in the National Assembly is normally taken by voice ("Ayes" and "Nos") or by standing division if claimed by a member under Standing Order 48. Sittings in January 2019 (passing Bill 17/2018) were boycotted by the PPP/C following the December 21, 2018 No-Confidence Motion. Bill 2/2024 passed on February 2, 2024 with the 33-vote governing majority.

## 3. Members, parties, seats

- **Official member list:** Current parliamentarians of the Twelfth Parliament (2020–present) are indexed with portrait and party affiliation at `https://parliament.gov.gy/about-parliament/parliamentarian`.
- **Historic members:** Former parliamentarians across earlier Parliaments (First through Eleventh) are listed in the historical directory on the parliamentary portal.
- **Chamber composition:** 65 voting members: PPP/C 33, APNU+AFC 31 (22 APNU, 9 AFC), Joinder List (LJP/ANUG/TNM) 1.
- **Wikidata coverage:** High coverage for National Assembly members (Irfaan Ali `Q5920339`, Ashni Singh `Q4805212`, Winston Jordan `Q20858021`, Christopher Ram `Q124748365`).

## 4. Other official records

- **Guyana Revenue Authority (GRA):** `https://www.gra.gov.gy` — guidance notes, Property Tax Individual (PT 1) and Company (PT 2) return forms, statutory thresholds, and public notices regarding Section 6(y).
- **Official Gazette of Guyana:** `https://officialgazette.gov.gy` — official promulgation of Acts of Parliament, ministerial orders, commencement orders, and subsidiary legislation.
- **Ministry of Legal Affairs:** `https://mola.gov.gy` — revised Laws of Guyana database.

## 5. Access notes

- `parliament.gov.gy`, `gra.gov.gy`, and `officialgazette.gov.gy` are freely accessible to standard HTTP clients without Cloudflare or bot barriers.
- PDF Hansards for the Tenth (2011–2015), Eleventh (2015–2020), and Twelfth (2020–present) Parliaments are digital text PDFs searchable with `pdftotext` or standard Python PDF parsers.
