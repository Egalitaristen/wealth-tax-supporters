## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **2014.** Economist Elyès Jouini calls for an *impôt sur la fortune* of about 1% on capital in an op-ed ([Leaders, 1 October 2014](https://www.leaders.com.tn/article/15170-elyes-jouini-vers-une-fiscalite-plus-equitable-l-impot-progressif-sur-la-fortune), `A0014`).
- **2020.** L'Économiste Maghrébin's editorial of 24 February says an ISF "serait une vraie niche" for revenue and fairness, with conditions (`A0015`). In May 2020 Jouini again calls for a solidarity tax on wealth during the Covid crisis ([L'Économiste Maghrébin](https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/?p=325710), [La Presse, 17 May 2020](https://lapresse.tn/62249/elyes-jouini-universitaire-et-ancien-ministre-lutter-contre-leconomie-de-rente-ce-nest-pas-lutter-contre-les-grandes-entreprises/)). Nawaat publishes a signed piece proposing a solidarity tax on fortune ([28 August 2020](https://nawaat.org/2020/08/28/la-tunisie-de-lenfer-fiscal-a-la-justice-sociale/), `A0016`).
- **2022.** Amine Bouzaïene's report for Al Bawsala proposes a tax on large fortunes covering the richest 10% (`A0011`, `A0012`; [report](https://www.albawsala.com/files/2022/06/Rapport-La-justice-fiscale-.pdf)). The 2023 finance law (decree-law 2022-79, article 23) sets a 0.5% tax on real estate worth 3 million dinars or more, which the register counts as a property tax (`X0011`).
- **2023.** On 1 June President Saïed floats "additional taxes" on those who benefit from subsidies without needing them ([North Africa Journal](https://north-africa.com/tunisias-president-considering-taxing-the-wealtht-to-prevent-imf-meddling/)): a subsidy idea, not a wealth tax (`X0010`). Oxfam's October 2023 MENA briefing covers Tunisia (`A0013`).
- **PLF 2026 (October 2025).** The government bill (2025/114, initiated by the Presidency of the Republic) has an article 50 that replaces the 2023 provision with an annual *impôt sur la fortune* on the immovable and movable property of individuals: 0.5% on 3 to 5 million dinars, 1% above, main residence, professional assets and cars up to 12 CV exempt, declaration by 30 June ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/10/16/impot-sur-la-fortune-qui-sera-reellement-concerne-par-le-plf-2026/)).
- **11 to 25 November 2025, committees.** The Finance Minister presents the tax to the ARP finance committee on 11 November (`E00001`, [minutes](https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/115857)); professional bodies ask to exempt professional assets and raise the threshold to 10 million dinars ([minutes](https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/115861)). On 21 November the two finance committees sit together on article 50: deputies object (no feasibility study, no means to value private property, risks for investment and savings) and propose two amendments (bank and post deposits excluded; 0.5% for 2 to 3 million dinars and 1% above 3 million); the CNRD committee accepts the article, the ARP committee does not, and the CNRD members leave the sitting with the ARP rapporteur ([minutes](https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/115873), `E00021`). The ARP committee drops it, 10 against and 3 for ([Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/11/24/bras-de-fer-autour-de-limpot-sur-la-fortune-larp-dit-non-le-conseil-national-dit-oui/1374553/)).
- **26 November.** Deputy Ali Zaghdoud announces that the bloc "Pour que le peuple triomphe" will re-table the article with higher rates, 0.5% to 1% for 3 to 5 million dinars and 1% to 3% above ([Webdo](https://www.webdo.tn/fr/actualite/national/impot-sur-la-fortune-le-bloc-pour-que-le-peuple-triomphe-veut-retablir-larticle-50/388386/)). On 28 November the committee chairs sign a document on how to settle the dispute and the minister pledges to bring the article back to the plenary ([Jawhara FM](https://www.jawharafm.net/ar/article/%D8%B3%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85--%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%87%D8%AF%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D8%A5%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%B7%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D8%B5%D9%84-50-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%85%D8%A9/105/285192), [L'Économiste Maghrébin](https://www.leconomistemaghrebin.com/2025/11/28/arp-slim-salem-fin-du-bras-de-fer-sur-limpot-sur-la-fortune-avant-ladoption-de-la-lf-2026/)).
- **1 December.** The plenary rejects the article: 30 for, 14 abstentions, 69 against, 113 deputies present ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/01/parlement-larticle-50-sur-limpot-sur-la-fortune-supprime/)); the OTE issues a communiqué asking parliamentarians to reinstate and strengthen it (`E00011`).
- **4 December.** The minister re-tables a revised article under article 112 of the standing orders; the ARP adopts it, 72 for, 14 against, 16 abstentions, then the whole law 89-23-12 ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/05/loi-de-finances-2026-larp-adopte-le-texte-final-et-valide-limpot-sur-la-fortune/), [Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/12/04/plf-2026-lassemblee-adopte-limpot-sur-la-fortune-apres-un-premier-rejet/1376661/)). The task brief gave a 5 million dinar threshold; the sources give 3 million (0.5%) and 5 million (1%).
- **12 December 2025.** Law 2025-17 is published in the Official Gazette n. 148; the tax is article 88 (text read on pp. 4254-4255, [PDF](https://arp.tn/document/download/115725)). It exempts money deposited in banks, financial institutions and the post office.
- **2026.** General note 13 of 2026 of the tax administration (June) explains the tax; the first returns were due 30 June 2026 ([Kapitalis](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/2026/06/14/tunisie-les-modalites-dapplication-de-limpot-sur-la-fortune/)).
- **Opposition (not recorded):** the ARP finance committee majority, UTICA ([Courrier de l'Atlas](https://www.lecourrierdelatlas.com/le-patronat-tunisien-demande-le-retrait-du-nouvel-impot-sur-la-fortune/)), Ridha Chkoundali ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/03/impot-sur-la-fortune-chkandali-denonce-une-mesure-dangereuse-et-appelle-a-la-supprimer-du-projet-de-loi-de-finances/)), Maher Ktari, Anis Wahabi, Mehdi Maazoun, and a Business News column. Tarek Mahdi, named in the brief, was not found.

## 4. Parties and blocs
Parliament is organised in blocs, not parties. Only the bloc "Pour que le peuple triomphe" (`A0008`) was found asking to restore the tax, with higher rates. No party manifesto or statement was found for the tax: searches for the Mouvement Echaab, Courant populaire, Workers' Party and others returned nothing (Arabic, R002). The bill was initiated by the Presidency of the Republic; the register records the Finance Minister who presented it, not the President (no statement of his on a wealth tax).

- **Structure:** 24 governorates, 264 delegations, 350 municipalities. Taxation of wealth is a national power; no local actor was found, none expected.
