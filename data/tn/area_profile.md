## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Real estate wealth tax, 2023.** The 2023 finance law (decree-law 2022-79, article 23) set a 0.5% tax on real estate worth 3 million dinars or more ([Oxfam MENA briefing](https://www.oxfamfrance.org/app/uploads/2023/10/Oxfam_The-Middle-East-and-North-Africa-gap_report-051023.pdf), [OTE bulletin](https://www.economie-tunisie.org/sites/default/files/econews_68_arabe.pdf)). A tax on one asset class is a property tax and is not counted (`X0011`).
- **2022 report.** Amine Bouzaïene's report for Al Bawsala proposed a tax on large fortunes covering the richest 10% (`A0011`, `A0012`; [report](https://www.albawsala.com/files/2022/06/Rapport-La-justice-fiscale-.pdf)).
- **2023 Oxfam briefing** on Egypt, Lebanon, Morocco and Tunisia calls for wealth taxes (`A0013`).
- **PLF 2026 (October 2025).** The government bill's article 50 abolishes the 2023 provision and creates an annual *impôt sur la fortune* on the immovable and movable property of individuals, 0.5% on 3 to 5 million dinars and 1% above, main residence, professional assets and cars up to 12 CV exempt, declaration by 30 June ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/10/16/impot-sur-la-fortune-qui-sera-reellement-concerne-par-le-plf-2026/)).
- **Hearings, 11-18 November 2025.** The Finance Minister presents the tax to the ARP finance committee (`E00001`, [minutes](https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/115857)); professional bodies ask to exempt professional assets and raise the threshold to 10 million dinars ([minutes](https://arp.tn/ar_SY/document/download/115861)).
- **Committee stage, around 21-24 November 2025.** The CNRD finance committee approves the article with amendments; the ARP finance committee rejects it (10 against, 3 for) ([Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/11/24/bras-de-fer-autour-de-limpot-sur-la-fortune-larp-dit-non-le-conseil-national-dit-oui/1374553/)).
- **1 December 2025.** The plenary rejects the article again ([Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/12/01/le-parlement-rejette-a-nouveau-larticle-sur-limpot-sur-la-fortune-en-pleniere/1375983/)); the OTE publishes a communiqué asking parliamentarians to reinstate and strengthen it (`E00011`).
- **4 December 2025.** The minister re-tables the article under article 112 of the standing orders; the ARP adopts it, 72 for, 14 against, 16 abstentions, and the whole finance law 89-23-12 ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/12/05/loi-de-finances-2026-larp-adopte-le-texte-final-et-valide-limpot-sur-la-fortune/), [Business News](https://businessnews.com.tn/2025/12/04/plf-2026-lassemblee-adopte-limpot-sur-la-fortune-apres-un-premier-rejet/1376661/)). The task brief gave a 5 million dinar threshold; the sources give 3 million dinars (0.5%) and 5 million (1%).
- **2026.** The tax replaces the 2023 real estate tax (article 88 of the finance law). The DGELF explanatory note 13/2026 is dated June 2026 and the first returns were due 30 June 2026 ([Kapitalis](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/2026/06/14/tunisie-les-modalites-dapplication-de-limpot-sur-la-fortune/), [Tuniscope](https://www.tuniscope.com/article/434922/actualites/societe/tunisie-593812)).
- **Opposition (not recorded):** UTICA asked for withdrawal ([Courrier de l'Atlas](https://www.lecourrierdelatlas.com/le-patronat-tunisien-demande-le-retrait-du-nouvel-impot-sur-la-fortune/)); economist Ridha Chkoundali ([La Presse](https://www.lapresse.tn/2025/11/03/impot-sur-la-fortune-chkandali-denonce-une-mesure-dangereuse-et-appelle-a-la-supprimer-du-projet-de-loi-de-finances/)); the finance committee majority. Tarek Mahdi, named in the brief, was not found on any page read.

## 4. Parties
Tunisian parliamentary politics since 2022 is organised in blocs, not parties. Only the bloc "Pour que le peuple triomphe" (`A0008`) was found asking to restore the tax, with higher rates (1% to 3% above 5 million dinars). No party manifesto since 2010 was searched (open lead D0010).
