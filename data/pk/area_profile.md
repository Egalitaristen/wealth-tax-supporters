## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Historic Wealth Tax Act, 1963:** Pakistan had an active annual net wealth tax under the *Wealth Tax Act, 1963*, which applied progressive rates to movable and immovable property. It was repealed under military ruler Pervez Musharraf via the *Finance Act, 2003* under elite lobbying pressure.
- **MQM Alternative Proposal to RGST (2010):** In November 2010, MQM parliamentary leader Dr. Farooq Sattar (`A0015`) formally submitted an alternative revenue package to the Prime Minister proposing a wealth tax on big feudals and landlords alongside agricultural income taxation as an alternative to consumer sales taxes ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/581990/mqm-proposes-alternative-for-rgst-2)).
- **Economic Advisory Council Recommendation (2011):** In April 2011, the EAC and Revenue Advisory Council (RAC) recommended reintroducing the wealth tax to expand the direct tax base ([The News](https://jang.com.pk/thenews/apr2011-weekly/nos-24-04-2011/pol1.htm)). Local tax bars, including Tax Bar Association Larkana (`A0013`), echoed calls for wealth tax revival ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/605732)).
- **Income Support Levy Act, 2013:** Enacted as part of the *Finance Act, 2013* by Finance Minister Ishaq Dar (`A0001`, PML-N). Imposed a 0.5% wealth levy on net declared movable assets exceeding Rs 1 million, designated for the Income Support Fund to assist the poor ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1017809/bid-to-grapple-with-economic-mess)). Following substantial elite and business pushback, it was repealed via the *Finance Act, 2014*.
- **PTI Wealth Tax Commitment (2018–2019):** Incoming Finance Minister Asad Umar (`A0002`) announced in August 2018 (interview with Bloomberg / [Radio Pakistan](https://www.radio.gov.pk/04-08-2018/pti-govt-to-reduce-taxes-on-energy-supply-factories-agriculture-asad)) that the PTI government would introduce a wealth tax to cover revenue lost from reducing industrial and energy taxes. In April 2019, at an SDPI forum, Umar confirmed draft proposals for a wealth tax and inheritance tax ([SDPI](https://sdpi.org/govt-will-make-efforts-to-develop-economic-consensus/news_detail)).
- **Civil Society Campaigns (2022):** The Pakistan Kissan Rabita Committee (`A0017`), Crofter Foundation, and Fight Inequality Alliance led actions across 10 Pakistani cities during the Global Week of Action against Inequality demanding adoption of a wealth tax on billionaires ([APMDD](https://apmdd.org/peoples-groups-and-movements-denounce-deepening-inequality-say-its-time-to-tax-the-rich-not-the-poor/)). Concurrently, Oxfam in Pakistan (`A0018`) proposed a 2% to 5% progressive wealth tax on multi-millionaires and billionaires ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1669320)).
- **Asma Jahangir Memorial Lecture (2023):** Former Planning Commission Chief Economist Dr. Pervez Tahir (`A0014`) called for re-imposing wealth tax, inheritance tax, and estate duties while halting regressive indirect taxes ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1737397)).
- **Section 7E Deemed Income Tax (Finance Act 2022):** Introduced a deemed income tax of 1% on fair market value of vacant or unutilized immovable property over Rs 25 million. This was challenged in high courts and struck down as ultra vires by the Supreme Court in 2026. (Excluded from the core register as property taxation).
- **FBR 2024–2025 Budget Proposal:** The Federal Board of Revenue proposed a wealth tax / capital value tax on movable assets and gold in 2024 budget planning. The IMF rejected the proposal during loan negotiations, instructing Pakistan to focus on personal income tax and GST ([Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2471676/imf-rejects-wealth-tax-proposal)).
- **Public Opinion (2024–2025):** Nationwide survey of 7,500 Pakistani adults conducted by ICTD (Max Gallien, Vanessa van den Boogaard) and LUMS (Dr. Umair Javed) published in ICTD Policy Brief 17 (June 2025) found 42% support for a general wealth tax, with support exceeding 70% when progressive thresholds protect ordinary families and revenues are earmarked for poverty relief ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1919276), [ICTD](https://ictd.ac/publication/public-support-for-wealth-and-progressive-taxes-in-pakistan/)).

## 4. Parties
- **Pakistan Muslim League (Nawaz) (PML-N):** Center-right / big tent. Enacted the Income Support Levy Act 2013 (wealth levy on movable assets) under Finance Minister Ishaq Dar (`A0001`), though repealed it the following year.
- **Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI):** Populist / center. Pledged to introduce a wealth tax in 2018 under Finance Minister Asad Umar (`A0002`), but shifted to conventional indirect taxes and amnesty schemes before implementation.
- **Muttahida Qaumi Movement (MQM / MQM-P) (`A0016`):** Urban party. Parliamentary leader Dr. Farooq Sattar (`A0015`) formally submitted proposals to the Prime Minister demanding wealth tax on big feudals and landlords.
- **Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP):** Center-left. Emphasizes social safety nets (Benazir Income Support Programme), but historically resisted wealth taxes on agricultural and rural landholdings due to its agrarian landlord base.
- **Haqooq-e-Khalq Party (HKP):** Democratic socialist party led by Farooq Tariq (`A0008`) and Dr. Ammar Ali Jan. Explicitly advocates progressive wealth taxation on the super-rich, land redistribution, and taxation of luxury estates.
- **Awami Workers Party (AWP):** Socialist party. 2024 manifesto focuses on progressive income taxation across all sectors including commercial agriculture, rather than a net wealth tax (`X0006`).
- **Jamaat-e-Islami (JI):** Islamist party. Regularly advocates zakat enforcement and wealth levies on feudals and corrupt elites in pre-budget white papers (`D0002`).

## 5. Government below national level
- **Structure:** 4 provinces (Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan), 1 federal capital territory (Islamabad), and 2 autonomous territories (Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Gilgit-Baltistan). Divided into 160 administrative districts (`G0011`–`G0170`).
- **Taxation powers:** Under the 18th Constitutional Amendment (2010), taxation of agricultural income and services was devolved to the provinces (e.g. Punjab Revenue Authority, Sindh Revenue Board). Taxation of wealth and capital (excluding immovable property) remained on the Federal Legislative List. Thus, subnational assemblies cannot enact a comprehensive net wealth tax; local actors call on federal authorities.
- **Local tax bars:** Bar associations at district level occasionally debate national tax policy; General Secretary of Tax Bar Association Larkana (`A0013`, `G0154`) published a notable call for wealth tax revival.
- **Boundaries source:** OCHA / HDX Pakistan Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB, CC BY-IGO 3.0, `https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-pak`). Merged into `data/pk/boundaries.json` (160 districts, 7 regions).

  - `براہ راست ٹیکس` (direct tax)
- **Watch terms (excluded taxes):**
  - `Capital Value Tax` / `CVT`: Single-asset transaction tax on property purchases or vehicle registrations.
  - `Section 7E` / `Deemed income tax`: Vacant land/property tax.
  - `Agricultural Income Tax` (`زرعی انکم ٹیکس`): Income tax on farm profits, devolved to provinces.
