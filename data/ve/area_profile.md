## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Impuesto a los Grandes Patrimonios (IGP):** created by the "Ley Constitucional" of the 2017 National Constituent Assembly (ANC), dated 2 July 2019 and published in Gaceta Oficial 41.667 of 3 July 2019, reprinted for material errors in Gaceta 41.696 of 16 August 2019 ([El Español / EFE, 3 Jul 2019](https://www.elespanol.com/invertia/economia/20190703/constituyente-chavista-aprueba-impuesto-grandes-patrimonios/410960325_0.html); [reprint](https://www.venamcham.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Gaceta-Oficial-41696-Ley-Impuesto-Patrimonio-Reimpresion.pdf)). It taxes the net wealth of individuals and companies that the tax authority classes as special taxpayers; the reprint set the entry threshold at 150 million tax units (the first text said 36 million for individuals and 100 million for companies). Rate: 0.25% to 1.5%, fixed by the Executive; 0.25% since 2019. Accrual date: 30 September each year.
- **Status: in force and collected.** The tax authority itself opened the 2023-24 filing and payment period on 2 October 2024 ([Seniat via the Ministry of Economy and Finance](https://www.mppef.gob.ve/seniat-inicia-plazo-para-declaracion-y-pago-del-impuesto-a-los-grandes-patrimonios/)); a Forvis Mazars note of September 2023 calls it the fifth year of application at 0.25% ([Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ve/es/insights/forvis-mazars-insights/breves-comentarios-sobre-el-igp)); a 2026 practitioner guide treats the 2026 filing as due and values the threshold at the September 2025 tax unit ([Nayma Consultores](https://naymaconsultores.com/claves-para-declarar-impuesto-a-los-grandes-patrimonios/)). The [2026 SENIAT calendar](https://accesoalajusticia.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Calendarios_Fiscales_SENIAT_2026.pdf), Providencia SNAT/2025/000091, Article 1(h), schedules Grandes Patrimonios declarations in October and November 2026 by RIF digit. This official administrative text is a third-party copy; it establishes scheduled obligations, not actual receipts.
- **Legitimacy:** the ANC is not recognised by much of the international community, and the opposition-aligned press and Fedecámaras criticised the tax (`leads.csv`). A 2020 legal book on the tax questions the ANC's competence to create it (Meleán Brito, Universidad de los Andes).
- **May 2023:** Acción Democrática deputy Luis Eduardo Martínez said he would promote a "Ley de Impuesto al Patrimonio" for Venezuelans abroad, mainly to register their assets ([Diario Las Américas](https://www.diariolasamericas.com/america-latina/proponen-impuesto-bienes-venezolanos-el-exterior-n5335542/amp)). No bill was found; [ColombiaCheck, 1 June 2023](https://colombiacheck.com/chequeos/falso-en-venezuela-no-se-ha-aprobado-una-ley-para-que-venezolanos-en-el-exterior-paguen) interviewed Martínez and reported that the plan was dropped and his group would not vote for a similar proposal.
- **Out of definition:** the Impuesto a las Grandes Transacciones Financieras (IGTF, a tax on payments), the 2018 VAT reform and the income-tax advances. Recorded in `exclusions.csv`.

## 9. Known networks
- Broad political, legislative, and intellectual constellation across the Bolivarian movement, university academia, state media, and opposition legislative initiatives:
  - **Executive & Legislative Enactment:** Nicolás Maduro (President, introduced the wealth tax in 2017), Diosdado Cabello (ANC President), Tania Díaz (First Vice-President), Gladys Requena (Second Vice-President), Andrés Eloy Méndez (ANC Economic Commission Chair 2019), Orlando Camacho (Fedeindustria / ANC floor mover).
  - **Former opposition proposal:** Luis Eduardo Martínez announced a proposal concerning assets abroad in May 2023, but subsequently said it was dropped and his group would not vote for a similar measure.
  - **Economists & Tax Specialists:** Pasqualina Curcio (USB professor), Tony Boza (economist/deputy), Luis Salas Rodríguez (former Vice President for the Economy / UBV), Juan Carlos Valdez (tax lawyer and analyst).
  - **Public Intellectuals & Broadcasters:** Luis Britto García (essayist/columnist), Mario Silva (*La Hojilla* host / deputy), María Alejandra Díaz (constitutional lawyer / ANC constituent).
  - **Trade Unions:** Octavio Solórzano (Fenasirtrasalud / FENTRASEP).
  - **Institutional Administration:** SENIAT (national tax authority administering the IGP).
  - **Opposition and business:** Several business groups and opposition economists criticised the 2019 IGP. Support for taxing fortunes also appeared outside the governing movement: [Marea Socialista’s 2014 draft](https://www.aporrea.org/ideologia/a191599.html) proposed a 5% gross personal wealth tax, and the [civic platform’s 2018 programme](https://www.aporrea.org/ideologia/n322336.html) proposed a tax on large fortunes while criticising the government.
