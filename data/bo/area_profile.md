## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **2020 campaign:** the MAS candidate Luis Arce proposed a tax on fortunes above USD 10 million and Evo Morales backed it ([EFE via elDinero, 2 Sep 2020](https://www.eldinero.com.do/119284/es-viable-el-impuesto-a-la-riqueza-que-propone-el-partido-de-evo-morales/)).
- **Ley 1357 (28 Dec 2020):** the Chamber of Deputies approved the Impuesto a las Grandes Fortunas first; on 17 Dec 2020 the Senate made it permanent ([Correo del Sur](https://correodelsur.com/politica/20201217/senado-modifica-ley-y-establece-que-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas-sera-permanente.html)); Arce promulgated it ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.py/mundo/2020/12/29/bolivia-instaura-un-impuesto-a-los-152-mas-ricos-del-pais)): 1.4%, 1.9% and 2.4% above Bs 30 million.
- **Repeal fight:** on 25 Nov 2025 the Paz government announced it would repeal the IGF; Oxfam en Bolivia answered on 26 November ([press release](https://www.bolivia.oxfam.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/COMUNICADO_IMPUESTO_GRANDES_FORTUNAS_261125.pdf)). On 11 Feb 2026 the Chamber's planning committee said it rejected the repeal ([eju.tv](https://eju.tv/2026/02/presidente-de-la-comision-de-planificacion-anuncia-que-se-rechazo-la-anulacion-el-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas/)). A LexLatin note of 21 April 2026 still described the tax as in force `[verify: search summary only]`; the tax authority extended the 2025 filing deadline on 24 March 2026 ([RND 102600000009](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RND-No-102600000009.pdf)).
- **Opposition (not recorded):** the Paz government; the Cámara Nacional de Industrias warned of "desindustrialización" in 2020 ([elDinero](https://www.eldinero.com.do/119284/es-viable-el-impuesto-a-la-riqueza-que-propone-el-partido-de-evo-morales/)).
