## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** the Impuesto a las Grandes Fortunas (IGF), created by [Ley 1357 of 28 December 2020](https://www.lexivox.org/norms/BO-L-N1357.xhtml): a national tax on the net fortune of individuals above Bs 30,000,000 at 31 December each year, at 1.4%, 1.9% and 2.4% by bracket (arts. 5 and 9). Regulated by Decreto Supremo 4436 of 30 December 2020. The tax authority's [RND 102600000009 of 24 March 2026](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RND-No-102600000009.pdf) extends the 2025 filing deadline, so the tax was still being administered then.
- **Repeal attempt:** the government announced in November 2025 that it would remove the IGF and the financial-transactions tax. On 11 February 2026 the chair of the Chamber of Deputies' planning commission said the commission had rejected the bill to repeal the IGF and approved only the repeal of the financial-transactions tax ([eju.tv](https://eju.tv/2026/02/presidente-de-la-comision-de-planificacion-anuncia-que-se-rechazo-la-anulacion-el-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas/)). He added that the executive would have to present a new bill to reopen the debate. A search result of 21 April 2026 (LexLatin) still described the tax as in force `[verify: search summary only]`. Check the Gaceta Oficial and the Assembly's records for any later repeal before relying on the `in_force` state.
