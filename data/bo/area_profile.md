## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **2020 campaign:** the MAS candidate Luis Arce proposed a tax on fortunes above USD 10 million and Evo Morales backed it ([EFE via elDinero, 2 Sep 2020](https://www.eldinero.com.do/119284/es-viable-el-impuesto-a-la-riqueza-que-propone-el-partido-de-evo-morales/)).
- **Ley 1357 (28 Dec 2020):** the Chamber of Deputies approved the Impuesto a las Grandes Fortunas first; on 17 Dec 2020 the Senate made it permanent ([Correo del Sur](https://correodelsur.com/politica/20201217/senado-modifica-ley-y-establece-que-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas-sera-permanente.html)); Arce promulgated it ([La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.py/mundo/2020/12/29/bolivia-instaura-un-impuesto-a-los-152-mas-ricos-del-pais)): 1.4%, 1.9% and 2.4% above Bs 30 million.
- **Repeal fight:** on 25 Nov 2025 the Paz government announced it would repeal the IGF; Oxfam en Bolivia answered on 26 November ([press release](https://www.bolivia.oxfam.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/COMUNICADO_IMPUESTO_GRANDES_FORTUNAS_261125.pdf)). On 11 Feb 2026 the committee chair said the repeal was rejected ([eju.tv](https://eju.tv/2026/02/presidente-de-la-comision-de-planificacion-anuncia-que-se-rechazo-la-anulacion-el-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas/)); the [Chamber’s account](https://diputados.gob.bo/noticias/comision-de-diputados-aprobo-proyecto-de-ley-para-la-abrogacion-del-impuesto-a-las-transacciones-financieras/) says the committee did not reach consensus on approving repeal, which does not establish a collective position in favor of the tax. The same day the plenary approved only the repeal of the financial transactions tax and the minister of the Presidency said the government would insist on the IGF ([eju.tv](https://eju.tv/?p=4891564), [Oxígeno](https://oxigeno.bo/tras-su-rechazo-en-comision-el-gobierno-insistira-con-la-ley-para-eliminar-el-impuesto-a-las-grandes-fortunas/)); the repeal bill was no. 115/2025-2026. The tax authority extended the 2025 filing deadline on 24 March 2026 ([RND 102600000009](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/RND-No-102600000009.pdf)). **Status (R002, 1 Nov 2026): in force.** No later vote or repeal was found; the latest dated source is a practitioner page updated on 14 May 2026 that still describes the tax ([boliviaimpuestos.com](https://boliviaimpuestos.com/b/5V1)); the Gaceta Oficial was not read.
- **Opposition (not recorded):** the Paz government; the Cámara Nacional de Industrias warned of "desindustrialización" in 2020 ([elDinero](https://www.eldinero.com.do/119284/es-viable-el-impuesto-a-la-riqueza-que-propone-el-partido-de-evo-morales/)).
