## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** none. Kenya has no net-wealth tax; the IPF paper says it "lacks an effective and enforceable framework for taxing the wealthy" ([IPF, Dec 2025](https://ipfglobal.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/TAX-THE-RICH-CAN-KENYA-GET-WEALTH-TAXATION-RIGHT_.pdf)). Capital gains tax (raised from 5% to 15% in the Finance Act 2022), property rates and the 2023 housing levy are out of definition.
- **Treasury, 2018 to 2021:** a Draft Income Tax Bill 2018 proposed a 35% top income rate (income tax, out of definition); in March 2021 the Treasury Principal Secretary said it was "in discussions over the wealth tax" ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/treasury-plans-wealth-tax-to-fix-july-budget-3317572)). Nothing was introduced.
- **President Ruto, 29 September 2022:** told Parliament "We are overtaxing trade and undertaxing wealth" ([VOA](https://www.voaafrica.com/a/ruto-vows-tax-reform-to-reduce-inequality/6768971.html)); Business Daily read this as endorsing a wealth tax ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/ruto-pledges-wealth-tax-on-tycoons-to-fix-budget-3967380)). No bill followed.
- **Oxfam and allies, January 2023:** analysis with the Fight Inequality Alliance, the Institute for Policy Studies and Patriotic Millionaires: 1,890 Kenyans with USD 5m or more; a 2% and 3% net wealth tax could raise USD 731m a year ([Oxfam in Kenya](https://kenya.oxfam.org/node/188)). Business Daily reported an earlier (2022) Oxfam figure of up to Sh125 billion `[verify]`.
- **Finance Bill 2024:** withdrawn after the June 2024 protests. A "wealth tax" label in the press for the motor-vehicle levy is a loose use, not a net-wealth tax.
- **NTA, November 2024:** "Taxing Wealth in Kenya" proposes 1.5% / 3% / 5% tiers on about 7,200 high-net-worth individuals ([report](https://www.nta.or.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Wealth-Tax-in-Kenya.pdf)); re-presented in September 2025 `[verify: Standard photo caption]`.
- **IPF and Oxfam in Kenya, December 2025:** the "Tax the Rich!" paper; press coverage puts the potential at about Sh130 billion a year ([The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/2025-12-11-kra-missing-sh130billion-in-revenues-from-failure-to-tax-the-super-rich-1)).
- **Finance Bill 2026 (April to June 2026):** IPF, Oxfam Kenya and KHRC pressed the Finance Committee to tax high-net-worth individuals instead of PAYE earners ([The Star, 28 May 2026](https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/2026-05-28-activists-ask-state-to-mobilise-sh129-billion-from-rich-kenyans)). The Assembly passed the Bill `[verify: search summary]`. No wealth-tax amendment by a legislator was found.
- **Poll:** Business Daily reports an Ipsos survey for Earth4All (March to April 2024, 1,000 Kenyans) in which 70% strongly agree that the rich should pay a tax on their wealth ([Business Daily](https://www.businessdailyafrica.com/bd/economy/most-kenyans-support-taxes-on-the-rich-ipsos-4679508)). The pollster's own tables were not found, so it is a lead and not in `meta.json`.
