# Area profile — Montenegro

## 1. Scope
First pass (2026-11-04), national level, in Montenegrin/Serbian and English. Very little was found; this is a coverage note, not evidence of no support.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
No wealth tax exists. In 2012 the finance minister rejected union, NGO and party calls for progressive taxation (income). The 2022 "Europe Now" reform and the 2023-24 extra-profit levy are not wealth taxes. A 2018 report said initiatives for a luxury tax went unanswered.

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
Skupština (skupstina.me, zakoni.skupstina.me). The bills registry was not searched directly.

## 4. Parties
No party found with a wealth-tax position. Programmes of Levica, URA, Evropa sad, Demokrate, DPS, SDP not read.

## 5. Government below national level

## Round 2 (2026-11-04)
Montenegro still has one grade-C row (Kostić, 2012), and RFE shows he listed yachts, houses, boats, meadows and vineyards, so it may mean property taxation. Searches for Levica, Evropa sad, URA, Demokrate, SDP, unions and think tanks found nothing; the only Skupština bill seen is a Demokratska Crna Gora bill taxing officials' state housing benefits at 100% (not a wealth tax). The Skupština registry itself is still unsearched.
