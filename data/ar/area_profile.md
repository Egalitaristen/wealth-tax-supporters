# Area profile — Argentina

## Scope
Partial seed started 2026-10-27. National, provincial and local supporters are in scope. Spanish and English were searched; other languages and most local records remain unsearched.

## Wealth-tax proposals
The [official bill register](https://www.hcdn.gob.ar/diputados/mkirchner/proyecto.html?exp=4534-D-2020) lists 15 signatories to bill 4534-D-2020. The [parliamentary history](https://www4.hcdn.gob.ar/dependencias/dip/Debates_2021/Ley%2027605%20%20Aporte%20Solidario/Tr%C3%A1mite%20de%20la%20ley.pdf) identifies its enactment as law 27,605; the one-time levy applied to declared assets above ARS 200 million. [IPyPP](https://ipypp.org.ar/descargas/2024/Por%20un%20paquete%20fiscal%20que%20termine%20con%20el%20hambre.pdf) proposed a surcharge on the existing personal-assets tax in 2024.

## Geography and limitations
[Georef](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/georef/descarga-de-la-base-completa) provides the local-government register. The live v2.1 API supplied 24 provincial jurisdictions and 2,280 local governments, including communes and juntas. Its [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/georef/condiciones-de-uso-y-licencia) require attribution and indication of changes.

The map uses [IGN provincial outlines](https://www.ign.gob.ar/capas-sig/metadata/provincia.pdf) and the [IGN government-local polygon layer](https://www.ign.gob.ar/NuestrasActividades/InformacionGeoespacial/CapasSIG), whose [metadata](https://www.ign.gob.ar/capas-sig/metadata/municipio.pdf) permits free use with attribution. The layer was simplified and matched to Georef by code, with nine additional matches checked by both name and official location. It has 2,064 mapped polygons; 216 registered local units have no matched polygon, including all 165 units in Santiago del Estero. They appear as clearly labelled Georef location markers, not boundaries. Provincial outlines provide geographical context. The shapes are incomplete and some codes differ between datasets. A [2017 metadata record](https://www.ign.gob.ar/archivos/sig250/metadatos/municipio.pdf) identifies particular gaps, but does not establish current coverage.

A limited search found approved resolutions in Berazategui and Luján, and a Córdoba provincial legislative speech. This is not a systematic sweep; all local units remain pending.
