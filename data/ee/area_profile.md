## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Estonia has a land tax and, since 2025, a security tax and a car tax, but no net-wealth tax.
- 2023 budget talks: SDE chair Lauri Läänemets said "asset taxes, taxes on wealthy, rich people or businesses - there's no escaping it" (ERR, 2023-09-14); his concrete proposals were a progressive income tax and a tax on company assets. Eesti 200 favoured property taxes. Excluded, except the one statement (grade C).
- 2024-26: employers and the chamber of commerce floated an "ettevõtete varamaks" (company asset tax); SDE and Eesti 200 preferred a profit tax. A corporate tax: excluded.
- 2026-04: Kerly Randlane (TUUM Consulting) argued for wealth taxes in place of a progressive income tax (ERR): recorded, grade B.
- No Riigikogu bill on a personal net-wealth tax found. No poll on a wealth tax found (Norstat polls cover the car tax and a progressive income tax).
