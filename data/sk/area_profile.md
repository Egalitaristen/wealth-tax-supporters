## 2. Wealth-tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Slovakia has no net wealth tax; the property tax on buildings and land is among the lowest in the OECD [verify: search summary].
- The 2023-25 consolidation packages raised income tax (a 35% band), VAT, a bank levy and a financial transaction tax. None is a wealth tax.
- Smer's 2023 talk of taxing banks and the rich concerned banks and income (excluded).
- The one named supporter found is economist and former finance minister Brigita Schmögnerová, who in a 2025-09-26 Pravda commentary computes a 5% wealth tax on the richest Slovaks and calls for a Slovak Patriotic Millionaires (grade B).
- Slovak Smer MEPs Beňová and Roth Neveďalová, and Hajšel, voted for the EP wealth-tax amendments of 2023.
- No bill in the National Council, no party programme and no poll on a wealth tax was found. The finding itself is that support is thin.
