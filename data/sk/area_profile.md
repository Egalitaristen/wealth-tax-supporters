## 2. Wealth-tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Slovakia has no net wealth tax; the property tax on buildings and land is among the lowest in the OECD [verify: search summary].
- The 2023-25 consolidation packages raised income tax (a 35% band), VAT, a bank levy and a financial transaction tax. None is a wealth tax.
- Smer's 2023 talk of taxing banks and the rich concerned banks and income (excluded).
- Economist and former finance minister Brigita Schmögnerová who in a 2025-09-26 Pravda commentary computes a 5% wealth tax on the richest Slovaks and calls for a Slovak Patriotic Millionaires (grade B).
- Slovak MEPs Beňová, Roth Neveďalová and Hajšel voted for EP wealth-tax amendments in 2023. On [2 April 2025](https://howtheyvote.eu/votes/174622), Beňová, Roth Neveďalová, Ľuboš Blaha, Erik Kaliňák and Judita Laššáková voted for a rejected amendment proposing an EU minimum tax of 3% on wealth above €100 million.
- A [2025 KOZ SR poverty report](https://www.kozsr.sk/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Sprava-Chudoba-04.pdf), authored by Ján Košč, suggests considering an additional tax on net wealth above a high threshold; it is an option, not a detailed or formally adopted policy. Its footnote separately explains that Slovakia’s existing “millionaire tax” is an income tax.
- [Dobrá voľba’s 2020 programme](https://dobravolba.sk/images/Dokumenty/DOBRA_VOLBA_2020_VOLEBNY_PROGRAM.pdf) calls for to tax work less and wealth more; its intended base is unclear (grade C). The [official party register](https://rez.vs.minv.sk/PolitickeStrany/detail?id_spolok=224263) records its 2023 dissolution by merger.
- Political scientist Tomáš Imrich Profant, quoted in a [2020 tax report](https://www.pracujucachudoba.sk/storage/app/media/na_stiahnutie/myty-a-fakty-o-danovo-odvodovom-zatazeni-na-slovensku.pdf), connected a tax on assets to concentrated wealth; the tax base is unclear (grade C).
- In 2024, [Proforum, Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung and FEPS jointly campaigned](https://feps-europe.eu/event/social-and-environmental-justice-in-the-eu/) for the EU Tax the Rich citizens’ initiative in Slovakia. [Teologické fórum](https://www.teoforum.sk/?id=8&view_more=2427) urged readers to sign. Attendance at their panels is not evidence of an individual position.
- Political commentator Eduard Chmelár [appealed for support for the EU initiative](https://noveslovo.eu/glosy/podporte-zdanenie-extremnych-bohatych/) in February 2024.
- On 17 June 2025, [Klimatická koalícia coordinator Dana Mareková signed a letter to EU finance ministers](https://eeb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Own-Resources-Letter-June-25_final-2.pdf) supporting a progressive wealth tax. Sociologist Zuzana Fialová [endorsed the proposal in an interview](https://zurnal.pravda.sk/rozhovory/clanok/757846-koho-este-viac-zdanit-pracujucich-alebo-superbohacov/).
- No national wealth-tax bill or qualifying poll was found; this is a limited search, not proof that none exists.
