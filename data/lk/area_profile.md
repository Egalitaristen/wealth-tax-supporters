## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- A wealth tax was introduced in 1958 (Kaldor's advice) and abolished from the 1992/93 assessment year after the 1990 Tax Commission found it yielded little ([The Morning, 2 Sep 2022](https://www.themorning.lk/imf-staff-level-deal-comes-with-wealth-tax)).
- Oct 2013: Treasury officials weighed a "super-rich tax" for the 2014 budget; it was dropped under pressure ([Sunday Times, 27 Oct 2013](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/131027/business-times/uproar-over-super-rich-tax-plan-66732.html)).
- Feb 2016: Prime Minister Wickremesinghe says a tax on those who possess wealth was needed ([Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/160221/business-times/sri-lankas-rich-should-pay-wealth-tax-says-prime-minister-183471.html)).
- 1 Sep 2022: the IMF staff-level agreement includes a wealth tax. Mar 2023: the Extended Fund Facility lists a property tax and a gift and wealth tax for 2025 ([Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/online/news-online/Government-envisages-property-tax-gift-and-wealth-tax-in-2025/2-1141230)).
- Jun 2024: the President announces a "wealth tax" that is an imputed rental income tax on high-value property, due from April 2025 ([Daily FT](https://www.ft.lk/top-story/President-goes-to-Parliament-for-wealth-tax/26-763206)). It was not enacted as a tax on net wealth.
- 2025: the government tells the IMF it cannot meet the property, gift and inheritance tax dates; the NPP government (from Sept 2024, Parliament from Nov 2024) has not proposed a wealth tax ([HRW report, 14 Oct 2025](https://www.hrw.org/report/2025/10/14/tax-giveaways-struggling-schools/how-low-taxes-drove-sri-lankas-economic-crisis)).
- Apr 2026: Crisis Group tells the NPP to push for wealth taxes. Sep 2026: IPS backs wealth taxation for Budget 2027. The Budget 2027 speech was on 12 Nov 2026 and was not read.
