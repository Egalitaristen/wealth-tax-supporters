## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- The Wealth Tax Act of 1963 was repealed in 1999 because of valuation problems and double-taxation worries ([CPD/TBS, 13 May 2026](https://cpd.org.bd/direct-wealth-tax-needed-for-fairer-distribution-of-wealth/)).
- From FY2011-12 a surcharge on income tax payable replaces it. A 2015 budget restructured it to 10% to 30% by net-wealth slab ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/1233733)). Today net wealth above Tk 4 crore pays 10% to 35% of income tax payable.
- 13 June 2019: Finance Minister AHM Mustafa Kamal proposed that taxpayers with net wealth of Tk 500 million or more pay the higher of 0.1% of net wealth or 30% of income tax payable as surcharge ([bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/budget2019-20/finance-minister-proposes-0.1-surcharge-for-wealth-over-tk-500m)).
- March 2023: economists told the finance minister to introduce a wealth tax in the FY24 budget ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/602210)). May 2023: CPD's study of property taxation called a mandatory wealth tax inevitable ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/637438)).
- 2025: the interim government's Task Force on Economic Strategy Redesign recommended a wealth tax on personal and inherited assets ([Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/business/25zdga6xpk)).
- March to May 2026: the NBR chairman said the NBR was exploring a wealth tax; the NBR drafted 0.25% to 1% on declared net wealth above Tk 4 crore (0.25% from Tk 4 to 6 crore, 0.5% on the next Tk 5 crore, 0.75% on the next Tk 5 crore, 1% above Tk 16 crore), replacing the surcharge, to raise about Tk 5,000 crore ([CPD/TBS](https://cpd.org.bd/direct-wealth-tax-needed-for-fairer-distribution-of-wealth/)).
- 4 June 2026: the government was set to drop the plan after Prime Minister Tarique Rahman's directive ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/govt-may-drop-planned-wealth-tax-bike-and-rickshaw-ait-4190076)). Budget FY2026-27 was presented on 11 June 2026; whether the Finance Act contains any wealth tax was not confirmed (open).

## 5. Government below national level
Divisions (8) and districts (64) are administrative units; the elected local tier is the union, upazila and zila parishads and the city corporations. Local bodies cannot levy a wealth tax. The tier loaded is the district. **Local decision:** skip. No sample was taken; the debate is national (NBR, budget, think tanks).
