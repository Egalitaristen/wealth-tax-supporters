# Area profile — Ireland

## 1. Scope
- Area: Ireland; type: country.
- Depth: Seed.
- Levels of government in scope: national, then sampled local authorities. Ireland has 31 statutory local authorities; their official boundary IDs are loaded in `geo_units.csv`. [Tailte Éireann dataset](https://data.gov.ie/en_GB/dataset/local-authorities-national-statutory-boundaries-ungeneralised-2024/resource/51c985d6-d7e7-4d73-a293-4c5d116fccbc)
- Languages to search in: English and Irish (`cáin rachmais`).
- Date of first run: 2026-10-26.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- The Parliamentary Budget Office says a net wealth tax does not currently exist in Ireland. [PBO, 2024](https://data.oireachtas.ie/ie/oireachtas/parliamentaryBudgetOffice/2024/2024-09-04_an-overview-of-taxes-on-wealth-in-ireland_en.pdf)
- Labour's finance spokesperson called for a targeted net wealth tax in January 2026. [Labour Party](https://labour.ie/news/2026/01/19/net-wealth-tax-as-important-for-our-democracy-as-our-economy/)
- Social Democrats published a 0.5% tax above €1m and 1% above €2m in January 2024, and a 2% proposal above €5m in October 2026. [Social Democrats, 2024](https://www.socialdemocrats.ie/oxfam-report-highlights-need-for-renewed-debate-on-wealth-tax/) and [2026](https://www.socialdemocrats.ie/budget-2027-must-introduce-a-tax-on-super-wealth/)
- Oxfam Ireland campaigns for a net wealth tax on household wealth above €3m. [Oxfam Ireland](https://www.oxfamireland.org/blog/why-does-ireland-need-a-wealth-tax-on-the-super-rich)
- People Before Profit's 2024 policy sets progressive rates from 2% on net wealth above €4.7m to 5% above €1bn. [Economic Policy](https://www.pbp.ie/content/files/2024/10/Economic-Policy.pdf)
- The Irish Congress of Trade Unions recommends a modest net wealth tax on household net assets over €1m. [Budget 2025 submission](https://www.ictu.ie/sites/default/files/publications/2024/ICTU%20Budget%20Submission%202025.pdf)
- Economist Tom McDonnell proposed a 0.6% tax above €1m in a 2016 NERI publication. [An Irish Wealth Tax](https://www.nerinstitute.net/blog/irish-wealth-tax)
- The Green Party's 2022 manifesto proposes a wealth tax on individuals holding assets over €10m. [Towards 2030](https://www.greenparty.ie/sites/default/files/2022-03/GREEN_PARTY_TOWARDS_2030-WEB-VERSION.pdf)
- NERI's March 2026 working-paper summary assesses a recurrent net-household-wealth tax as viable and estimates that a threshold close to €3m could yield close to €800m annually. [NERI Working Paper 78](https://www.nerinstitute.net/research/net-wealth-tax-ireland-possibilities-and-pitfalls)
- Sinn Féin's June 2023 statement says that the party has long advocated for a wealth tax. [Sinn Féin](https://sinnfein.ie/news/government-policy-must-address-wealth-based-climate-emissions-senator-lynn-boylan/)
- R003 added individual proposals from People Before Profit TDs Richard Boyd Barrett and Paul Murphy, the authors of Oxfam Ireland's 2026 wealth-tax advocacy, TASC, David Cronin, Kathy Sheridan, Barra Roantree and Thomas Piketty. [PBP](https://www.pbp.ie/oxfam-report-highlights-extreme-wealth-inequality/), [TASC](https://www.tasc.ie/assets/files/pdf/20190522210243.pdf), [Oxfam Ireland report](https://www.oxfamireland.org/sites/default/files/2026-01/wealth-inequality-report-final-version.pdf)
- R004 added individual public statements from Sinn Féin Senators Lynn Boylan and Paul Gavan, PBP TD Mick Barry, and ICTU economist Paul Sweeney. It also added Senator Alice-Mary Higgins's formal 1% wealth-tax recommendation for household assets above €15m, and Sinn Féin TD Thomas Gould's statement of party support. [Sinn Féin](https://sinnfein.ie/news/government-policy-must-address-wealth-based-climate-emissions-senator-lynn-boylan/), [ICTU](https://www.ictu.ie/news/make-wealthy-pay-more-budget-congress-tells-dail-committee), [Oireachtas](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/debate/seanad/2022-12-13/13/)

Broughan; Oxfam Ireland CEO Jim Clarken; and academic commentator Julien Mercille. - R006 added economists Terrence McDonough and Stephen Kinsella, each with a personal public case for wealth taxation. [McDonough testimony](https://inquiries.oireachtas.ie/banking/hearings/terrence-mcdonough-international-eu-domestic-policy-contexts/), [Kinsella column](https://www.stephenkinsella.net/2015/09/29/everyone-should-pay-their-fair-share/)

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
- National legislature: Houses of the Oireachtas. Debate, question, bill and committee pages are published at [oireachtas.ie](https://www.oireachtas.ie/). R004-R005 located indexed official debate records, while direct automated retrieval still sometimes returns no usable text. The register now has Dáil and Seanad body rows and position rows for every listed politician, but their exact constituencies and current-seat status still await a verified official member-list import.

## 6. Search terms
- English: wealth tax; net wealth tax; wealth levy; billionaire tax; tax on super wealth; tax on extreme wealth.
- Irish: cáin rachmais.
- Do not count capital gains tax, inheritance tax, property tax or generic calls to tax the rich alone.
