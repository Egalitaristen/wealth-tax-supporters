# Area profile — Finland

## 2. Wealth-tax history and proposals

Finland stopped collecting its previous wealth tax in 2006 ([Statistics Finland](https://stat.fi/meta/kas/tulo_varal_vero_sv.html)). Pre-2010 acts do not qualify for this register.

- [LA 55/2012 vp](https://www.eduskunta.fi/asiat-ja-aanestykset/valtiopaivaasiat/asiakirjat/edktunnus/EDK_LA_55_2012_vp), dated 12 October 2012, proposes an annual 0.8% net-wealth tax above EUR 300,000. All 39 official signatories were checked; one deceased signer is excluded. These are historical individual acts, not evidence of today's party position.
- [TPA 25/2022 vp](https://www.eduskunta.fi/fi/asiat-ja-aanestykset/valtiopaivaasiat/asiakirjat/edktunnus/EDK-2022-AK-12526), dated 10 March 2022, calls for a 0.5% tax on wealth above EUR 1 million, excluding the main home. All 12 named signatories are recorded. Its base includes deposits, bonds and insurance savings.
- [SAK's 2019 proposal](https://www.sak.fi/blogit/varallisuuserojen-kimppuun-verolla-sak-esittaa-yleista-varallisuusveroa/) taxes a broad set of investment assets at 0.1% above EUR 100,000, with already taxed real property outside the base. [Jarkko Eloranta's 2025 defence-tax proposal](https://www.sak.fi/ajankohtaista/uutiset/sakn-jarkko-eloranta-suomeen-puolustusvero/) uses a similar base, at 0.1–0.2%.

## 6. Search terms and exclusions

Finnish: varallisuusvero, varallisuusveron palauttaminen, miljonäärivero, suurten omaisuuksien vero, puolustusvero. Swedish: förmögenhetsskatt, miljonärsskatt, miljardärsskatt. English: Finland wealth tax, millionaire tax, wealth levy.

Generic tax-the-rich language, inheritance and dividend taxes, financial transaction taxes and exit-tax-only proposals do not qualify. An event speaking slot does not prove support. Ordinary petition signers are excluded.
