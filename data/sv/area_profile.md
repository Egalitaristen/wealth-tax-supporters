## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **A tax on wealth (impuesto al patrimonio) existed until 1994**, when the ARENA government abolished it (per FUDECEN's Cabrera, Co Latino 10 Mar 2026, and the 2026 editorial; not checked against the 1994 decree `[verify]`).
- **2014: FMLN government's tax package** (unproductive property above $350,000, 1% minimum tax on company net assets, financial-transactions tax): real-estate and corporate taxes, an exclusion.
- **2018: ICEFI** gave the parties a proposal that included a wealth tax and a property tax. The FMLN's 2018 platform reportedly offered a wealth tax on large fortunes (lead, not recorded).
- **March 2025: Oxfam's Central America report** proposes 2.5% to 5% above US$5m; El Mundo reported it with El Salvador's figures.
- **February to March 2026: FUDECEN** (president Óscar Cabrera, with Diana Navarro Rivas) proposes an annual net-wealth tax of 2%, 3.5% and 5% above US$5m, US$30m and US$100m on about 110 people; Archbishop Escobar Alas asked on 24 March 2026 for the wealth tax to be restored.
- **Government line:** Hacienda says there are no plans for new taxes (2025); the Asamblea backed exemptions for large investments.
