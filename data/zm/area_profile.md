## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** none. Zambia has no net wealth tax; property is taxed only on transfer (5% property transfer tax) and on rental income, estate duty was abolished and there is no capital gains tax ([ActionAid/CTPD report, section 5.4](https://zambia.actionaid.org/sites/zambia/files/publications/FINAL%20-An%20Analysis%20of%20the%20Zambian%20Taxation%20System.pdf)).
- **Calls for one:** ActionAid Zambia's pre-budget statement of 25 September 2020 asked for a "categorical net-wealth tax" (`A0001`); its tax advisor Mtwalo Msoni called for a wealth tax and a wealth registry the day before (`A0002`); CUTS Lusaka's September 2020 Fair Tax Monitor report calls the absence of wealth taxes a major flaw (`A0003`). The ActionAid/CTPD report of June 2022 designs a net wealth tax but then recommends better enforcement of existing asset taxes "instead" (`X0001`). Climate Action Network Zambia is on the November 2025 pan-African letter asking the G20 presidency to back a 1% wealth tax on Africa's richest 1% (`A0004`).
- **Politics:** in September 2026, after the 13 August general election, Socialist Party president Fred M'membe published a policy manifesto with a new wealth tax on luxury real estate, offshore assets and unproductive domestic capital ([Lusaka Times](https://www.lusakatimes.com/2026/09/01/mmembe-lays-out-sweeping-reform-agenda-ahead-of-hichilemas-second-term-inauguration/), `A0005`). No bill or motion for a wealth tax was found; the UPND government (President Hichilema, second term) has not proposed one.
- **Poll:** none on a wealth tax. Afrobarometer found 73% say it is fair to tax the rich at higher rates (October 2024), a different question (`X0002`).
