## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Nothing is in force.** Ecuador has taxed net wealth twice, each time as a one-off payment, and both have run their course.
- **2016 earthquake law:** President Rafael Correa sent the Ley Orgánica de Solidaridad y de Corresponsabilidad Ciudadana to the Assembly on 22 April 2016 ([El Comercio](https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/proyecto-tributario-terremoto-llego-asamblea/)). Article 4 made individuals with net wealth of USD 1 million or more on 1 January 2016 pay 0.90% once ([Registro Oficial 759, 20 May 2016](https://files.eluniverso.com/archivos/2016/05/sro759_20160520_nell_azu.pdf)). The plenary approved the law with 94 votes ([Asamblea Nacional](https://www.asambleanacional.gob.ec/es/node/44232)). The minister expected USD 110 million from about 0.03% of the population ([Asamblea Nacional](https://www.asambleanacional.gob.ec/es/blogs/dr-vethowen-chica-arevalo/43248-ley-de-solidaridad-y)).
- **2021 temporary contributions:** President Guillermo Lasso's bill (submitted 28 October 2021) created a temporary contribution on net wealth: persons above USD 1 million at 1 January 2021 paid 1% to 1.5%, companies above USD 5 million 0.8% a year for two years. The Assembly committee changed the thresholds on 13 November 2021 ([Expreso](https://www.expreso.ec/actualidad/economia/reforma-tributaria-asamblea-propone-cambios-contribucion-temporal-115629.html)); the plenary did not vote within 30 days and the law took effect through publication on 29 November 2021 ([NMS law firm](https://nmslaw.com.ec/blog/2021/12/06/vigencia-ley-desarrollo-economico/)). The 5,227 persons concerned paid by 31 March 2022 ([Primicias](https://primicias.ec/noticias/economia/empresas-personas-contribuciones-tributarias-ecuador)). A student thesis says the tax was repealed in 2023 [verify].
- **Not wealth taxes (excluded):** the 2015 inheritance-tax bill (withdrawn), the 2019 one-off contribution on company income, the 2020 pandemic contributions on salaries and profits, and the 2024 contributions on salaries.
- **2019 to 2024 proposals from the left:** CONAIE and the Parlamento Popular handed the government a package in October 2019 that included a tax on the patrimonio of the large economic groups ([Primicias](https://primicias.ec/noticias/lo-ultimo/parlamento-popular-entrego-propuesta-economica)). In January 2024, against the VAT rise to 15%, the Revolución Ciudadana bloc proposed a permanent tax on those with wealth of USD 1 million or more ([Primicias](https://www.primicias.ec/noticias/economia/impuestos-banca-iva-asamblea)) and CONAIE listed a tax on wealth and large estates first among ten alternatives ([KCH](https://kchcomunicacion.com/?p=48454)). The Assembly raised VAT anyway [verify].
- **Opposition (not recorded):** business columnists argue that a wealth tax is harmful (Primicias opinion) [verify].
