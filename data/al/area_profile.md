# Area profile — Albania

## 1. Scope
- Area: Albania; Depth: Seed, national level only (2026-11-04). Languages: Albanian, English.
- Geography: 12 qarks and 61 bashkia, loaded with OCHA/HDX codes (CC BY-IGO). Not swept.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- No annual net wealth tax exists. A property tax reform (market-value basis, municipal) is in progress; it is out of definition.
- Lëvizja Bashkë (founded Dec 2022, one seat in the Kuvendi since 11 May 2025) campaigns on a progressive income-tax scale and "higher taxation of oligarchs' wealth" ([party site](https://levizjabashke.al/qendrime/vlora-duhet-ta-braktise-te-majten-e-oligarkeve-dhe-te-korruptuarve-per-te-majten-e-vertete-levizjen-bashke/); [Reporter.al](https://www.reporter.al/2025/04/02/levizja-bashke-e-majta-e-re-qe-po-krijon-pritshmeri-dhe-antagonizem/)).
- The ruling Socialist Party's line is "fair taxation" within a flat/progressive income frame; no wealth-tax statement was found.
