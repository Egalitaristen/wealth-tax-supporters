- Languages to search in: English (official)

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Historical origin:** An annual net wealth tax was introduced in British Guiana in 1962 under the PPP government of Dr. Cheddi Jagan, on the recommendation of Cambridge welfare economist Dr. Nicholas Kaldor. It was codified as the **Property Tax Act (Chapter 81:21)**. Despite violent political opposition at its inception ("Kaldor Budget" riots of 1962), the tax survived as a permanent pillar of Guyana's direct tax architecture.
- **Nature of the tax:** Under the Property Tax Act, "net property" is defined as the total value of all movable and immovable property (real estate, motor vehicles, shares, bank savings, receivables, jewelry) minus debts and liabilities as of December 31.
- **Budget 2013 reform:** Enacted through the *Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2013* (Bill No. 11/2013) presented by Finance Minister Dr. Ashni Singh. It shifted the valuation date from 1991 to 2011, raised the individual exemption threshold from GYD $7.5m to GYD $40m (taxing the remainder at 0.75%), and raised the corporate threshold to GYD $10m.
- **Budget 2019 reform:** Enacted through the *Property Tax (Amendment) Act 2018* (Bill No. 17/2018) presented by Coalition Finance Minister Winston Jordan. It reduced the rate on the first GYD $20m above threshold from 0.75% to 0.5% (with 0.75% on the remainder) and raised the corporate threshold to GYD $40m.
- **Budget 2024 individual abolition:** Enacted through the *Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2024* (Act No. 2 of 2024) presented by Dr. Ashni Singh. It inserted Section 6(y) into the Property Tax Act, fully exempting individuals from property tax and return filings starting from the year of income commencing January 1, 2025 (Year of Assessment 2026 onwards). Companies remain subject to the corporate net property tax.
- **Public defence and reaction:** When President Irfaan Ali announced the planned removal of individual property tax, senior chartered accountant and attorney Christopher Ram published a defence of the tax (*Stabroek News* and *ChrisRam.net*, 2025-12-31), arguing that "property tax – the last meaningful levy on accumulated wealth – is to be removed" and calling for reform rather than abolition, pointing out that wealth concentration will accelerate amid Guyana's offshore oil boom. Ram also commended a published letter by civic commentator Hemdutt Kumar (*Stabroek News*, 2025-12-20) describing the removal as "a victory for the wealthy and a betrayal of the poor".

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
- **National Assembly of Guyana:** Unicameral, 65 voting members (Twelfth Parliament 2020-present).
- **Official portal:** [parliament.gov.gy](https://parliament.gov.gy)
- **Debates (Hansard):** Published under Sittings & Hansards (searchable PDFs).
- **Bills & Acts:** [parliament.gov.gy/chamber-business/bills-status](https://parliament.gov.gy/chamber-business/bills-status) and Acts of Parliament repository.
- **Committees:** Standing and Select Committees records published on the parliamentary website.

## 4. Parties
- **PPP/C (People's Progressive Party/Civic):** Governing party (33 seats). Historically introduced the Property Tax in 1962 under Cheddi Jagan; returned to office in 2020 under Irfaan Ali and enacted the individual exemption via Act No. 2 of 2024.
- **APNU+AFC Coalition (A Partnership for National Unity / Alliance for Change):** Main parliamentary opposition (31 seats: 22 APNU, 9 AFC). Lowered rates in Budget 2019. Focused on cost of living, public sector wages, and NRF oil revenue governance.
- **Joinder List (LJP / ANUG / TNM):** 1 seat shared between Liberty and Justice Party, A New and United Guyana, and The New Movement.
- **Working People's Alliance (WPA):** Historic socialist party; leaders (Clive Thomas, David Hinds) advocate direct cash transfers from oil revenues (Buxton Proposal) rather than wealth taxes.
- **Change Guyana:** 2020 party led by accountant Nigel Hinds; explicitly campaigned to repeal the property tax as "oppressive".

## 5. Government below national level
- **Structure:** 10 Administrative Regions (Region 1 to 10), each with a Regional Democratic Council (RDC). Below the regions are 27 sub-districts comprising 10 Municipalities / Town Councils (Georgetown, Linden, New Amsterdam, Corriverton, Rose Hall, Anna Regina, Bartica, Lethem, Mabaruma, Mahdia) and about 70 Neighbourhood Democratic Councils (NDCs).
- **Taxation powers:** Sub-national bodies have no authority over national income, capital, or net-wealth taxation. Local authorities levy local rates and municipal property taxes on real estate assessment.
- **Register & Map:** 10 administrative regions and 27 sub-districts from OCHA / HDX COD-AB Guyana (valid 2021-12-16, CC BY-IGO, P-codes `GY01` to `GY10` and `GY0101` to `GY1002`). Map in `boundaries.json`.
- **Local decision:** Skip local unit sweep. Local councils have no mandate over net-wealth taxation.

## 6. Search terms
- English: `"wealth tax"`, `"net property tax"`, `"Property Tax Act"`, `"tax the rich"`, `"billionaire tax"`, `"Kaldor Budget"`, `"unexplained wealth"`
- Watch out: "property tax" in Guyana refers to two distinct taxes: (1) the national net-wealth tax administered by GRA under Cap. 81:21, which qualifies; and (2) municipal property rates levied by city councils on physical buildings/land, which are excluded under section 2.

## 9. Known networks
- **Ram & McRae (Chartered Accountants & Tax Advisers):** Produces the authoritative annual *Focus on Guyana's National Budget*.
- **Trade Unions:** FITUG (Federation of Independent Trade Unions of Guyana), GAWU (Guyana Agricultural and General Workers Union), GPSU (Guyana Public Service Union). Primarily campaign on income tax personal allowance thresholds and VAT exemptions.
