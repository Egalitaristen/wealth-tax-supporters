## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Lithuania has no net-wealth tax. Its "turto mokestis" is a real-estate tax: the 2021 law taxed high-value property, and a new Nekilnojamojo turto mokesčio įstatymas applies from 1 January 2026 (main home taxed only above a threshold) [verify: search summaries of pilotas.lt and realu.lt only]. Property-only taxes are outside the definition.
- Other debates: progressive income tax (Social Democrats, Peasants and Greens, 2010s), the 2021 "solidarity" tax on income above EUR 150,000 (Sysas, LRT 2021-10-11), an inheritance-tax "milijonierių mokestis" bill by Gintautas Paluckas [verify]. All excluded.
- No net-wealth-tax bill or party-programme line was found. No poll on a wealth tax found (an LRT 2023 poll covers only a universal property tax: 3% in favour).

## Correction (2026-11-06, R005)
- Navaitis was removed from the supporter list after his 2024 article clarified that he envisaged corporations paying or transferring assets. A corporate asset tax does not establish support for a tax on personal wealth under this register’s definition. Retained as lead D0008 for checking other statements.

- R005 checked official individual roll calls for two distinct EU wealth-tax amendments. Liudas Mažylis voted for the December 2023 solidarity-levy proposal; Virginijus Sinkevičius voted for the April 2025 3% proposal. Blinkevičiūtė and Mažylis voted against the latter, a specific later proposal; these votes do not by themselves prove opposition to every wealth-tax design. Official results: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:C_202500480 and https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:C_202506429 .

## Further follow-up (2026-11-06, R006)
- The European Parliament’s 23 October 2024 budget amendment 56 explicitly called for a European wealth tax. The official roll-call affirmative roster contained no Lithuanian MEPs; Blinkevičiūtė abstained. Amendment: https://amendeu.com/dossiers/2024-0176-BUD ; roll call: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:C/2025/04702 . This is a single-vote result, not a complete survey of that legislature.
- Lithuanian reports using “millionaires’ tax” often mean inheritance tax, while common “turto mokestis” results concern real estate; neither is sufficient evidence of a broad personal wealth tax. A press report mentions another possible February 2024 European Parliament wealth-tax vote, retained as D0009 until its official amendment and roster are established.
