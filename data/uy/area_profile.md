## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** the Impuesto al Patrimonio (IP), Título 14 of the Texto Ordenado 1996 (Decreto 338/996), an annual tax on net assets. The consolidated text is now the Texto Ordenado 2023 approved by Decreto 101/024 of 4 April 2024: [art. 4](https://impo.com.uy/bases/todgi2023/101-2024/4_T14) creates "un impuesto anual que recaerá sobre el patrimonio" of the taxpayers it lists. The article was rewritten by Ley 18.083 (2006) and amended by Ley 19.088 (2013), per the notes on the [1996 text](https://impo.com.uy/bases/todgi1996/338-1996/1_T14). Added to `data/resources.json` as a bill with state `in_force`.
- Not read: the full list of taxpayers, the non-taxable minimum and the rates (the pages read show none of them). The tax also reaches companies, so it is not only a tax on individuals' wealth `[verify]`.
- Proposals to change it (a levy on the richest, or an abolition) were not searched.
