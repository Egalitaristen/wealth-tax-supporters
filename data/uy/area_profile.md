## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** the Impuesto al Patrimonio, Título 14 of the Texto Ordenado ([art. 4](https://impo.com.uy/bases/todgi2023/101-2024/4_T14)). In 2013, Ley 19.088 (Carpeta 1168/2013, Distribuido 2007/2013) reinstated the tax on agricultural operations and established progressive farm net-wealth surcharges of 0.70% to 1.50%. Plenary debate and passage in the Senate ([Diario de Sesiones No. 213, 8 May 2013](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/diarios-de-sesion/3806/IMG)) featured major floor defenses by Senate miembro informante Rafael Michelini, Vertiente Artiguista leader Enrique Rubio, former Agriculture Minister Ernesto Agazzi, and economists Héctor Tajam and Alberto Couriel. In the Cámara de Representantes ([Diarios No. 3862 and 3863, 11-12 June 2013](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/diarios-de-sesion/3831/IMG)), floor defenses were delivered by majority miembro informante Gonzalo Mujica, tax spokesperson Alfredo Asti, and Deputies Alfredo Pardiñas, Andrés Lima, and Jorge Pozzi. The [FES brief](https://uruguay.fes.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Minuta_Uruguay.pdf) calculates that only 287 of roughly 2,300 holders of the 10-year foreign-income exemption are currently captured.
- **The 25% Wealth Tax Surcharge Bill (Carpeta 804/2022, Distribuido 1227/2022 & 79/2025):** On 18 October 2022, Senators Óscar Andrade, Charles Carrera, Daniel Caggiani, Daniel Olesker, Uruguay Russi, and Alejandro Sánchez introduced Carpeta 804/2022 (Asunto 156712) in the Senate ([Diario de Sesiones No. 33](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/diarios-de-sesion/senadores/49/33)). Article 1 establishes a 25% surcharge on the Impuesto al Patrimonio earmarked for the Fondo Nacional de Vivienda (FONAVI). On 11 March 2025, upon commencement of the 50th Legislature, Frente Amplio caucus coordinator Sen. Daniel Caggiani moved the desarchivo of Carpeta 804/2022, approved by the Senate plenary in a 27/29 show of hands ([Diario de Sesiones No. 5, p. 36](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/diarios-de-sesion/senadores/50/5)), and re-distributed as Distribuido 79/2025. Committee deliberations in the Comisión de Vivienda y Ordenamiento Territorial include hearings on 14 November 2023 ([VT 1872/0](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/versiones-taquigraficas/senadores/49/1872/0), Sen. Óscar Andrade defending the tax against Housing Minister Lozano) and 9 September 2025 ([VT 444/2025](https://parlamento.gub.uy/documentosyleyes/documentos/versiones-taquigraficas/senadores/50/444/0), FADU-UdelaR architects Lucía Anzalone and Andrés Cabrera endorsing the 25% surcharge).
- **PIT-CNT proposal & Consultative Commission:** At its May Day 2025 rally the union confederation proposed a 1% tax on the richest 1%; on 21 November 2025 the PIT-CNT presented the formal report drawn up by its consultative commission (including economists from IECON-UdelaR, Instituto Cuesta Duarte, and Cinve's Laboratorio Fiscal: Mauricio De Rosa, Joan Vilá, Gabriel Oyhantçabal, Andrea Vigorito, Juan Geymonat, Andrés Blanco, Alejandra Picco, Carlos Grau Pérez, and Gustavo Viñales Guillama). The proposal creates an annual surcharge on the Impuesto al Patrimonio de las Personas Físicas (IPPF) above USD 1 million to finance child poverty programs ([IPPF report](https://www.pitcnt.uy/sites/default/files/2025-11/IPPF.pdf)).
- **Frente Amplio Bases Programáticas 2025–2030:** Approved at the VIII Congreso Extraordinario 'Compañero Tabaré Vázquez' (December 2023), Section 3 (p. 29) explicitly mandates advancing tax reform by reducing consumption taxes and strengthening progressive taxation on net wealth, large capital, high fortunes, and offshore deposits ([Bases Programáticas](https://www.frenteamplio.uy/wp-content/uploads/Bases-Programaticas-2025-2030.pdf)). At its October 2026 Congress, the FA plenary formally welcomed the PIT-CNT proposal as a key contribution to tax justice, scheduling structured agreements for the 2027 tax review.
- **Party Backing & Public Debate:**
  - Senator Constanza Moreira (Casa Grande) explicitly defended taxing net wealth over income at a public forum in the Palacio Legislativo Anexo ([El Popular, 16 July 2025](https://elpopular.uy/constanza-moreira-sobre-el-1-es-momento-de-hacerlo/)).
  - Deputy Bruno Giometti (PCU / Cuesta Duarte) published a detailed defense of the wealth tax on the richest 1% ([El Popular, 28 Nov 2025](https://elpopular.uy/es-hora-de-terminar-con-la-pobreza-infantil-vamos-por-el-1/)).
  - IECON researcher Dr. Mauricio De Rosa advocated progressive net wealth tax rates from 0.5% to 2% ([El Popular, 13 July 2025](https://elpopular.uy/mauricio-de-rosa-el-impuesto-al-1-es-viable/)).
  - Senator Gustavo González (Partido Socialista) clarified that the proposal amends the existing Impuesto al Patrimonio structure rather than establishing a separate tax machinery ([Caras y Caretas, 1 Aug 2025](https://www.carasycaretas.com.uy/politica/grabar-los-ricos-el-senador-gustavo-gonzalez-se-puede-hacer-nuevo-impuesto-n85942)).
- **Minimum Wealth Tax Proposal:** FES Uruguay and the International Tax Observatory published Vicente Silva's policy brief (April 2026) proposing a 2% to 3% minimum effective wealth tax on individual net worth over USD 100 million ([FES Minuta](https://uruguay.fes.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Minuta_Uruguay.pdf)).

## 3. Legislature
- Parlamento (Senado, 30 senators plus the Vice President; Cámara de Representantes, 99 deputies): [parlamento.gub.uy](https://parlamento.gub.uy). Primary legislative portal tracks Carpeta 804/2022 (Asunto 156712) in the Senate Commission on Housing and Territorial Planning. Plenary desarchivo vote logged in Diario No. 5 (11 March 2025, 27/29 affirmative show of hands). Uruguayan parliamentary procedure votes almost exclusively by collective show of hands (*por la afirmativa*); named roll calls (*votación nominal*) are rare exceptions. Sponsoring senators are verified via the signed Distribuido documents.

## 4. Parties
- Frente Amplio (governing coalition), comprising Partido Comunista, Partido Socialista, MPP, Casa Grande, Vertiente Artiguista, and others. Governing platform (Bases Programáticas 2025-2030, p. 29) incorporates wealth and high-capital taxation. Opposition parties (Partido Nacional, Partido Colorado, Cabildo Abierto) oppose wealth tax rate increases.

## 5. Government below national level
- 19 intendencias (departmental governments) and 125 municipios. Intendente of Montevideo, Mario Bergara, stated the surcharge format was reasonable. Subnational levels do not levy independent net wealth taxes.
