## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** the Impuesto al Patrimonio, Título 14 of the Texto Ordenado ([art. 4](https://impo.com.uy/bases/todgi2023/101-2024/4_T14)). The [FES brief](https://uruguay.fes.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Minuta_Uruguay.pdf) says only 287 of the roughly 2,300 holders of the ten-year foreign-income exemption are within its scope.
- **PIT-CNT proposal.** At its May Day 2025 rally the union confederation proposed a 1% tax on the richest 1%; it put proposals to President Orsi in June 2025 ([El Economista](https://eleconomista.com.ar/internacional/impuesto-1-porciento-ricos-uruguay-n86545)). The Executive said no ([Montevideo Portal](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Pit-Cnt-presentara-propuesta-concreta-sobre-impuesto-a-ricos-con-aportes-de-academicos-uc930202)). Two economists of the Instituto de Economía (De Rosa and Vilá) wrote to the union on 2 July 2025 ([letter](https://mail.pitcnt.uy/sites/default/files/2025-07/Consideraciones%20de%20impuesto%20riqueza.pdf)). On 21 November 2025 the PIT-CNT presented a surcharge on the Impuesto al Patrimonio de las Personas Físicas above a USD 1 million minimum, to fund a state fund for child poverty ([Montevideo Portal](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Pit-Cnt-presento-propuesta-de-sobretasa-del-Impuesto-al-Patrimonio-en-que-consiste-uc944033)); [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/uruguay/el-pit-cnt-vuelve-insistir-el-impuesto-los-mas-ricos-y-sera-el-eje-central-del-1-mayo-n6272391) adds that a commission with the Instituto Cuesta Duarte and Cinve's Laboratorio Fiscal drew it up and that it was relaunched on 1 May 2026.
- **Frente Amplio split.** Senator Gustavo González (Partido Socialista) promotes a bill; the Partido Socialista backs it ([PS communiqué, 8 Aug 2025](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Partido-Socialista-defiende-impuesto-del-1-como-medida-viable-justa-y-necesaria--uc932621)); Senator Felipe Carballo agrees ([El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nacional/resistido-orsi-y-oddone-impuesto-al-1-mas-rico-suma-respaldo-cuatro-senadores-del-frente-amplio-n6008459)). The party moved the tax debate to its Comisión de Programa after Orsi and Oddone called the tax inconvenient ([El Observador, 16 Nov 2025](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nacional/diputados-del-fa-dicen-que-podrian-votar-las-dos-manos-el-impuesto-al-1-mas-rico-pero-que-si-el-debate-presupuesto-fue-feroz-no-imaginan-otro-impuesto-n6024736)).
- **A bill already filed.** On 11 March 2025 the Frente Amplio's Senate group reintroduced a bill (first filed in 2022, archived) for a 25% surcharge on the Impuesto al Patrimonio to fund the Fondo Nacional de Vivienda, signed by Óscar Andrade, Charles Carrera, Daniel Caggiani, Daniel Olesker, Uruguay Russi and Alejandro Sánchez ([El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/economia-y-empresas/senadores-del-fa-vuelven-proponer-sobretasa-25-el-impuesto-al-patrimonio-n5989552), [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/uruguay/revive-el-oficialismo-un-proyecto-sobretasa-al-impuesto-al-patrimonio-pese-que-yamandu-orsi-prometio-que-no-habria-subas-n6125127)). The bill text and number were not found on parlamento.gub.uy.
- **The commission's report.** The PIT-CNT's [proposal](https://www.pitcnt.uy/sites/default/files/2025-11/IPPF.pdf) is the report of a consultative commission; its first footnote names nine members, who are recorded as its authors. A bill by González for the 1% tax is mentioned by Ámbito (17 Aug 2025) but no filed bill was found.
- **Frente Amplio congress, 16 to 18 October 2026:** it approved a motion that "values" the proposal as "un aporte relevante al debate sobre justicia tributaria" and says it shares its orientation, with the debate projected to 2027 ([El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nacional/congreso-del-frente-amplio-respaldo-creacion-impuesto-al-1-mas-rico-y-llamo-alcanzar-acuerdos-2027-n6060850), [Prensa Latina](https://www.prensa-latina.cu/2026/10/19/fa-revive-debate-sobre-impuesto-a-mas-ricos-en-uruguay/)).
- **Opposition (not recorded):** Orsi, Economy Minister Oddone and Cabildo Abierto's Manini Ríos ([Montevideo Portal](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Noticias/Manini-Rios-sobre-propuesta-de-impuesto-a-los-ricos--En-principio-no-estamos-de-acuerdo--uc932602)); Blancos and Colorados `[verify: search summary only]`.
- **Poll:** a poll of 8 to 12 August 2025 by the Usina de Percepción Ciudadana reported 51% support for an extraordinary tax on the richest 1% `[verify: only a secondary report found, so it is not listed in meta.json]`.
