# Area profile — Malta

First pass 2026-11-04 (R001). Depth: Seed.

## 1. Scope
- Area: Malta, a country. Levels in scope: national; the 68 local councils are loaded in `geo_units.csv` (ISO 3166-2:MT codes) but not swept. Members of the European Parliament from Malta would be recorded here if found.
- Languages searched: English, Maltese.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- Malta has no net wealth, gift or inheritance tax. Finance Minister Clyde Caruana told Newsbook in September 2023 that Malta was not considering one ([Newsbook](https://newsbook.com.mt/en/malta-isnt-considering-wealth-tax-introduction-finance-minister-says/)). Labour and the Nationalist Party both court wealthy residents and foreign investors; no statement from either backing a wealth tax was found.
- Prime Minister Abela said in October 2024 that the 2025 budget would carry no new taxes ([Malta Independent](https://www.independent.com.mt/articles/2024-10-02/local-news/Upcoming-Budget-will-look-to-create-and-distribute-wealth-will-not-include-new-taxes-PM-Abela-6736264574)) [verify, from a search summary].
- A Greens/EFA study of September 2023 estimated about 43 million euro a year for Malta from a moderate progressive wealth tax; ADPD's Ralph Cassar welcomed it.

## 3. Legislatures
- House of Representatives (parliament.mt): no member backing a wealth tax was found; ADPD has no seat. Hansard and private members' motions were not searched in full (open lead D0003).

## 4. Parties
- ADPD – The Green Party (A0001): Budget 2027 paper (21 October 2026) asks for a fairer contribution from accumulated wealth; grade C. A Malta Independent opinion piece reports named calls for a wealth tax from ADPD, Moviment Graffitti and Momentum; it blocks scripts (lead D0001).
- Partit Laburista, Partit Nazzjonalista, Volt Malta, Imperium Europa: nothing found.

## 5. Government below national level
- 68 local councils (the lowest tier; elected, no tax powers). Official ISO 3166-2:MT codes. Not swept: yield is expected to be nil because councils cannot tax wealth.
