# Area profile — Denmark

## Scope
Seed pass, national supporters with regional and local discovery; 5 regions and 98 municipalities are registered, not exhaustively searched. Danish and English. Christiansø is not a municipality and is excluded from the local tier.

## Debate and sources
Denmark abolished its former wealth tax in 1997. [Oxfam Danmark](https://oxfam.dk/formueskat-qa/) describes the history. [B 70](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20211/beslutningsforslag/b70/index.htm), a progressive wealth-tax proposal, was rejected in 2022. Enhedslisten, SF and Socialdemokratiet proposed wealth taxes with different thresholds in 2026; see [DI comparison](https://www.danskindustri.dk/folketingsvalg/formueskat-i-danmark-hvad-betyder-det/fakta-om-formueskat/). FH and Oxfam published their own proposals in [January](https://fho.dk/blog/2026/01/15/fh-formueskat-skal-finansiere-skattelettelser-til-lavindkomstfamilier/) and [February 2026](https://oxfam.dk/pressemeddelelser/oxfam-danmark-er-positive-overfor-s-forslag-om-formueskat/).

## R009 Legislative records deep-dive (9 November 2026)
An exhaustive scan of Folketinget's open data API (`oda.ft.dk`) across all parliamentary sessions from 2010 to 2026 established that B 70 (2021-22) is the sole post-2010 parliamentary motion proposing an actual net wealth tax (*formueskat*). Primary documents for B 70 (proposal as introduced, Skatteudvalget committee report, 1st reading plenary minutes M100, 2nd reading roll call M121) were read in full from Folketingstidende PDFs, alongside the 2025 opening debate (M3) and 2026 debate (M57). All 10 affirmative votes on B 70 were mapped to ODA FT Afstemning 8849. Forespørgsel F 6 and Forslag til vedtagelse V 9 (2013-14) by Finn Sørensen (EL) were examined and confirmed as an income surtax (*millionærskat* on high earnings) which lapsed without a vote. Nine evidence rows (E00001, E00002, E00004, E00005, E00007, E00008, E00042, E00049, E00050) were updated from walled ft.dk pages to primary Folketingstidende PDFs, increasing machine-verified quotes to 41 of 51 with zero failures. Official documents M0009 through M0014 were tracked in `documents.csv`, and the country records guide was created at `skill/references/records/dk.md`.
