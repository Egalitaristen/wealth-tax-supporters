# Area profile — Denmark

## Scope
Seed pass, national supporters with regional and local discovery; 5 regions and 98 municipalities are registered, not exhaustively searched. Danish and English. Christiansø is not a municipality and is excluded from the local tier.

## Debate and sources
Denmark abolished its former wealth tax in 1997. [Oxfam Danmark](https://oxfam.dk/formueskat-qa/) describes the history. [B 70](https://www.ft.dk/samling/20211/beslutningsforslag/b70/index.htm), a progressive wealth-tax proposal, was rejected in 2022. Enhedslisten, SF and Socialdemokratiet proposed wealth taxes with different thresholds in 2026; see [DI comparison](https://www.danskindustri.dk/folketingsvalg/formueskat-i-danmark-hvad-betyder-det/fakta-om-formueskat/). FH and Oxfam published their own proposals in [January](https://fho.dk/blog/2026/01/15/fh-formueskat-skal-finansiere-skattelettelser-til-lavindkomstfamilier/) and [February 2026](https://oxfam.dk/pressemeddelelser/oxfam-danmark-er-positive-overfor-s-forslag-om-formueskat/).
