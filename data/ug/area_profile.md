# Area profile — Uganda

Seed pass of 2026-11-13 (run R001). Web-search summaries were only used to find pages; each quote was checked on the page itself (two on Wayback copies, because the live pages sit behind a bot check or refuse scripts). **The register is thin: five supporters (the URA Commissioner General with a formal act, Oxfam in Uganda, SEATINI Uganda with its executive director, and a Kampala feminist organisation).** A blank here means "not found on a readable page", not "no support".

## 1. Scope
- Area: Uganda; type: country
- Depth: Seed, national level only. No district sweep.
- Register: the country root, 4 regions and 135 districts from the OCHA COD-AB layers (valid 2020-08-24). Uganda has created more districts and cities since; they are not in the layer.
- Languages searched: English. Luganda and Swahili were not searched.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** none. Uganda taxes income, VAT, property rates and (on business assets) capital gains; no net wealth, estate or inheritance tax.
- **June 2020:** URA Commissioner General John Musinguzi told Parliament's National Economy Committee to "consider imposing a wealth tax" as a COVID-19 response ([PML Daily, 22 June 2020](https://pmldaily.com/news/2020/06/ura-wants-wealthy-people-to-pay-tax.html), read on the Wayback copy). A headline of the Uganda Radio Network says an inheritance tax was proposed as well (lead D0003). No bill followed.
- **2015 to 2023, the high-net-worth debate.** The URA created a high-net-worth individuals unit in 2015; Oxfam, SEATINI and others called for taxing the rich more (SEATINI, March 2017) and Oxfam held a National Tax Symposium on taxing the wealthy. Those texts are about compliance on income and property and are excluded (X0001 to X0003).
- **February 2022:** Oxfam, SEATINI, CSBAG, UWONET and DFI presented the Commitment to Reducing Inequality index for Uganda and named a "wealth tax on the richest" as an option (A0002, A0003, A0004).
- **November 2023:** Akina Mama wa Afrika published a pan-African feminist primer arguing for wealth taxes (A0005).
- **April 2026:** the 2026/27 tax package (fuel, cement, mobile money, a charge on gains from non-business assets) went through Parliament. The Daily Monitor editorial "Ensure the rich too pull their weight" (21 April 2026) is a lead (D0001). A February 2026 Afrobarometer release shows 77% think it fair to tax the rich at a higher rate; that is not a wealth tax question (X0005).
- **Bills and motions:** none found. The Finance Committee report on the Income Tax (Amendment) Bill 2026 was scanned and has no wealth tax wording (M0001).

## 3. Legislature
- Parliament of Uganda (one chamber, 529 members in the 12th Parliament, 2026 to 2031). Bills and committee reports are at [bills.parliament.ug](https://bills.parliament.ug/) (PDFs); Hansard is at cmis.parliament.go.ug and is not text searchable from here.
- Seats: 372 NRM, 68 independents, 49 NUP, 12 UPC, 9 FDC, 6 DP, 3 other parties and 10 army representatives, from the [Rio Times guide](https://www.riotimesonline.com/uganda-politics-key-players-2026/) citing the Electoral Commission's report. The Commission's own report was not found; the Daily Monitor gives the same NRM and opposition totals. No member is named and no supporter sits in Parliament, so no seat is lit.
