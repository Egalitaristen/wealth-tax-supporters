# Area profile — Namibia

Seed pass of 2026-11-13 (run R001). Web-search summaries were only used to find pages; each quote was checked on the page itself. **This register is nearly empty on purpose: after a real search, the only wealth tax statements that could be read on an open page are two grade C rows.** A blank here means "not found on a readable page", not "no support".

## 1. Scope
- Area: Namibia; type: country
- Depth: Seed, national level only. No regional or constituency sweep.
- Register: the country root, 14 regions and 107 constituencies (the local tier with an open map and official codes). The 2023 delimitation raised the number of constituencies to 121; the OCHA layer (valid 2020-01-09) still has 107. Local authorities (57 municipalities, towns and villages) are not loaded.
- Languages searched: English. Afrikaans and German (Republikein, Allgemeine Zeitung) were not searched.
- Map: OCHA / HDX COD-AB Namibia (CC BY-IGO 3.0), P-codes `NA01`-`NA14` and `NAxxyy`.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** none. Namibia has no net wealth tax, estate duty or capital gains tax ([PwC rate card](https://www.pwc.com/na/en/assets/pdf/namibia-tax-reference-and-rate-card-2025.pdf); [PwC summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-namibia/individual/other-taxes)).
- **2015-2016 "Solidarity Wealth Tax":** President Hage Geingob announced a tax on "the wealthy in high income brackets", ring-fenced for poverty programmes with a sunset clause ([New Era, 6 April 2016](https://neweralive.na/posts/sona2016-solidarity-tax-income-grant-explained)). It was a surcharge on income brackets, was never enacted, and is excluded (`X0001`).
- **2024 BIG Coalition submission:** per search summaries, the Basic Income Grant Coalition told the Finance Minister that "a relatively small wealth tax" plus less illicit financial flows could fund a grant. The only page found, [Namibian Sun, 17 September 2024](https://namibiansun.com/government/big-coalition-tax-the-fat-cats-to-help-the-poor2024-09-17), is behind a paywall and shows only its lede, so it is an open lead (`D0001`), not a row.
- **2025-2026:** the mid-year budget review introduced a 10% dividend withholding tax for individuals and NamRA plans a unit for wealthy individuals; both are income taxes and excluded (`X0002`). The 2026 budget (People, Productivity, Prudence) had no wealth tax; two commentators criticised that (`A0001`, `A0002`).
- **Bills and motions:** none found. Parliament's order papers show an Income Tax Amendment Bill (a tax court) and nothing on wealth.

## 3. Legislature
- National Assembly: 96 elected members plus up to 8 appointed by the President without a vote; National Council (42 members, two per region). Order papers and minutes are PDFs on [parliament.na](https://www.parliament.na) with no search page.
- Seats: the 96 elected seats by party come from the Electoral Commission of Namibia's declaration of 3 December 2024 ([ECN statement](https://d4f7y6nbupj5z.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/20241203-Speech_Results-Declaration-speech-Namibia-3Dec2024ETN3.pdf)). No member is named and no supporter sits in the Assembly, so no seat is lit.

## 4. Parties
- SWAPO (51), IPC (20), AR (6), LPM (5), PDM (5), UDF (2) and seven one-seat parties. Manifestos of 2024 (eaglefm.com.na and nbcnews.na) were not read in full; search summaries found no wealth tax in them (AR: employer tax holiday; NEFF: nationalisation; RDP: inheritance tax).
