# Area profile — Senegal

## 1. Scope
- Area: Senegal (country). Depth: Seed (R001, 2026-11-13).
- Levels in scope: national. Geography register: 14 regions, 46 departments, 125 arrondissements (OCHA COD-AB, CC BY-IGO). The elected communes are not loaded (no open map with official codes found).
- Languages searched: French and English. Wolof, and French-language press by site search (Seneweb, Le Soleil, APS), were not searched in depth.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- No tax on net wealth exists; guides to Senegalese taxation list only registration duties and property tax (contribution foncière).
- 2025 reforms (tax code amendment law 17/2025, adopted 17-18 September 2025; PRES recovery plan of 1 August 2025) added taxes on money transfers, gambling, tobacco and digital income, and a revised income tax scale; none targets wealth.
- The 2026 supplementary finance bill was rejected by the Assemblée nationale on 3 November 2026 (109 for the previous question, 20 against, 3 abstentions) and Pastef has rejected the 2027 budget in committee; neither text concerns a wealth tax.
- No bill, amendment, party programme or union motion for a wealth tax was found. A [verify] gap: the Pastef programme text itself was not read.

## 3. Legislature
- Assemblée nationale (165 seats, single chamber): https://www.assemblee.sn/ (Angular site; the seat table is rendered by script, read in a browser).

## 4. Parties
- Pastef 130, Takku Wallu 16, Diam Ak Njarin 7, Samm Sa Kaddu 3, Andu Nawle 2, seven lists with one seat each (assemblee.sn, 2026-11-13).

## 5-9. Civil society and others
- Forum Civil with Tax Justice Network Africa works on tobacco, mining and mobile-money tax; no wealth tax position found.
- Oxfam's July 2025 Africa report urges taxing the richest 1% across the continent; it is regional and not recorded.
- Afrobarometer 2021: 79% of Senegalese say a higher tax rate for the rich is fair (about rates, not a wealth tax; not used as a poll).

## 10. What was found and the plan
- One supporter: Aminata Touré (grade B), who at the Vatican/ICRICT event of 13 February 2025 hoped the richest would pay "higher taxes on wealth" and that a minimum tax on the super-rich could be an early protocol of the UN tax convention.
- Support inside Senegal looks thin or unrecorded: broad searches found no wealth tax campaign. This is a finding about the search, not proof of absence.
