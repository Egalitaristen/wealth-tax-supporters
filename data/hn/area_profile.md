# Area profile — Honduras

Seed pass of 2026-11-13 (run R001). Thin result: the 2023 to 2025 tax debate was fought over the Ley de Justicia Tributaria (LJT), which is not a wealth tax; explicit calls for taxing large fortunes are few.

## 1. Scope
- Area: Honduras; type: country. Depth: Seed, national level only. Languages searched: Spanish and English.
- Register: 18 departamentos and 298 municipios from [OCHA / HDX COD-AB Honduras](https://data.humdata.org/dataset/cod-ab-hnd) (valid 2016-10-05, CC BY-IGO), P-codes `HN01…` and `HN0101…`. Municipio names come without accents.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **No tax on personal net wealth exists.** Honduras has the Impuesto al Activo Neto (1% of the net assets of companies with assets above L3 million, creditable against income tax; created by Decreto 51-2003) ([SEFIN consolidated text](http://www.sefin.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/TEXTO_CONSOLIDADO_IMPUESTO_AL_ACTIVO_NETO_25JUNIO2018.pdf), found by search, not opened `[verify]`). It is a corporate minimum tax and is not counted here.
- **Ley de Justicia Tributaria (March 2023, Xiomara Castro government; SAR minister Marlon Ochoa)**: cuts exemptions and special regimes, restores global income tax and raises the large-company rate. The consolidated bill text ([SAR](https://www.sar.gob.hn/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Ley-de-Justicia-Tributaria-Consolidada.pdf)) contains no tax on wealth, inheritance or fortunes. Not passed as of the latest coverage found (Congress refused it; Libre's 2025 candidate Rixi Moncada promised a harder version). Recorded as an exclusion, with the 85 economists' letter of July 2024.
- **Statements for taxing fortunes:** Libre deputy Ramón Barrios, February 2023; Oxfam's Central America report "Más recursos para más derechos" (March 2025, presented in Tegucigalpa on 15 May 2025 with Oxfam Honduras, Hugo Noé Pino and SAR minister Mariana Ríos): progressive tax of 2.5% to 5% on net wealth above US$5m.
- **Opposition:** COHEP and the banks fought the LJT.
