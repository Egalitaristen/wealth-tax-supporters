# Area profile — Philippines

First pass, 28 October 2026. **Incomplete seed:** national research begun; regional and local sampling remains outstanding. English and Filipino are the intended search languages. This is not a complete list.

## Debate and sources

- [HB 10253](https://docs.congress.hrep.online/legisdocs/basic_18/HB10253.pdf), filed in 2021, proposed a tax on net assets above PHP 1 billion; its six named authors have been processed.
- The House published [HB 8123](https://docs.congress.hrep.online/legisdocs/basic_20/HB08123.pdf), introduced by three Makabayan party-list representatives, and [HB 9274](https://docs.congress.hrep.online/legisdocs/basic_20/HB09274.pdf), introduced by Leila de Lima, in 2026. Both propose annual taxes on wealth.
- [IBON Foundation](https://www.ibon.org/tl/tax-the-super-rich/) and [Freedom from Debt Coalition](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/economy/785603/group-pushes-for-alternative-stimulus-program-to-address-covid-19-crisis/story/) have promoted wealth taxes. [Oxfam Pilipinas](https://oxfam.org.ph/new-study-richest-1-bag-nearly-twice-as-the-rest-of-the-world-put-together-during-pandemic/) calls for net wealth taxes among other measures.
- The [UP CIDS monograph](https://cids.up.edu.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Inequality-Tax-Justice-and-the-Philippine-Wealth-Tax-Campaign.pdf) provides leads on the campaign, including unions and economists.

## Geography and limitations

Its [third-quarter 2026 workbook](https://psa.gov.ph/system/files/scd/PSGC-3Q-2026-Publication-Datafile.xlsx) could not be downloaded through the restricted shell. The register now contains 18 regions, 82 provinces, 149 cities and 1,493 municipalities from the [July 2026 PSGC mirror](https://github.com/bendlikeabamboo/barangay/blob/main/barangay/data/barangay_flat.json), cross-checked against PSA third-quarter totals and changes. A synthetic Isabela City parent was excluded; its official city is attached to Region IX. Eight special geographic area municipalities are attached to BARMM. [geoBoundaries ADM3](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/) offers 2020 boundaries under CC BY 3.0 IGO, but their codes and correspondence with the current list have not been verified. HDX was blocked. Local searches are preliminary; no completed local records sweep is claimed.
