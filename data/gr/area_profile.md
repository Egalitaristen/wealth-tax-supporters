# Greece — preliminary research, R001

## Scope and limitations

This is an initial national discovery, not a completed seed pass. Greek and English were searched. Parliament, other parties, civil society, unions, economists and subnational government have not been systematically surveyed. The official local geography is loaded; no boundary map has been loaded. Do not interpret missing entries as opposition.

## Evidence

- In September 2026 Alexis Tsipras presented the Greek Left Alliance programme including a 1% annual contribution on net wealth of the richest 1%; [Capital.gr](https://www.capital.gr/politiki/4014243/deite-live-tin-parousiasi-tou-oikonomikou-programmatos-tis-elas/) reproduces his remarks, and [CNN Greece](https://www.cnn.gr/politiki/story/550594/patriotiki-eisfora-1-ston-ployto-kai-ethniko-ta) reports the party's detailed proposal.
- [in.gr](https://www.in.gr/2026/09/06/economy/forologisi-tou-poly-megalou-ploutou-ena-rizospastiko-metro-pou-to-ypostirizoun-kai-megalotrapezites/) quotes banker Michalis Sallas proposing 0.6–0.7% on net wealth above €1 million, excluding the primary home and deducting debts.
- A general net wealth tax was absent in Greece in the 2025 comparison by [Tax Foundation](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/eu/wealth-taxes-europe/). Property taxes alone do not meet this register's definition.

## Search terms and cautions

Greek: φόρος πλούτου, φόρος μεγάλης περιουσίας, καθαρή περιουσία, πατριωτική εισφορά, φόρος Ζουκμάν. English: Greece wealth tax, net wealth tax, billionaire tax. Distinguish taxes on real estate alone from a tax on broad net assets.

## Continuation — R003

- [Giorgos Pappas](https://www.iefimerida.gr/politiki/giorgos-pappas-katagrapsoyme-thyrides) personally described the ELAS proposal as a tax on net wealth. His subsequent [clarification](https://www.myelas.gr/el/nea/dilosi-tou-anapliroti-tomearchi-oikonomikon-giorgou-pappa) says the proposed assessment would not be based on jewellery. He described the contribution as temporary; other programme reporting describes it as permanent.
- [Gavriil Sakellaridis](https://neaaristera.gr/drasthriothtes/oi-erotapantiseis-tis-synentefxis-typou-tou-epikefalis-tis-neas-aristeras-gavriil-sakellaridi-sto-plaisio-tis-90is-deth/) endorsed taxation of large fortunes while reserving judgment on details of ELAS's particular scheme. [Gregory Papanikos](https://www.atiner.gr/gtp/Papanikos(2015)-Taxing-Wealth.pdf) proposed a comprehensive flat wealth tax in 2015, largely replacing other taxes; the paper describes a broad asset base without clearly specifying debt deductions.
- In the [3 December 2013 parliamentary debate](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/a08fc2dd-61a9-4a83-b09a-09f4c564609d/es20131203.pdf), Euclid Tsakalotos advocated taxing movable and immovable wealth with a substantial exemption. The official current [constituency list](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/vouleftes/ana-eklogiki-perifereia/?Constituency=9b5ba31c-ac2b-4cff-ac69-aa8d00bc2e78) lists him as an independent MP. The original 2013 party platform remains an open lead; a wealth register or real-estate tax alone does not establish a net-wealth tax.
- The new quotations were checked against their opened sources; the automated checker confirmed three and could not fetch the parliamentary PDF, whose text was manually inspected in the web reader. Searches for trade-union endorsements did not establish a qualifying position; that layer requires more work. Former constituency boundaries do not necessarily match current regional units, so Sakellaridis remains at the national root; Pappas is a party official rather than a known legislator.

## Continuation — R004

- The [3 December 2013 parliamentary record](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/a08fc2dd-61a9-4a83-b09a-09f4c564609d/es20131203.pdf) records SYRIZA's budget rapporteur advocating taxation of movable and immovable wealth above a substantial exemption. In [the 19 December debate](https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/a08fc2dd-61a9-4a83-b09a-09f4c564609d/es20131219ap.pdf), MP Eugenia Vamvaka described a progressive tax on total wealth and assets used for accumulation. Both are recorded as historical, grade B positions. Contemporary accounts differ on whether the 2013 party proposal was a general wealth tax or an immovable-property tax accompanied by a wider register; the original policy text and the present party position remain open for verification.
- **Correction to R003:** Gregory Papanikos has been moved from actors to exclusions pending stronger evidence: his paper explicitly defines its base as assets and does not establish that liabilities are deductible. His proposed broad assets tax is therefore not safely classifiable as the net-wealth tax required by this register. This is an evidence limitation, not a claim that he opposed wealth taxation.
- Anna Diamantopoulou's [article](https://www.diamantopoulou.gr/article/arthro-annas-diamantopoulou-sto-bhma-koinonikes-anisotites-kai-politikes-prokliseis/) supports coordinated wealth taxation and discusses Greek net-wealth proposals, but the opened page displays no date; she remains an open lead until publication can be dated.
- The two parliamentary quotations were verified in the web reader because the automated checker cannot retrieve the official PDFs from this environment. The initial union and civil-society searches still have not yielded a clearly attributable qualifying position.

## Continuation — R005

- The [Tax Extreme Wealth signatory list](https://taxextremewealth.com/signatures) identifies three Greek signers. A [5 September 2023 archived copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20230905121032/https://taxextremewealth.com/signatures) already names Nikos Papandreou and Dimitris Papadimoulis; an archived 28 May 2024 copy also names Petros Kokkalis. Dionysis Temponeras, Theoni Koufonikolakou, Nikos Nyfoudis and Kostas Karpouchtsis each discussed the specified ELAS contribution individually; Nyfoudis wrote a signed op-ed specifying the 1% net-wealth base. These are not inferred from party membership.
- A [December 2025 local newspaper](https://amarysia.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/7997_PENTELI_251225_.pdf) attributes support for the French Zucman proposal to Anna Diamantopoulou and identifies her 3 November 2025 article. That article's current online text contains references to later material; her entry is consequently based on the dated contemporary report, at grade B, pending an unmodified original.
