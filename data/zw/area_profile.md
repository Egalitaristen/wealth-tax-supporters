## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force, but not a net wealth tax:** the Finance Act 2023 (No. 13 of 2023), in force from 1 January 2024, charges an annual "wealth tax" on residential dwellings. The 2024 budget (30 November 2023) proposed 1% on homes worth US$100,000 or more; the Act applies it above US$250,000, with owners over 70 exempt ([Afriwise overview](https://www.afriwise.com/blog/the-new-wealth-tax-in-zimbabwe-an-overview)). It taxes one asset class, so it is excluded (X0001). Collection was suspended and, per the deputy finance minister, had not begun by September 2025 ([NewsDay, 16 September 2025](https://www.newsday.co.zw/local-news/article/200046087/govt-yet-to-collect-wealth-tax)).
- **November 2021 to January 2022:** the Fight Inequality Alliance Zimbabwe (about 18 groups) asked for a wealth tax in its 2022 budget input and at a press briefing in Harare (A0001, A0002). Oxfam's Southern Africa report priced a 5% tax on wealth over US$1 billion at US$65 million a year (A0005).
- **December 2022:** the chairperson of the Budget and Finance Committee told the National Assembly that a wealth tax would raise revenue (A0006); the alliance repeated its call after the budget (A0001, A0003).
- **October 2025:** the alliance asked for a wealth tax before the 2026 budget consultations (A0001, A0004).
- **Bills and motions:** none for a net wealth tax found. The property tax was challenged in court by an opposition MP (lead D0006).
