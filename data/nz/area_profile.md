# New Zealand — seed pass, 25 October 2026

## 1. Scope
National supporters since 2010, with a sampled search for local support. This is a seed, not an exhaustive register. Every territorial authority is mapped even where its records have not been searched. No individual is included merely for belonging to a party or coalition. The site suggestions queue was read: zero new suggestions.

## 2. Proposals and history
The [Greens' 21 June 2026 announcement](https://www.greens.org.nz/greens_commit_to_tax_the_super_rich_and_large_corporates_fairly_cut_income_tax_for_96) proposes 2.5% on net assets above NZ$10m, excluding family homes. This differs from its 2023 NZ$2m individual threshold. [Te Pāti Māori's 2026 manifesto](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=11dbbk3BTNigHUb4xANPnTKxb2TC9dAgg) proposes 1.5% above NZ$2m, 2% above NZ$5m and 2.5% above NZ$10m. Its [2023 policy](https://img.scoop.co.nz/media/pdfs/2307/Te_Pti_Mori_TAX_POLICY.pdf) used different rates. [Alliance's September 2026 policy](https://allianceparty.nz/alliance-tax-plan-first-20000-income-tax-free/) proposes an annual 2.5% above NZ$2m per individual.

## 3. Legislature
[Hansard](https://hansard.parliament.nz/) and party statements were searched for wealth-tax support and former ministers' positions. No qualifying roll-call list was harvested in this seed. Do not infer support from an income-tax vote. Grant Robertson's valedictory language about taxing assets allows several designs; obtain a direct net-wealth endorsement or signed design decision before adding it. David Parker's later interviews and the Treasury's [2023 tax advice](https://www.treasury.govt.nz/sites/default/files/2023-07/b23-tax-4791453.pdf) are high-priority leads.

## 4. Parties
Greens, Te Pāti Māori and Alliance have qualifying policies. The [PHCC 2026 party survey](https://www.phcc.org.nz/briefing/election-2026-will-parties-tax-policies-support-public-health-and-equity) distinguishes Labour's capital-gains policy and Opportunity's land tax; these alone do not meet this database's definition. A politician's own statement is required separately from a party policy. List MPs can remain at the country root where a finer public base was not verified; James Shaw is a former list MP, not a missing constituency.
