# Area profile — Germany

First written 2026-10-20 (run R001). Lines marked *[verify]* come from a search summary or a secondary page and were not checked against the primary document.

## 1. Scope
- Area: Germany (Bundesrepublik Deutschland); type: country, federal.
- Depth: **Seed** (SKILL.md 6.1). National level in every layer, the 16 Länder as a register, one Landtag sampled (Berlin). No municipalities loaded.
- Levels of government in scope: national (Bundestag, Bundesrat, Bundesregierung), supranational (German MEPs, not yet searched), regional (16 Länder; Landtage), local left out: municipalities cannot levy a wealth tax and were not sampled.
- Languages to search in: German (main), English.
- Date of first run: 2026-10-20.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- The Vermögensteuer is **suspended, not repealed**. It has not been levied since 1997 after the Federal Constitutional Court's 1995 ruling on unequal valuation of property; the Kohl government did not pass a new valuation law ([Bundestag motion 21/4029, section I](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/040/2104029.pdf): "die Vermögensteuer 1997 ausgesetzt"; [Leipziger Zeitung](https://www.l-iz.de/politik/engagement/2025/07/tax-the-rich-startschuss-online-bundestagspetition-wiedereinfuhrung-vermogensteuer-628360)). The yield would go to the Länder (motion 21/4029).
- Bundestag record since 2010 (all read in the Drucksache, `harvest.csv` H0001–H0008):
  - 17th term: Left motion 17/2944 (2010-09-14, 5% above EUR 1m); Left motion 17/8792 (2012-02-29); Left motion 17/10778 (2012-09-25, EU-wide levy); **Greens bill 17/10770 (2012-09-25), a one-off levy of 15% of taxable wealth paid in ten instalments** (roll call 2013-06-27: 66 yes, 318 no, 195 abstentions).
  - 18th term (2013–2017): two searches found no Bundestag motion on the wealth tax (not exhaustive; DIP not searched).
  - 19th term: Left motions 19/94 (2017-11-22, 5% above EUR 1m) and 19/28908 (2021-04-22, one-off levy of at least EUR 300bn on the richest 0.7%).
  - 20th term: Left motion 20/4307 (2022-11-08, one-off levy), rejected on 2022-11-10 in a roll call, 36 for and 649 against ([Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2022/kw45-de-vermoegensabgabe-920034)); the 36 yes voters are in `harvest.csv` H0011. Left motion 20/11630 (2024-06-04): a global minimum tax on billionaires, internationally coordinated, no rate set (11 named sponsors).
  - 21st term: **Greens motion 21/8267 "500-Millionen-Steuer" (2026-09-29)**: an annual tax on net wealth above EUR 500m, 2% rising linearly to 3% at EUR 1bn and 4% from EUR 10bn, income tax paid credited, about EUR 20bn a year; 15 named sponsors plus the group chairs ([Drucksache](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/082/2108267.pdf)). Left motion 21/4029 (2026-02-06): 1% from EUR 1m rising to 5% at EUR 50m and 12% above EUR 1bn. First reading 2026-03-06 ([Bundestag](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2026/kw10-de-vermoegensteuer-1150522)); the Finance Committee recommended rejection on 2026-10-07, with CDU/CSU, AfD, SPD and Greens voting against the Left ([Drucksache 21/8481](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/084/2108481.pdf)). The plenary vote is not yet recorded.
- Debates and hearings read: 2010-10-07 (first reading of 17/2944), 2012-09-27 (Greens bill and Left motion; Trittin and Gabriel for a levy or the tax), 2021-05-06 (19/28908; SPD's Kiziltepe: "Wir als SPD wollen die Vermögensteuer wieder einführen"), 2022-11-10, 2026-03-06; the Finance Committee hearing of 2021-04-19 on the FDP bill to repeal the Vermögensteuergesetz (three of eight experts for the tax, H0012).
- Länder governments: in 2011–2014 ten Länder finance ministries worked on a draft wealth tax law (1%, private and business assets, allowances) that ended without a result because of disagreement on business assets ([Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Drucksache 6/3512](https://kleineanfragen.de/mecklenburg-vorpommern/6/3512-wiedereinfuehrung-der-vermoegensteuer.pdf)); in April 2012 NRW, Rhineland-Palatinate, Baden-Württemberg and Hamburg announced a Bundesrat initiative, and NRW's finance minister Walter-Borjans defended the draft ([NRW press release, 2012-09-13](https://www.land.nrw/pressemitteilung/lindner-erweckt-mit-falschen-zitaten-falschen-eindruck-finanzminister-norbert)).
- Roll calls: 2013-06-27 on the Greens' bill 17/10770, 66 yes (65 Greens, 1 SPD), 318 no, 195 abstentions, the SPD and Left abstaining ([name list](https://www.bundestag.de/resource/blob/194544/20130627_4_vermoegensabgabe.pdf)); 2022-11-10 on 20/4307, 36 yes (all Left or SSW).
- Landtage (Left group motions asking for a Bundesrat initiative, read at the Landtag's own site): Saxony-Anhalt 7/845 (2017-01-19), Hesse 19/4497 (2017-02-07), Saxony 6/9213 (2017-04-05) and 7/192 (2019-10-11), Berlin 19/1952 (2024-10-08). Read but before the window (2010-01-01): Hamburg 19/2095 (2009-01-28, eight named sponsors, recorded as exclusions), Hesse 18/399 (2009-05-05), Lower Saxony 16/501 (2008-09-30), Saarland (2009-12-09, Left and SPD). Hesse 18/1070 could not be fetched.
- Government: the CDU/CSU–SPD coalition (Chancellor Merz) has no wealth tax in its agreement; a Marvi (SPD) speech in the first reading says the SPD programme provides for it but "nicht diese Koalition". [ZDF](https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/deutschland/klingbeil-steuererhoehung-koalition-spd-100.html) reports the coalition agreement has no tax increases.
- Party positions found: Left (annual tax, plus one-off levies in 2021 and 2022), Greens (2016 congress resolution; 2021 programme 1% above EUR 2m; 2025 programme a global billionaire tax), SPD (2019 congress 1% above EUR 2m; 2025 programme "für sehr hohe Vermögen revitalisieren"). BSW position not read (site blocked). CDU/CSU, FDP and AfD oppose; not recorded.
- Civil society: alliance "Vermögen besteuern jetzt" (statement 2024-10-04, 22 organisations, later 27 plus 12 supporters); earlier alliance "Umfairteilen" (2012–2013); Attac's "Tax the Rich" Bundestag petition (2025); taxmenow and earlier millionaires' appeals (2011, 2021, 2022).
- Polls: Forsa for Stern, July 2024, 62% in favour of a levy on wealth above EUR 1m (34% against) — in `meta.json`.

## 6. Search terms
| Term | Note |
|---|---|
| Vermögensteuer, Vermögenssteuer | Both spellings occur; the official law is *Vermögensteuergesetz* |
| Vermögensabgabe | One-off levy (Lastenausgleich model, Art. 106(1) no. 5 Basic Law) |
| Millionärsteuer, Millionärssteuer | Left's name for the 5% above EUR 1m design |
| Milliardärssteuer, Milliardärsteuer, Zucman-Steuer, Reichensteuer | Billionaire minimum tax (2% above EUR 100m); *Reichensteuer* is also the top **income** tax rate — read the context |
| Mindeststeuer für Superreiche, Überreichtum | Context terms |
| Erbschaftsteuer, Schenkungsteuer | **Excluded** on their own |
| Vermögensregister | A register is not a tax |
| Grundsteuer, Reichensteuer (Einkommensteuer), Soli | **Excluded** |
- Ambiguous: *Vermögensbesteuerung* can include inheritance tax; *Vermögensabgabe* is sometimes used for a levy on property alone (Lastenausgleich 1952).

## Parliamentary debate and referral follow-up (R053, 14 November 2026)

The official [Berlin verbatim debate](https://www.parlament-berlin.de/ados/19/IIIPlen/protokoll/plen19-069-pp.pdf), pp. 6934–6941, identifies direct wealth-tax arguments by André Schulze and Steffen Zillich. It also replaces a third-party transcript used for Lars Rauchfuß and the Berlin SPD group; their 10-million-euro threshold supports grade A. The groups’ stances on motion 19/1952 remain distinct from support for a wealth tax generally.

The [Bundestag debate](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btp/21/21108.pdf), pp. 13754–13760, confirms that 21/8267 was referred to committees on 12 November, not finally rejected. Karoline Otte introduced the Greens’ proposal; Parsa Marvi supported wealth-tax revival but said it was not in the coalition agreement; Christian Görke supported the proposal’s direction while advocating a broader tax. His qualification is preserved. Some secondary pages incorrectly reported an October rejection; the official record controls.
