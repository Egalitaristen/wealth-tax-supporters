## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Historical context:** Czechia has not historically had an annual net wealth tax (*daň z čistého jmění*). The tax system relies predominantly on personal income taxes (*daň z příjmů fyzických osob*), corporate taxes, value-added tax (*DPH*), and social insurance contributions. The real estate property tax (*daň z nemovitých věcí*) generates less than 1.5% of total tax revenue.
- **Active proposals & models:**
  - **Zelení / Palanský Model (2024):** In May 2024, Strana zelených (Green Party) published a detailed academic study by economist Dr. Miroslav Palanský (*Studie: Daň ze superbohatství*).
    - Threshold: Slices starting at 111 million CZK (~€4.4 million) at a 3.5% rate; progressive variants modeled from 33 million and 68 million CZK.
    - Yield: Estimated at 58 billion CZK (~€2.3 billion) annually, affecting approximately 4,200 individuals.
    - Mechanism: Taxes accumulated net wealth after debt deduction (e.g. mortgages), with exemptions for primary residences, designed to fund healthcare, education, and climate modernization.
    - Champions: Johanna Nejedlová (lead EP candidate), Michal Berg (party co-chair), Dr. Miroslav Palanský.
  - **SOCDEM Model (formerly ČSSD, 2021 & 2023):**
    - Included in the official 2021 election manifesto: a wealth tax on "giant fortunes" above 100 million CZK (*majetková daň z obřích majetků nad sto milionů korun*) to cover covid costs; the 1% rate comes from Špidla's 2023 article, not from the 2021 report.
    - Reaffirmed in 2023 policy articles by former Prime Minister Vladimír Špidla and backed by party chair Jana Maláčová.
  - **Left out as generic:** Trade union chairman Josef Středula (ČMKOS) said taxes must rise, including "zdanění majetku", and economist Ilona Švihlíková said "majetkové daně neexistují". Neither names a wealth tax, and Czechia dropped its inheritance and gift taxes in 2014, so both are in `exclusions.csv`.
  - **Wealth Managers & Business Commentators:** Mikuláš Splítek (portfolio manager at Erste Asset Management) published a prominent 2025 column in *Hospodářské noviny* endorsing a progressive wealth tax to curb compounding wealth inequality.
  - **European Parliament Roll Calls:** Czech Pirate Party MEPs (Markéta Gregorová, Marcel Kolaja, Mikuláš Peksa) voted in favor of EU wealth tax amendments on EU own resources in May 2023 (vote 155121) and December 2023 (vote 162327).
- **Key Exclusions (Ground Rule 1):**
  - **Jakub Michálek (Piráti MP):** Proposed taxing capital gains on sales of companies/stocks over 40 million CZK and windfall profits, but explicitly not a recurring annual net wealth tax. Excluded under `out_of_definition`.
  - **KSČM (Komunistická strana Čech a Moravy):** Election platform proposed a 40% income tax bracket for income over 1 million CZK/year and asset declarations, not an annual net wealth tax. Excluded under `out_of_definition`.

## 4. Parties & parliamentary alignment
- **ANO 2011 (80 seats):** Centrist / populist; opposes net wealth taxes.
- **ODS (27 seats):** Center-right / conservative; staunch opponent of property and wealth taxes.
- **STAN (22 seats):** Mayors and Independents; focus on municipal tax revenues and local property tax coefficient autonomy.
- **Piráti (18 seats):** Progressive / digital liberal; MEPs voted for EU wealth tax; national deputies currently focus on capital gains and windfall taxation rather than domestic net wealth tax.
- **KDU-ČSL (16 seats):** Christian democrats; oppose net wealth tax.
- **SPD (15 seats):** Right-wing populist; opposes wealth taxation.
- **Motoristé sobě (13 seats):** Right-wing libertarian / anti-regulation; strictly opposes new taxation.
- **TOP 09 (9 seats):** Fiscal conservative; strictly opposes wealth taxation.
- **SOCDEM (Extra-parliamentary):** Primary left champion; supports 1% wealth tax above 100m CZK.
- **Strana zelených (Extra-parliamentary):** Primary green champion; supports 3.5% super-wealth tax above 111m CZK based on Palanský's 2024 study.

## 5. Government below national level
- **14 Kraje (Regions):** Higher territorial self-governing units (*vyšší územní samosprávné celky*), established by Constitutional Act No. 347/1997 Coll.
- **6,258 Obce (Municipalities):** Basic territorial self-governing units.
- **Taxation powers:** Subnational units do not possess independent legislative power to introduce taxes. Tax revenues are redistributed under the Act on Budgetary Allocation of Taxes (*Rozpočtové určení daní - RUD*). Municipalities can only adjust the local coefficient (*místní koeficient*) of the national real estate tax. Hence, all wealth tax initiatives must be legislated at the national parliament level.

## 6. Search terms
- **Specific wealth tax terms:** `daň ze superbohatství`, `daň z bohatství`, `daň z čistého jmění`, `zdanění velkého majetku`, `zdanění superbohatých`, `zdanění miliardářů`.
- **Ambiguous terms to watch:** `majetková daň` / `majetkové daně` (frequently used in Czech public debate to refer strictly to the real estate property tax *daň z nemovitých věcí* or transfer/inheritance taxes). Always verify whether the proposal specifies net wealth (*čisté jmění*) or real estate only.
