## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** none. Ghana has no tax on net wealth. The "Growth and Sustainability Levy" is a levy on company profits; the 2018 "high net worth income tax" was an income-tax band ([Citi Newsroom](https://citinewsroom.com/2018/08/30/35-income-tax-hits-mps-as-they-lament-ghc2000-cut-in-salaries/)); property rates are local. The Ghana Revenue Authority's High Net Worth Office enforces income tax on wealthy individuals ([MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/taxation-of-high-net-worth-individuals-in-ghana/)). None of these counts.
- **Bills and motions:** none found. No wealth-tax bill or motion in Parliament turned up in web searches; parliament.gh and Hansard were not read, so this is not proof of absence. `resources_proposed.jsonl` holds the one document found.
- **Live debate:** carried by economists and civil society rather than legislators. Prof Godfred Bokpin (University of Ghana) asked for a wealth tax in January 2022 and for annual taxation of net worth in August 2026. AbibiNsroma Foundation and two other Ghana-based organisations are on the November 2025 pan-African open letter asking South Africa's G20 presidency to champion a 1% wealth tax on Africa's richest 1%.
- **Government and opposition tone:** the Minority has vowed to block new taxes and levies ([MyJoyOnline](https://www.myjoyonline.com/minority-vows-to-block-new-taxes-cites-hardship-on-ghanaians/)); the TUC and ICU object to new taxes that fall on workers. Neither side was found asking for a tax on wealth.
