## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **No wealth tax exists.** Georgia has a flat 20% income tax, a property tax on real estate and vehicles above an income threshold, and no inheritance tax. The 2010 Transparency International tax overview says there was no wealth, inheritance or capital gains tax.
- **2003:** the Zhvania government floated a wealth tax (before the 2010 window; `X0005`).
- **2017 and 2023:** the Social-Democrats for the Development of Georgia and a list of 25 organisations, including the Georgian Trade Unions Confederation, asked to delete article 94 of the constitution, which bars tax rises without a referendum. The coverage frames it as opening the way to progressive income tax; no text found mentions wealth (`X0001` to `X0003`).
- **2024 to 2025:** the Georgian Dream majority raised the income threshold for the property tax (`X0004`) and passed relief for offshore assets brought to Georgia (`X0006`); opposition MPs criticised the second as protecting Bidzina Ivanishvili. Nobody called for a wealth tax.
- **Election of 26 October 2024:** the CEC count gave Georgian Dream 89 of 150 seats; the four opposition lists boycotted or lost their mandates.
