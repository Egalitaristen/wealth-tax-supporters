## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **In force:** the Vermögenssteuer in the [Steuergesetz (SteG, 640.0) of 23 September 2010](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/2010340000), version of 1 July 2026. Section B of the law is the "Vermögens- und Erwerbssteuer": its object is the taxpayer's whole movable and immovable wealth (Art. 9), valued at market value at the start of the tax year with debts deducted (Art. 11 and 12). Natural persons resident in the country are taxed on all their wealth (Art. 6).
- **How the amount is computed:** the wealth is converted into a standardised return (Sollertrag) of 4% (Art. 56), which is taxed at the national tariff (the tariff article applies to the taxable income "including the wealth converted into income"). So the effective rate on net wealth is low. This is borderline: it is a deemed-return method like the Dutch box 3, but the law itself calls it a wealth tax (Vermögenssteuer) on net wealth, and the tax base is wealth, so it is kept as in force. `[verify: the tariff and conversion rule were read in the law's text, not in an official guide; a Liechtenstein tax guide should confirm the effective rate]`
