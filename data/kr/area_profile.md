# South Korea: area profile

## Scope and limitations
The database remains a partial seed. Korean and English searches cover selected national party policies, a parliamentary debate, civil society and a limited regional sample; none are exhaustive. Historical support may not reflect current views.

## Context and sources
- Chung Dong-young proposed an annual tax on net assets above 3 billion won in 2011: https://www.donga.com/news/amp/all/20110121/34265760/9.
- In 2026 the Korean Confederation of Trade Unions called for a wealth tax and taxation of net assets above 5 billion won: https://nodong.org/statement/7923815.
- A 2024 Labour Party event collected discussions of wealth taxation: https://www.laborparty.kr/?mod=document&page_id=13648&pageid=1&uid=2801. Individual positions and party policy require further verification before inclusion.
- Korea also taxes property and inheritance; these taxes alone do not satisfy the database definition: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-surveys-korea-2026_6b87f585-en/full-report/tax-reform-for-growth-and-revenue_d7cb1938.html.

## Geography and source limitations
The register and map contain 16 regions and 230 local units based on the 1 October 2026 SGIS-derived [admdongkor boundaries](https://github.com/vuski/admdongkor/tree/master/ver20261001), CC BY 4.0 with Statistics Korea SGIS KOGL Type 1 attribution. Non-autonomous districts were combined into their cities; Sejong is one map unit, and Jeju administrative cities lack elected councils. The codes still need comparison with an official current list. R003 sampled Seoul, Gyeonggi, Busan, Gwangju, Ulsan and Jeju through web searches; this was not a sweep of each district. Gwangju and Ulsan produced attributable regional or local entries, while the other sampled regions produced no distinct eligible council record. Do not expand a unit-by-unit sweep without stronger evidence of yield. Direct shell access was restricted; the suggestions database could not be checked.
