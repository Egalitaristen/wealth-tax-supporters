## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- Cyprus has no tax on net wealth. The central property tax was abolished in 2017; the 2013 deposit haircut and the special defence contribution are not wealth taxes.
- The 2025-26 tax reform (income tax scale, corporate tax to 15%) passed the House in December 2025. AKEL tabled two bills that month: an annual 0.1% tax on immovable property above 3 million euro and a levy on registered companies by asset size. The Finance Ministry opposed them and they were rejected [outcome from Dialogos and Nomoplatform reports; the plenary record at parliament.cy blocks scripts]. Both are property or company levies, so they are excluded from this register (X0001).
- AKEL's memorandum of 18 September 2025 asks for "measures taxing wealth", mainly high-value property.
