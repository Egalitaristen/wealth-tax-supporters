Updated 2026-11-13 (seed pass R001).

## 1. Scope
- **Area:** Serbia (Republic of Serbia, Kosovo excluded); ISO2 `RS`; country root `G0001`.
- **Depth:** Seed. National level: parties, think tanks, economists, commentators, unions, polls. 5 supporters (grade B 2, C 3), 5 evidence rows, 7 exclusions, 12 queries, 4 open leads.
- **Levels in scope:** national (National Assembly `B0001`). The register lists 4 statistical regions, 25 districts and 168 municipalities and city municipalities with a map (Eurostat GISCO, CC BY 4.0). Local councils were not searched: taxing wealth is a national power and the property tax is the only wealth-related tax local government sets.
- **Languages:** Serbian in Latin and Cyrillic, English.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **No wealth tax exists** and none has been tabled in the National Assembly. The tools Serbia uses are the property tax (set by municipalities), the inheritance and gift tax (first-line heirs and spouses pay nothing) and the annual income tax on high earners, which the press calls *porez na bogate* ([N1](https://n1info.rs/biznis/porez-na-bogate-najveci-prijavljeni-prihod-16-milijardi-dinara-200-miliona-veci-nego-godinu-pre/)). None of them is a tax on net wealth (exclusions `X0001` to `X0005`).
- **April 2021.** The Ne davimo Beograd initiative says it will raise a set of taxes on accumulated wealth: on property not lived in, on total wealth and on inheritance and gifts (`A0001`).
- **December 2022.** The Green-Left parliamentary club and Ne davimo Beograd table a one-off crisis tax on companies' extra profit and relief for family homes in the property tax ([Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/ekonomija/ne-davimo-beograd-predlaze-uvodjenje-poreza-na-ekstra-profit-ostvaren-u-vreme-krize/)); out of definition (`X0002`).
- **March 2023.** A Dveri vice-president says Serbian tax policy "does not know" a tax on wealth and luxury (`A0005`).
- **August 2023.** The Green-Left Front programme mentions a "tax on net worldwide wealth" in passing, in the section on taxing luxury vehicles, boats and aircraft (`A0002`).
- **June 2024.** Political scientist Dušan Pavlović presents the Berlin Declaration's progressive wealth tax in Vreme (`A0004`).
- **January 2026.** Sociologist Vladimir Simović of the Centre for Emancipatory Politics wants the extremely rich taxed on total wealth (`A0003`).
- **25 October 2026.** Early parliamentary election. The Student List won 140 of 250 seats and the Vučić-led list 103 (final results 5 November). Its economic plan (7 November) aggregates property for the property tax and raises tax on large inheritances; out of definition (`X0001`).

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
See `skill/references/records/rs.md`. No bill or amendment for a wealth tax was found; the asset-origin bill of 2019 (`3199-19`) is a tax on unexplained assets and was not read in full (`M0002`).

## 4. Parties
Green-Left Front (ZLF; successor of Ne davimo Beograd): property tax reform and a passing mention of the net worldwide wealth tax. Student List: property and inheritance only. Dveri: complaint that there is no wealth tax. The Party of Freedom and Justice, Narodna stranka and the Democratic Party were searched: only income or property tax found.
