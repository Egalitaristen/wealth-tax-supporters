## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- No net wealth tax. Serbia has a property tax with locally set rates [verify].
- "Porez na bogate" is the public name of the annual personal income tax for incomes above three average annual wages (10% to 15%) ([N1](https://n1info.rs/biznis/porez-na-bogate-oslobadjanje-fondovi-vista-rica/), [021.rs](https://www.021.rs/info/biznis-i-ekonomija/314788/milioneri-u-srbiji-su-menadzeri-i-direktori-strucnjaci-o-tome-treba-li-ih-dodatno-oporezovati)); out of definition.
- 2020 law on the origin of assets taxes unexplained wealth [verify]; out of definition.
- 2022: Zeleno-levi poslanicki klub proposes a crisis tax on extra profits (windfall).
- 2023: ZLF programme: property tax reform, tax on cars, boats and aircraft by value, inheritance tax ([program](https://zelenolevifront.rs/program/)); no net wealth tax.
- Coverage of the Zucman tax and the G20 track exists (Vreme, 021.rs, Danas) but names no Serbian supporter. A Danas columnist wrote that Serbia lacks an MP like Elizabeth Warren ([Danas](https://www.danas.rs/kolumna/gojko-vlaovic/ispravna-ideja-senatorke-voren/)).
