## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Current status:** Malawi has never enacted a standalone wealth tax or tax on individual net worth.
- **Existing tax framework:** Administered by the Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA) under the Ministry of Finance. Relies on Personal Income Tax (PAYE), Corporate Income Tax (CIT, 30%), Value Added Tax (VAT, 16.5%), and Estate Duty under the Estate Duty Act (up to 11% on inheritance upon death).
- **Key debate moments & proposals:**
  - **July 2025 CRI Profile Launch:** Economics Association of Malawi (ECAMA), Oxfam in Malawi, Centre for Social Concern (CfSC), and the Malawi Economic Justice Network (MEJN) launched the *Commitment to Reducing Inequality (CRI) Malawi Country Profile 2025*. ECAMA President Dr. Bertha Bangara-Chikadza presented a 5-point reform agenda proposing a 0.7% moderate wealth tax ($70 million/year) to fund an essential package of healthcare for 890,000 citizens. CfSC Economic Governance Officer Agnes Nyirongo proposed a solidarity wealth levy on the richest 1% to 5% of individuals.
  - **January 2022 Post-Pandemic Redesign:** Oxfam in Malawi (Mathias Kafunda) called on government to claw back extreme wealth through progressive taxation following the *Inequality Kills* report.
  - **January 2026 Pension Tax Pushback:** Malawi Congress of Trade Unions (MCTU) rejected Treasury proposals on taxing retirement pensions, demanding alternative revenue measures targeting wealth, tax evasion, and high-income earners.
- **Excluded near-misses:**
  - Top 40% PAYE income tax band for monthly earnings above K10 million (income tax, not net wealth).
  - Minimum Alternative Tax (MAT) on gross turnover (corporate revenue tax).
  - Capital gains tax on listed shares (gains upon realization).
  - Mobile money transfer levy and bank transaction fees.

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
- **Chamber:** National Assembly of Malawi (unicameral parliament; 193 seats, expanded to 229 seats for 2025 elections by MEC).
- **Debate & Vote records:** [parliament.gov.mw](https://parliament.gov.mw). Parliamentary Hansard and bills registers are published intermittently.
- **Committees:** Budget and Finance Committee of Parliament (chaired by Hon. Gladys Ganda) conducts pre-budget consultations.
- **Parliamentary position:** No government wealth-tax bill or private member's motion has been introduced in the National Assembly. Parliamentary discussions have focused on Taxation (Amendment) Bills adjusting PAYE, VAT, and customs duties.

## 4. Parties
- **Malawi Congress Party (MCP):** Ruling party under President Lazarus Chakwera. Fiscal focus on PAYE brackets, compliance on rental properties, and domestic resource mobilization; no wealth tax proposal in manifesto.
- **Democratic Progressive Party (DPP):** Major opposition led by former President Peter Mutharika. Focuses on private sector investment, corporate tax incentives, and expenditure cuts.
- **United Transformation Movement (UTM):** Focuses on digital tax compliance and anti-corruption; no wealth tax plank.
- **United Democratic Front (UDF) & AFORD:** No wealth tax policies recorded.

## 5. Government below national level
- **Structure:** 3 Regions (Northern `MW1`, Central `MW2`, Southern `MW3`), subdivided into 32 Local Government Authorities: 28 District Councils and 4 City Councils (Lilongwe City `MW210`, Blantyre City `MW315`, Mzuzu City `MW107`, Zomba City `MW314`).
- **Taxation powers:** Under the Local Government Act, local councils only collect property rates (city/ground rates), market fees, and business licensing fees; they have no statutory authority over income, capital, or net wealth.
- **Local decision (6.5):** Skip unit-by-unit sweep. Local authorities lack jurisdiction over wealth taxation and publish no debate minutes discussing national tax policy.

## 9. Known networks
- **Economics Association of Malawi (ECAMA):** Professional economist body, active proponent of wealth tax reforms.
- **Centre for Social Concern (CfSC):** Faith-based social justice research organisation, proponent of a solidarity wealth levy on the top 1–5%.
- **Oxfam in Malawi:** Country branch of Oxfam International; publishes CRI country profiles and inequality reports.
- **Malawi Economic Justice Network (MEJN):** Civil society coalition on economic governance, member of Tax Justice Network Africa (TJNA).
- **Malawi Congress of Trade Unions (MCTU):** National trade union federation.
- **ActionAid Malawi:** Active in corporate tax justice and combating illicit financial flows.
