## 2. Wealth-tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- Hungary has no net wealth tax. The Orbán governments never proposed one.
- 2020-05: opposition MPs (Szél, Hadházy, Szabó Szabolcs) tabled a special levy on recipients of state money; rejected in committee. Out of definition (see exclusions).
- 2023-10-11: six Párbeszéd MPs tabled T/5575 "A szolidaritási vagyonadóról" (1.7% / 2.1% / 3.5% above HUF 600m).
- 2025-08-30: Péter Magyar (Tisza) pledged a wealth tax above HUF 5bn; the Tisza programme later set 1% above HUF 1bn.
- 2025-10-03: Párbeszéd tabled T/12722 "A milliárdosok vagyonadójáról" (1% above HUF 1bn).
- 2026-04: Tisza won the election. A government decision of 14 May 2026 (1147/2026) ordered preparation of the law [verify: seen in a search summary only].
- 2026-10-06: the government announced 1% above HUF 1bn and 1.5% above HUF 100bn from 1 January 2027, first payment by 31 August 2027, and put the draft out for public consultation. Whether Parliament has voted is not checked.
- Business federations (MGYOSZ, MKIK, VOSZ and others) commented on the draft and want carve-outs; recorded as exclusions, not supporters.
