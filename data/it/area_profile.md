## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- **Existing partial/asset-specific taxes (excluded on their own):** Italy does not levy a comprehensive recurring tax on net personal wealth. Instead, since the December 2011 Monti "Salva Italia" decree (D.L. 201/2011), Italy levies separate taxes on specific asset classes: IMU (municipal property tax on real estate excluding primary residences), IVIE (real estate held abroad), IVAFE (financial assets held abroad), and the proportional stamp duty (*imposta di bollo*) of 0.2% on bank/securities accounts. Because Italian public debate frequently calls IMU and the 0.2% stamp duty *"mini-patrimoniali"*, every candidate statement using the word *patrimoniale* must be checked to ensure it refers to a unified tax on the stock of net wealth, large fortunes, or a billionaire minimum tax rather than property or stamp taxes alone.
- **December 2020 — Orfini–Fratoianni Budget Law amendment (`H0002`):** During the 2021 Budget Law (*Legge di Bilancio 2021*) examination in the Chamber of Deputies Budget Committee, Matteo Orfini (PD) and Nicola Fratoianni (Sinistra Italiana / LeU) tabled an amendment (co-signed initially by 9 MPs from LeU and PD and subsequently by Enza Bruno Bossio and M5S MP Andrea Colletti) to replace IMU and the bank/securities stamp duty with a progressive annual net wealth tax starting at 0.2% on net wealth between EUR 500,000 and EUR 1m, 0.5% up to EUR 5m, 1% up to EUR 50m, and 2% above EUR 50m (plus a 3% rate above EUR 1bn for 2021) ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 3 Dec 2020](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2020/12/03/patrimoniale-ok-allemendamento-fratoianni-risparmio-per-ceto-medio-orfini-e-nel-dna-pd-lotta-a-disuguaglianze-non-solo-ai-convegni/6025437/); [Matteo Orfini](https://www.matteoorfini.it/patrimoniale/)). After committee rapporteur Stefano Fassina gave a negative opinion and the ruling coalition opposed it, the amendment was withdrawn before a roll-call vote ([Domani](https://www.editorialedomani.it/politica/italia/la-patrimoniale-stata-ritirata-no-del-governo-e-della-maggioranza-ei6vvqby)).
- **September 2022 — Forum Disuguaglianze e Diversità proposal:** Ahead of the 2022 general election, ForumDD (coordinated by Fabrizio Barca) published its comparative platform proposing a progressive net wealth tax from 0.2% above EUR 1m up to 2% above EUR 50m (plus a 3% one-off pandemic solidarity levy) ([ForumDD comparison table PDF](https://www.forumdisuguaglianzediversita.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/25-Settembre_ForumDD_TABELLE-8.x37342.pdf)).
- **November 2023 / December 2024 — Campagna Sbilanciamoci! *Controfinanziaria* (`H0005`):** The coalition of 54 civil-society organisations annually proposes an *"Imposta sulle grandi ricchezze"* — an ordinary progressive net wealth tax on personal movable and immovable wealth above EUR 1m up to 2% above EUR 500m, estimated to raise EUR 24bn/year ([Controfinanziaria 2024 PDF](https://sbilanciamoci.info/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Controfinanziaria_2024_web.pdf)).
- **22 May 2024 — *Manifesto per un'agenda Tax the Rich per l'Italia* (`H0001`):** Drafted by Demetrio Guzzardi, Elisa Palagi and Andrea Roventini (Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies, Pisa) and signed by 134 economists from 50 Italian and foreign universities, the Manifesto was presented at the Italian Senate in a roundtable coordinated by Oxfam Italia and Patriotic Millionaires ([Sant'Anna news](https://www.santannapisa.it/it/news/tassare-lestrema-ricchezza-e-giusto-e-fattibile-134-economisti-di-50-universita-hanno-firmato); [Avvenire](https://www.avvenire.it/economia/lavoro/tassare-la-ricchezza-estrema-e-giusto-e-fattibile-ecco-come_77103); full signatory list in [Policy Maker](https://www.policymakermag.it/italia/chi-sono-i-154-economisti-italiani-che-vogliono-tassare-i-super-ricchi-ecco-il-manifesto/)). It calls for a progressive annual wealth tax on the richest 0.1% of Italian citizens (individual net wealth above EUR 5.4m, ~50,000 individuals) with rates of 1.7% (EUR 5.4m–8m), 2.1% (EUR 8m–20m) and 3.5% (above EUR 20m), estimated to raise EUR 13.2–15.7bn/year, alongside the European Citizens' Initiative *Tax the Rich* (`#LaGrandeRicchezza`).
- **December 2024 & November 2025 — AVS Budget Law amendments:** Alleanza Verdi e Sinistra (AVS) tabled annual budget amendments in the Chamber (Marco Grimaldi, [verdisinistra.it, 10 Dec 2024](https://verdisinistra.it/bilancio-grimaldi-avs-su-patrimoniale-non-molleremo-mai/)) for a progressive wealth tax above EUR 5.4m, and in the Senate (Senator Tino Magni, Nov 2025) for a 1.3% single ordinary annual wealth tax on net wealth above EUR 2m ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 14 Nov 2025](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2025/11/14/patrimoniale-avs-manovra-ricchezze-notizie/8195834/)).
- **November 2025 — CGIL solidarity contribution proposal:** CGIL General Secretary Maurizio Landini launched the union confederation's proposal for a 1.3%–1.5% annual solidarity contribution on net wealth above EUR 2m (the richest 1%, ~500,000 taxpayers), estimated to raise EUR 26bn/year for healthcare, education and wages ([CGIL, 12 Nov 2025](https://www.cgil.it/la-cgil/aree-politiche/segreteria-generale/maurizio-landini-un-contributo-di-solidarieta-per-ridurre-le-diseguaglianze-e-finanziare-sanita-e-scuola-dxc654m9)).
- **7 May 2026 — Popular initiative bill *1% Equo* ("Imposta grandi patrimoni", `H0003`):** Promoted by Partito della Rifondazione Comunista and Comitato 1% Equo (11 first signatories filed at the Court of Cassation on 7 May 2026), proposing an annual progressive wealth tax on net wealth above EUR 2m (excluding the primary residence) with brackets of 1% (EUR 2m–5m), 1.7% (EUR 5m–8m), 2.1% (EUR 8m–20m) and 3.5% (above EUR 20m) ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 7 May 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/05/07/imposta-patrimoniale-1equo-grandi-patrimoni-notizie/8378151/); [La Notte Online](https://www.lanotteonline.it/2026/05/08/patrimoniale-sui-grandi-patrimoni-parte-la-sfida-del-comitato-1equo-aliquote-fino-al-35/)). By autumn 2026 the campaign reported over 65,000 verified signatures.
- **September–October 2026 — M5S, Fondazione Demo (PD), and AVS *Restituzione fiscale*:**
  - On 18 September 2026, Movimento 5 Stelle put 20 programmatic points to its members including a national progressive levy on large financial assets (excluding small savings, pensions and government bonds) plus an EU wealth tax and exit tax ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/09/18/m5s-programma-fisco-extraprofitti-patrimoni-notizie/8510766/)). M5S MP Chiara Appendino and MEP Pasquale Tridico went further, advocating a full national wealth tax above EUR 5m ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 13 Jun 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/06/13/tassa-grandi-patrimoni-conte-appendino-notizie/8418494/)).
  - An economics working group coordinated by Emanuele Felice and former Finance Minister Vincenzo Visco for Fondazione Demo (chaired by PD MP Gianni Cuperlo) drafted a national Zucman-style tax of 2–3% on net wealth above EUR 100m (~2,500 taxpayers, est. EUR 8bn/year) ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/10/02/meno-tasse-al-98-degli-italiani-e-patrimoniale-ai-super-ricchi-la-proposta-di-avs-che-sfida-conte-e-schlein-coraggio/8525437/)).
  - On 2 October 2026, AVS leaders Nicola Fratoianni and Angelo Bonelli presented *"Restituzione fiscale"*, combining an IRPEF cut for 98% of taxpayers with an annual progressive wealth tax on net wealth above EUR 5.4m (est. EUR 15.9bn/year) and a 26% exit tax on unrealised capital gains ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/10/02/meno-tasse-al-98-degli-italiani-e-patrimoniale-ai-super-ricchi-la-proposta-di-avs-che-sfida-conte-e-schlein-coraggio/8525437/)).
- **Public opinion polls (in `meta.json`):**
  - **69% in favour** of a progressive wealth tax on net wealth above EUR 5m (57.1% for a generic wealth tax without specifying the threshold): Eumetra for *Piazzapulita* (La7), published June 2026 ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2026/06/10/patrimoniale-economisti-riforma-fiscale-notizie/8415001/)).
  - **70% in favour** of a progressive tax on large fortunes (richest 0.1%, net wealth above EUR 5.4m): Istituto Demopolis for Oxfam Italia, May 2024 ([Il Fatto Quotidiano, 8 Oct 2024](https://www.ilfattoquotidiano.it/2024/10/08/schlein-a-skytg24-tassa-internazionale-sui-super-ricchi-si-discuta-a-livello-europeo/7722578/)).

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
- **Camera dei deputati** (`camera.it`, `aic.camera.it`, `documenti.camera.it`) and **Senato della Repubblica** (`senato.it`). Both `senato.it` and `aic.camera.it` sit behind Radware ShieldSquare / Captcha protection and return HTTP 403 or a challenge page to plain HTTP clients. Furthermore, Italian budget law wealth-tax amendments (2020 Orfini–Fratoianni, 2024 Grimaldi, 2025 Magni) are examined in the 5th Committee (*Commissione Bilancio*), where amendments declared inadmissible, withdrawn, or rejected by voice vote do not generate plenary electronic roll calls. Press reports that quote the amendment texts and list sponsors verbatim (e.g. *Il Fatto Quotidiano*, *Avvenire*, party portals) are openly accessible to `checkquotes`.
- **Regional and Autonomous Provincial Councils:** e.g. Consiglio della Provincia autonoma di Bolzano (`landtag-bz.org`, open PDF repository at `api-idap.landtag-bz.org/doc/`), Consiglio regionale del Trentino-Alto Adige (`consiglio.regione.taa.it`).

## 5. Government below national level
- **Structure:** 20 regions (`level=region`, `ISO 3166-2:IT`), 107 provinces / metropolitan cities / free municipal consortia (`level=county`, ISTAT 3-digit `COD_UTS`), and 7,896 *comuni* (`level=council`, ISTAT 6-digit `PRO_COM_T` from the 1 Jan 2025 ISTAT register and generalised shapefiles, CC BY 4.0).
- **Taxing powers:** Regions and municipalities cannot independently levy a general net wealth tax (municipalities administer IMU on real estate within statutory caps set by national law). Regional and municipal motions (*mozioni*, *ordini del giorno*, *voti*) therefore call on the Italian Government or the EU to introduce a tax on large fortunes, or local party federations coordinate municipal URP signature collection for the *1% Equo* popular initiative bill.
- **Local decision (after sampling in R001):** **Sampled / targeted only.** In R001 we sampled regional/provincial councils and municipal signature campaigns: we found and recorded Voto n. 39/25 (*"Tax the Rich"*, co-sponsored by Sandro Repetto of PD and Brigitte Foppa of Verdi-Grüne-Vërc in the Provincial Council of Bolzano and Regional Council of Trentino-Alto Adige, rejected 10 Yes / 21 No / 2 Abstentions on 19 Sep 2025) and the Lucca provincial federation of Rifondazione Comunista coordinating signature collection across 9 municipalities in Lucca province. Most Italian municipal *albi pretori* expire after 15 days or require JS session tokens, so future local sweeps should target regional council portals and *1% Equo* / *Tax the Rich* local press releases rather than blind municipality-by-municipality crawling.

- **Ambiguous terms to watch:** *"patrimoniale"* on its own is often used by right-wing critics to describe IMU (property tax), cadastral value updates, or the 0.2% stamp duty on bank/securities accounts; *"extraprofitti"* refers to corporate windfall profit taxes (e.g. UIL leader Pierpaolo Bombardieri, excluded in `X0005`).
