# Area profile — Bulgaria

Updated 2026-11-04 (run R001). Lines marked [verify] come from a search summary only.

## 1. Scope
- Area: Bulgaria (country). Depth: Seed. National level; the 28 oblasti and 265 obshtini are loaded in `geo_units.csv` but not swept.
- Languages: Bulgarian, English.
- First run: 2026-11-04.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
- No net wealth tax exists; there is a municipal property tax and a flat 10% income tax [verify].
- 2012: a Capital article reports the BSP leadership discussing a "wealth" tax alongside ending the flat tax [verify, page blocks scripts; open lead in leads.csv].
- 2015 and 2018: BSP bills and alternative budgets for a progressive income tax (out of definition).
- 2017 to 2019: KNSB asks for a "данък богатство" on second homes, villas, luxury cars and yachts ([Mediapool 2017-09-10](https://www.mediapool.bg/knsb-pak-poiska-danak-bogatstvo-za-skapi-vili-koli-i-yahti-news269043.html), [Барикада 2017-06-05](https://baricada.org/2017/06/05/knsb-zaplati-danuk-bogatstvo/), [Утро Русе 2019-07-10](https://utroruse.com/article/761022/)).
- 2023: Bulgarian S&D MEPs vote for EU wealth tax amendments (roll calls of 10 May and 12 December 2023).
- April 2026 election: Progressive Bulgaria 131 of 240 seats; BSP is out of parliament [verify]. Capital, 19 Jun 2026: the cabinet considers a progressive housing tax (property, out of definition) [verify].
- 26 Oct 2026: Podkrepa proposes a 0.5% "wealth" tax on aggregate property above a threshold, aimed at owners of more than three high-value properties ([Кворум Силистра](https://www.kvorum-silistra.info/news/71557)).
- Opponent: BIA economist Dimitar Brankov warns it could hit the middle class ([bia-bg.com](https://www.bia-bg.com/service/view/25733/)).

## 3. Legislature
- Narodno sabranie (240 seats). Open data: `https://www.parliament.bg/api/v1/coll-list/bg/2` (groups) and `/api/v1/coll-list-ns/bg` (members with group and constituency). Bills and transcripts sit in a JavaScript app and were not searched by keyword.
- European Parliament roll calls: `PV-9-<date>-RCV_EN.pdf` (blocks scripts; Wayback copies read) and howtheyvote.eu.

## 4. Parties
- In parliament since 30 April 2026: Progressive Bulgaria, GERB-SDS, DPS, Democratic Bulgaria, We Continue the Change, Vazrazhdane. No party programme found that names a net wealth tax.

## 5. Government below national level
- 28 oblasti (NUTS 3) and 265 obshtini. Municipalities set the property tax rate within a statutory range, so local actors have no wealth tax power. Codes: Eurostat GISCO LAU/NUTS 2024. No local sweep.

## 6. Search terms
