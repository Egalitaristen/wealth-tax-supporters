# Slovenia — first research pass (4 November 2026)

## 1. Scope
- Area: Slovenia; national level, depth Seed. Languages: Slovene, English.
- Geography register: 212 občine under 12 statistical regions; none swept.

## 2. History and live proposals
- Property tax (nepremičninski davek) was struck down in part by the Constitutional Court in 2014 and is not a wealth tax; excluded.
- May 2022: the Svoboda–SD–Levica coalition agreement promises "progresivna obdavčitev premoženja"; scope unclear ([N1](https://n1info.si/novice/slovenija/koalicijska-pogodba-tekst/)). In September 2024 Prime Minister Golob said no wealth tax this term [verify].
- Dec 2024: the Finance Ministry's starting points tax non-residential real estate only; ZSSS asked for a tax on all wealth.
- 2024: the "Tax the Rich" ECI was backed by SD and Vesna; Greenpeace published scenarios in January 2025.
- 2026 election questionnaire (RTV SLO): Pirates name a tax on the wealth of the richest; Levica/Vesna name capital and non-owner-occupied property only.

## 3. Search terms
davek na bogastvo, davek na premoženje, premoženjski davek, obdavčitev najbogatejših, davek na neto premoženje. "Davek na premoženje" often means real estate.

## 4. Findings
- Only one Državni zbor bill on a wealth tax was looked for and none found; Levica's 2022 bill taxes empty and large dwellings (excluded). The EPA register was not searched in full.
- Union, NGO and two small parties are the main supporters found. No Slovenian economist on record was found.
