# Area profile — Egypt

First written 27 October 2026 (R001). Seed pass.

## 1. Scope

- Area: Egypt. The first pass covers national-level policy research; the legislature, parties, civil society, trade unions and governorates remain unswept. The geographic register contains Egypt's 27 governorates as the initial local reporting tier, using ISO 3166-2 codes checked against the [geoBoundaries Egypt ADM1 catalogue](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/EGY/ADM1/).
- Languages searched: Arabic and English.

## 2. Wealth-tax evidence found

- Nader Osama's January 2018 [*Toward a Wealth Tax in Egypt*](https://api-aps.aucegypt.edu/uploads/articlesAttachments/1622113668pdfEN_Wealth%20Tax_Policy_Web.pdf), prepared for Alternative Policy Solutions at the American University in Cairo, proposes a comprehensive progressive wealth tax: 1% above US$1 million of wealth, rising to 5% above US$1 billion. This is the register's first directly attributable record.
- Oxfam International's October 2023 [MENA briefing](https://policy-practice.oxfam.org/resources/the-middle-east-and-north-africa-gap-prosperity-for-the-rich-austerity-for-the-621549/) focuses on Egypt, Lebanon, Morocco and Tunisia. It recommends permanent progressive wealth taxes, including at least a permanent 2% tax on net wealth, to governments across the region. Oxfam International is recorded (grade B, regional recommendation); its five named authors are not, because the paper is addressed to four countries.
- Egyptian business leader Hassan Heikal proposed a one-off global wealth tax of 10–20% on net worth above US$10 million in 2011; it is documented by [Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContent/3/12/27292/Business/Economy/Egyptian-businessman-proposes-global-wealth-tax.aspx) and cited in Osama's policy paper. In Cairo lectures in June 2016, Thomas Piketty called progressive taxation of wealth preferable to a flat levy; [Ahram Online's report](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/18/219217/Books/Thomas-Piketty-The-Middle-East-is-the-most-unequal.aspx) records the statement.
- In a 2020 [Mada Masr opinion article](https://www.madamasr.com/2020/04/12/opinion/u/%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%86-%D9%88%D9%82%D8%AA-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%BA%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A1/), Mohamed Saad Abdel Hafiz urges the authorities and parliament to enact a wealth tax, discussing one-off or progressive 10–25% designs for wealthy Egyptians.
- In the House of Representatives' June 2018 budget debate, former Alexandria MP Kamal Ahmed called for a wealth-tax bill; Speaker Ali Abdel Aal asked the finance minister to record the case for applying one even once. [Al-Ahram Gate's contemporaneous report](https://gate.ahram.org.eg/News/1965815.aspx) records both statements.
- A July 2026 [Masrawy interview](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_economy/details/2026/7/26/3023406/) records economist Hani Tawfik's proposal for a 2.5–3% Egyptian wealth tax beginning at EGP 10 million, and finance professor Hassan El-Sady's call for higher taxes on large fortunes.
- Three additional domestic parliamentary/research records are now included: former MPs Mostafa Abu Zeid (a 1% one-off tax on total individual wealth) and Mostafa Ragheb (a request for Parliament to study a one-off wealth tax), and the National Planning Institute's study recommending a one-off tax on net wealth. The [Youm7 report](https://www.youm7.com/story/2017/12/11/%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%AD-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-1-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A5%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%AB%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AF-%D9%84%D8%B5%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD/3549579), [Parlmany report](https://www.parlmany.com/News/12/107919/%D9%84%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86-%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%AF%D9%89-%D9%86%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%BA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D9%86%D9%88%D8%B5%D9%84-%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B7%D9%86-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%A6%D9%88%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%86), and [El Balad report](https://www.elbalad.news/2722093) provide the direct quotations. Public searches did not locate a wealth-tax bill number, formal co-sponsor roster, or roll-call; this is therefore a harvest of individual proposals, not a claim that all MPs who backed one bill have been found.
- A further Arabic pass found Hassan Heikal's signed 2013 article setting a US$10 million threshold and describing both a one-off levy and a 2–3% recurring version. A 2020 [Masr360 report](https://masr360.net/2020/05/28/%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AB%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%B9%D8%A8-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AB%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%87%D9%84-%D8%AA%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%AD-%D9%81/) records detailed and supportive positions by human-rights lawyer Negad El Borai, former social-solidarity minister Gouda Abdel Khalek, investment executive Khaled Abuheif and economist Wael Nahhas. Their statements are recorded individually; the report is not used to infer support by any other people it mentions.
- A direct 2019 [Al Mal conference report](https://almalnews.com/639725/%D8%B3%D9%85%D9%8A%D8%AD-%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%88%D9%8A%D8%B1%D8%B3-%D8%B7%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A8%D8%AA-%D8%A8%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%A8-5-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89/) verifies businessman Samih Sawiris's proposal for a temporary 5% levy on bank-account balances above EGP 1 million. This is recorded narrowly as a financial-wealth levy. His earlier opposition to a comprehensive wealth tax is not erased or treated as support for that separate design.
- Egypt does not currently have a general net-wealth tax. The temporary levy described in contemporary reporting as a “wealth tax” applied to high annual income and is not treated as qualifying wealth-tax evidence; see [Ahram Online's 2015 account](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/138713.aspx).

## 3. Search terms and cautions

- Arabic: `ضريبة الثروة`, `ضريبة على الثروات`, `ضريبة على صافي الثروة`, `ضريبة الميراث`, `العدالة الضريبية`.
- English: `Egypt wealth tax`, `Egypt net wealth tax`, `Egypt progressive wealth tax`, `Egypt inheritance tax`.
- Do not treat a tax on property, luxury goods, capital gains, inheritance, or high incomes as a wealth tax unless the source explicitly reaches an included design.
