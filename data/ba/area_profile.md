# Area profile — Bosnia and Herzegovina

## 1. Scope
First pass (2026-11-04), national and entity level, in Bosnian/Croatian/Serbian and English. Cantons, municipalities and the Republika Srpska assembly were not searched record by record.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals (2010 onwards)
No wealth tax exists. The FBiH government rejected an SDP BiH initiative (about 2016); SDP renewed it in February 2018. FES Sarajevo published a 2017 analysis recommending a 1% tax. A 2026 press piece mentions FBiH initiatives for a wealth tax and progressive property tax without naming sponsors. The Republika Srpska law on assets of unproven origin (75% special tax) is excluded as illicit-enrichment policy.

## 3. Legislatures and where their records are
