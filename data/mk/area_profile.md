# Area profile — North Macedonia

## 1. Scope
- Area: North Macedonia; Depth: Seed, national level only (2026-11-04). Languages: Macedonian, English (Albanian-language parties not yet searched).
- Geography: 8 statistical planning regions and 80 opštini, OCHA/HDX codes (CC BY-IGO). Not swept.

## 2. Wealth tax history and live proposals
- Personal income tax is flat (10%); no net wealth tax. A one-off "solidarity tax" on company profits (2022) was annulled by the Constitutional Court: out of definition.
- Before the 2024 elections Levica promised a tax on households' net assets (0 up to EUR 120,000; 0.25% around EUR 240,000; 1% above EUR 500,000) and SDSM a "luxury (wealth) tax" ([RFE/RL, 2024](https://northmacedonia.rferl.org/a/progresiven-ramen-oligarhiski-danok-izborna-programa/32925279.html)). Finance Think and economist Slavko Lazovski criticise the proposals in that article.
- Levica's Dimitar Apasiev listed a "luxury, i.e. wealth" tax among the party's measures in 2016 ([Okno](https://okno.mk/node/60295)).
