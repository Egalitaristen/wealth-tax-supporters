# Area profile — Ukraine

## 1. Scope

- Area: Ukraine (country). Depth: Seed. National, oblast and local-government layers are in scope; only the national layer has begun.
- Ukrainian and English searches were used. First run: 26 October 2026.

## 2. Wealth-tax evidence found

- GROWFORD Institute’s May 2021 research explicitly calls the introduction of a wealth tax a test of solidarity in Ukraine. Its [source](https://www.growford.org.ua/research/opodatkuvannya-bagatstva-v-ukrayini-test-na-solidarnist/) is the first organisational record.
- Economist Kostiantyn Shvabii’s July 2021 [signed column](https://www.growford.org.ua/chy-potriben-ukrayini-podatok-na-bagatstvo-blog-shvabiya/) supports a 1% wealth tax and estimates its annual revenue.
- Three authors of a 2017 peer-reviewed [Journal of European Economy article](https://jeej.wunu.edu.ua/index.php/ukjee/article/view/817) set out conditions and administrative proposals for introducing a wealth tax in Ukraine. A further legal-tax article calls for a 1–1.5% tax on large wealth, and two later peer-reviewed articles make direct recommendations for wealth taxation.
- Nataliya Frolova’s 2023 peer-reviewed [Finance of Ukraine article](https://finukr.org.ua/?page_id=723&aid=4910) finds a wealth tax justified for post-war Ukraine and specifies a broad base that includes financial assets, not merely real estate or luxury goods.
- Ulyana Kropantseva’s 2014 [Lviv Polytechnic conference paper](https://wiki.lpnu.ua/wiki/images/c/c9/%D0%97%D0%91%D0%86%D0%A0%D0%9A_%D0%A2%D0%95%D0%97_1-4_%D0%9A%D0%A3%D0%A0%D0%A1%D0%98_%D0%A1%D0%9D%D0%A2%D0%9A-42_2014.pdf) proposes a wealth tax with a multi-asset base including securities and bank deposits; it is retained as lower-specificity Grade B evidence.
- Initial searches also returned property taxes, luxury-goods taxes, income taxes and anti-oligarch measures. These are excluded unless a source explicitly reaches an included wealth-tax design.

## 3. Legislature and government below national level

- The Verkhovna Rada’s [bill register](https://itd.rada.gov.ua/billinfo/Bills/Card/2526) is the first source to search for qualifying bills and named sponsors. The preliminary results inspected had property, income or de-oligarchisation titles and were not added without the bill text proving an included design.
- The geography register contains 27 regions, 139 districts and 1,769 hromadas, using the CC BY 4.0 [Open Admin Data republication](https://github.com/open-admin-data/ukraine-administrative-divisions) of the State Statistics Service’s KATOTTG codifier. A licensed hromada-level boundary file has not yet been prepared. No regional or municipal unit has been marked searched.

## 4. Search terms and cautions

- Ukrainian terms: `податок на багатство`, `податок на статки`, `оподаткування багатства`, `податок на чисті активи`, `податок на великі статки`.
- `податок на розкіш`, `податок на нерухомість`, `податок на доходи`, `податок на капітал` and anti-oligarch measures often denote excluded taxes; read the design before retaining a candidate.

## Review corrections

Seven rows (and their people) were removed: three students and a postgraduate (private individuals), a co-author whose quote is a passing mention in a list, a paper on a journal page that could not be opened, and two conference papers whose quotes are not in the PDF text. The three authors of the 2017 'Wealth taxation: challenges for Ukraine' paper are grade C, because the abstract describes the paper ('prospects ... considered') rather than stating a position; the Pyroha pair, Sverdan and the 2012 Rada speech are grade B. The quotes of the Growford report, the Pyroha paper, the Wunu and economdevelopment abstracts and the Rada transcript were read at their sources in a browser.
