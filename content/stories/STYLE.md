# Style for country stories

Every story is signed Egalitaristen and speaks in the site's own voice: plain, confident, sourced. It says what the sources show and what the site argues, and it does not hedge either. These rules apply to every story and to every rewrite. Run them by hand before a story goes in, because the Humanizer skill is not installed in the build environment.

## Say it straight

- State what the source says, then what the site argues from it. No "perhaps", "arguably", "may", "might", "it could be said", "some would say", "seems to", "tends to", "a bit". Where a source itself gives a range or an estimate, give the range or the estimate with its source and year, and nothing softer.
- No hedging boxes and no "the strongest objection that remains". If an argument against the tax is common, answer it with evidence.
- Every number carries a source id and a year. Every named person or party links to its register entry.
- Attribute by name. Never "experts say" or "studies show". Write "Brülhart, Gruber, Krapf and Schmidheiny (2022) found".
- Report losses plainly. A vote that went against us is stated with its result, once, without spin.

## Cut what makes text sound generated

- Significance inflation: pivotal, testament, landscape, crucial, underscores, showcases, fosters, delve, tapestry, vibrant, "plays a key role".
- "Not just X but Y" and "it is not X, it is Y".
- Lists of three used for rhythm. List what there is, however many.
- Em dashes as a habit. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop. At most one per paragraph. The attribution line of a pull quote may start with one.
- -ing tails that add fake depth: highlighting, reflecting, ensuring, showcasing, "contributing to".
- Promotional or sweeping openers and closers ("In today's world", "The future looks bright"). Start with a fact. End on the last fact.
- Generic conclusions ("In conclusion", "Ultimately").
- Filler transitions: Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, "It is worth noting", "It should be noted".
- Title Case Headings. Headings are sentence case.
- Bold-lead bullet lists. Use plain prose, `###` subheadings or a plain list.
- Emoji.

## Write like a person

- Concrete nouns, named sources, numbers with years.
- Vary sentence length. A short sentence after a long one.
- Active voice. Short plain words: "use", not "utilise".
- "We" only where the About page already uses it. Otherwise name the actor: "the site argues", "the register lists".

## Mechanics

- About 1,500 words of body. Facts, timeline and sources are on top of that.
- Sections are `##` with a fixed anchor: `## Cantons undercut each other {#competition}`. The anchor is the stable URL part. Never change one after a link to it exists.
- Put `{{supporters}}` on its own line in the section on who backs the tax. It draws the live register block, so do not repeat its lists by hand. Keep an editorial paragraph above it.
- Re-verify against the source every sentence you change. Keep every fact, number and source id intact when you rewrite.
