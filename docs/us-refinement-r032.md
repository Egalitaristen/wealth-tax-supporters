Kept county precision rather than guessing the current headquarters city from the Justice Campus project. **Federal Unionists Network Bay Area** uses its own hosted group page and Bay Area service region, without treating a social event’s Oakland venue as headquarters. **Assyrian Democrats of the Bay Area** is explicitly chartered in Santa Clara County; no headquarters city was established. **Contra Costa Defenders Association** is identified as a represented employee association in the county’s official labour agreement record; corrected its type to union and retained county precision.

**College Democrats at Sacramento State:** corrected the legacy “Sacremento” spelling using the university-hosted organisation page. CampusGroups/Ready Education platform social accounts were rejected. **Davis College Democrats:** used its own current bylaws and linked accounts. Parent/sister accounts were also excluded for CNA, Art of Living Los Angeles, Tax the Greedy Billionaires and the host of the federal unionists’ page.
