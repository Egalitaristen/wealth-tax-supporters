# Wealth tax status audit, 13 November 2026

Scope: whether any country other than Hungary (handled separately) enacted, changed or repealed a wealth tax from 2025 to 13 November 2026, so the gold borders in `data/wealth_tax_status.csv` and the bill states in `data/resources.json` are right. Result: **no change to the map**. The `has` and `had` rows are all still correct.
